Ecom Flow Non-Negotiables for 2027

1) Welcome Flow:

- Include 4 emails minimum in the sequence. Conversion drops off after day 4.
- Mix in a text-based email. These almost always outperform designed emails on placed order rate.
- Put the discount code in every email. People don't scroll back to find it.
- End the flow with urgency. Discount expiring and final chance messaging is what closes the fence-sitters.

2) Abandonment Flows:

- Get your site, browse, cart, and checkout abandoned flows all live. Each one catches a different level of intent.
- Run 4 emails minimum per flow, spaced 24 hours apart. Every day you wait, the lead gets colder.
- Split content for buyers vs non-buyers. Non-buyers need the welcome discount reminder, and buyers need "come back" messaging.
- Save your heaviest offer for the back half of the flow to close the fence-sitters.

3) Post Purchase & Winback:

- Fire a founder thank you right after purchase. PS cross-sells your highest converter and cuts buyer's remorse.
- Send onboarding emails that educate on usage. How to use it, when to use it, how to stay consistent.
- Time the next-order push with your data. Fire the cross-sell inside the spike window, not after it closes.
- Test winback offers aggressively. By then, customers have seen your campaigns and sales, so the offer is the only lever left.

4) Across All Flows:

- Benchmark your flows against your campaigns monthly. Flows should drive 25-35% of your total email revenue.
- Update your flows every 6-12 months. Audiences change, offers shift, and platforms update.
- Test offers consistently. The biggest lever you have is the offer. One new offer test per flow per month.
- Review every flow weekly. Flows break silently all the time and one weekly audit saves massive revenue loss.
