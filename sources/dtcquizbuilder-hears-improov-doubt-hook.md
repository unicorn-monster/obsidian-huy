Hears does est. ~$733k/mo selling earplugs.

Their top ad has run 121 days with 5 duplicates.

I think it works because it never pitches the snorer. It hooks the partner losing sleep:

"How many nights has his snoring cost you?"

---

Improov's active ads went from ~35 to ~120 a week between April and October.

Headline:
"5 Reasons Millions Are Ditching Sleep Pills, Gummies and Melatonin For Improov"

The subhead promises it works:
"...without the morning hangover."

---

Why I believe both work:

They lead with the buyer's private doubt before pitching.

To apply this to your own funnel:

Find the exact excuse your customer uses to close the tab.

Kill that blocker in line one of your ad and above the fold on your page.
