Wrote this 17 months ago and it's aged well.

Here's more breakthroughs I've had since then:

- Invest in team training from outside coaches + yourself. Put together one training for your team every single day. You must be an education company.

- Hire developers and build your own softwares. We pull the insights we actually want instead of being limited by what other platforms give us.

- Hire in-house recruiters. Talent is everything as an agency, so you want someone constantly reaching out, running interviews, and keeping a steady stream of good candidates coming.

- Run a time audit once a quarter. Figure out what's eating your time and what's actually high leverage, then replace everything that isn't.

- Invest in new coaching or mentors at least once a year to expand your network and get perspectives from people who've done it.

- Make extremely tactical content aimed at your specific ICP, sprinkled in with top of funnel stuff. Mix both and your ICP finds you.
