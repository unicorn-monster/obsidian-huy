title: CBO with minimum spends to test new ad sets
---

In the beginning you want to make sure everything you test gets spend, so you can understand what's working and what's not

If you don't force spend, you don't know if an ad isn't working or just hasn't had a proper chance. You want that clarity so you can double down on what works and move on from what doesn't

I normally go with one CBO with minimum spends per ad set in the beginning.

That way every new ad set and concept gets some initial spend, but I can also see what Meta naturally wants to spend more on, which is valuable.

But ABO also works, there's no single best way, especially in the beginning.
