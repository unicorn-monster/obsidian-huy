How to double down on a winning native ad

Here are 3 high-leverage iterations:

1. Keep the winning copy and spam new images.

The image alone can make a huge difference, so test a bunch of variations on your winning text.

2. Keep the winning image and body and test different hooks and leads.

These are the opening lines people see above the "see more" button.

Their job is to get readers curious and interested enough to expand the text and keep reading.

If your opening doesn't get them to click "see more," they never see the rest of your copy...

3 Change the POV / narrator.

For example, instead of someone talking about their own beauty routine, have a makeup artist talk about a client's routine, this also adds authority.

Adapt the whole story to that perspective and also the page you're running it from.
