6 subject lines I keep coming back to:

"We almost didn't send this"
"7 ways to style the linen shirt"
"quick question for you"
"Sleep through the night (finally)"
"Ends tonight at 11:59 PM PT" (only if it's real)
"Worth every penny"

Pick one, test it, keep what wins.
