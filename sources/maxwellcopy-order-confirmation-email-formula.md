This is what a 10/10 order confirmation email looks like...

- Makes the customer feel like they just joined something, not just bought something
- Reaffirms the exact benefits they're about to get from the product
- Sets clear expectations on how long results actually take
- Closes with an open invite to reach out if anything goes wrong

One of the highest-opened emails you'll ever send, so worth actually building these out properly!
