Here are authority figures you can use in your ads and landing pages that aren't doctors.

Authority works really well, so everyone goes after the most obvious ones: doctors, dermatologists, vets. But licensed professionals are not the only way to add authority, and they're way harder to sign and way more expensive to work with.

A good authority can be much simpler, someone your customer already trusts on that specific problem, or who obviously knows a lot about it.

- Beauty: makeup artists, hairstylists, barbers, sephora employees

- Health: ex pro athletes (way cheaper than active pros), coaches, military

- Food: chefs, bakers, butchers, bartenders

- Home: plumbers, electricians, carpenters, interior designers

- Tech: youtubers, photographers, dj's, gamers

Use them in your creative and funnels and watch your performance go up.
