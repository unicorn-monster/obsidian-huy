You’ll always have to make decisions without 100% certainty.

You might get to 40% certainty that something will work.

But taking risks and making decisions with incomplete data is just part of the game.

For choosing angles, do your research then make your best educated guess on what’s gonna work. 

Execute as well as you possibly can on that angle.

If it works - great! Iterate on it and scale.

If it doesn’t work - also good, now you have answers and can move forward with better data.

The key is treating every test you run as buying valuable information from the market, not as wins and losses.

Each angle you test either makes you money or teaches you something about your market.

Both outcomes move you closer to finding what actually works.
