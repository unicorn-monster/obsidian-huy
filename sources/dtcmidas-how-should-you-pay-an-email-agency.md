Hire an e-mail agency or freelancer - it’s definitely worth it.

Just make sure you pay a flat monthly retainer, not a percentage of attributed revenue.

A lot of email agencies pitch " we only get paid when you make money" (% of email revenue).

Sounds good but it's a terrible deal.

Their fee grows just because you spend more on ads and your list grows - not because they're doing better work.

Also it’s very easy to game attributed revenue from email.
