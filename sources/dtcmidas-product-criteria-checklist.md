These are my product criteria:

- Good margins, (ideally 80%+ gross, 70%+ after shipping & fulfillment)
- Small and easy to ship
- Consumable or repeat purchase potential, or possibility for product line expansion in same niche
- A growing market, not the tail end of a trend
- A product that solves one but preferably more specific problems
- Different audiences you can sell to

I'd always make my own ads from the start, it's so much easier now than it used to be

You don't want to spend your time dealing with stupid bans, reviews, restrictions and all that shit because you ripped someone else's content

It's just not worth your time in the long run

Invest the time up front in doing it properly yourself, it doesn't guarantee you'll never get a restriction, but you're not creating extra bs for yourself later
