There is always more to do than you can do.

And the tasks you enjoy most are usually not the ones that move the needle.

I catch myself doing them more often than I'd like to admit.

So this is how I now decide what to actually work on.

I keep 2 lists in my notes: a to do list and an ideas list.

Everything goes in there, then I score every item with ICE:

- Impact: how much does this move my North Star KPI (revenue / profit)

- Confidence: how sure am I that it works

- Ease: how fast can I ship it

1 to 10 on each

Big bets like launching a new product don't go through this system, those get decided on strategy.

Here’s an example of how it works:

- new logo and store redesign: 2 x 3 x 5 = 30

- moving to a 3PL for faster shipping: 8 x 7 x 2 = 112

- hiring a new creative strategist: 10 x 8 x 4 = 420

- improving abandoned cart flow: 4 x 5 x 5 = 100

- making new ads based on new winning angle: 9 x 7 x 8 = 504

Then I work top down.

Try to do 1-3 high leverage tasks a day, do that long enough and you’ll grow.
