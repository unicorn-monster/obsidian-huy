I've talked to 5-10 guys in the past month who started their stores 12 to 18 months ago and are doing $5-10M a month with an extremely lean team.

It's literally just them, maybe a couple people, and a few agencies for creative and landers and emails/sms.

And they're extremely profitable doing it.

On the other hand... I talk to older brands at the same revenue with massive teams who are barely profitable, results have plateaued, and they can't break through it.

Huge lesson in that.

Instead of building out headcount and layers on top of a funnel that isn't working yet, put all your attention on acquiring customers, testing offers, landers, and creatives...

Then once that's ripping, retention and LTV is where you go to multiply it.

A 10% LTV increase on a funnel at scale is business changing money...

Many older big brands spend so much time on tiny details that don't matter... instead of building new offers and funnels and taking the big shots the lean guys are taking lol.
