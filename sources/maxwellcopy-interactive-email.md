Interactive emails are the future.

Every time you want a customer to do something like buy, subscribe, or leave a review, you have to send them to your site.

Totally normal, but all that extra friction of getting them to load the page and go through the whole thing just gives you more chances to lose them.

Interactive emails kill all of that.

Having an email where someone can literally add a product to their existing order in one tap is one of the greatest things I've seen in this space.

This will be the future and how all of email marketing looks like.
