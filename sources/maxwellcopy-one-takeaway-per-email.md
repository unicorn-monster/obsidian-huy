title: One key takeaway per email rule
---

Unspoken rule of email copywriting…

Focus on just one key takeaway per email.

Most brands try to cram five benefits into a single send and the customer walks away remembering none of them.

Instead pick one thing and build the entire email around it.

This is a great example.

This whole email is about one thing: L-theanine paired with caffeine gives you smoother focus without the crash.

They don't mention the other twelve ingredients, or list every benefit… they just make that single point properly.

You've got a whole calendar to cover everything else! Don't try to do it in all one send.
