title: "The finger-click test exposes cope"
---

When you have an idea for a brand, ask yourself:

If you could click your fingers and the store was built, creative assets complete, product ready, ads made and ready to go all you need to do is hit publish. Would you do it?

If yes, realise that all the 'what ifs' are just cope for not wanting to do the work

Too much time and energy gets spent deliberating on hypotheticals. But really like most things you just need to test and find out

I'm guilty of this too and am trying to get out of the habit. All my 'lucky breaks' came from ploughing ahead without a second thought
