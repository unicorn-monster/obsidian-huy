title: "The best creative strategists usually don’t come from marketing backgrounds"
---

The best creative strategists I've worked with normally didn't come from a standard marketing background.

They came from engineering, journalism, sales, and other places that have nothing to do with ads.

I believe that is because the job is mostly doing research, breaking down problems systematically, understanding feedback loops, and understanding people.

An engineer thinks in tests and feedback loops, a journalist knows how to do proper research and how to tell a story, a sales person knows how to handle objections and what actually moves a person.

Teaching someone frameworks, direct response fundamentals, and formats is not that hard.

Empathy and aptitude for this kind of thinking is not something you can teach.
