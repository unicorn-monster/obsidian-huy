Hills I will 100% die on as an expert email marketer:

List growth and send frequency are by far your biggest levers in increasing performance.

The only A/B tests that are truly high leverage are offer tests. You can't micro AB test your way to 2x performance… but one offer test can 2x performance.

Keep your segmentation dead simple. Wide engaged is the way to go, while sprinkling in some targeted sends.

You don't need 10+ email flows. 4-6 is perfect, and even that can be pushing it in most cases.

Text based emails are king, but don't overuse them. They work best when they stand out.

Design is overrated. Nice to have for branding, but it's not what drives results.

Deliverability is dead simple. Send to engaged lists and stop sending to people who aren't engaging.

Spend a lot of time on your pop-up forms. A couple of tweaks there can double your list growth.

Never take email revenue attribution literally. Use it as a directional gauge on performance.
