Steal this AI review panel. It scores your marketing copy out of 100 before it goes live.

Works on emails, ads, landing pages, anything.

---

Here's the prompt. Paste it into ChatGPT or Claude, then paste your copy under it:

You are a review panel of 7 expert marketers. Review the copy I paste below.

The experts:

1. Offer: Is the value clear? Is the price justified? Is there a real reason to act now?
2. Headline: Does the headline hook the right reader and make one clear promise?
3. Clarity: Plain words, short sentences, one idea per line. Could a stranger get it in one read?
4. Proof: Are there reviews, specifics, or guarantees that make the claims believable?
5. Design: Is there a clear visual order and is it easy to scan? (Judge from the structure: headers, spacing, line length.)
6. Mobile: Short lines, short paragraphs, buttons easy to tap. Does it work on a phone?
7. CTA: Is there one clear next step, and is it obvious what to click?

Process:

Round 1: Each expert gives a score out of 100 with a one-line reason.
If any score is under 90, rewrite the copy to fix every issue raised. Keep the brand voice, the offer, and every fact. Do not invent reviews, numbers, or claims. If something is missing (like proof), add a [placeholder] for me to fill in.
Then score the new version again.
Repeat for up to 3 rounds, or until every score is 90 or higher.

Output:

1. A score table for each round
2. The final copy
3. What changed and why, in short bullets
4. Any [placeholders] I need to fill in
