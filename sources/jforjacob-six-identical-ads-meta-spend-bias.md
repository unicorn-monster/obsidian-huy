Was having a meeting with my strategist today, looking over the ad account to see what is and isn't working

Looking at an image ad set, 6 creatives, all wildly different spend and performance

Decided to take a few minutes to analyse what the difference was and try and take some learning

Realised there had been a fuck up and the exact same variant had been uploaded into that ad set 6 times

And I mean the difference in performance was night and day, 3 of them had got decent spend. One had great ROAS, one had decent ROAS, the other was brutal. The other 3 didn't get much spend, a few sales but not much to talk about and all low ROAS

Really makes me question my trust in Meta

Did one just hit a 'hot pocket' early and that's why it performed and continued to get spend and the others didn't? Imagine I had just launched it on it's own and it didn't get strong signals at the start, likely would not win any spend after first few $ spent

This is one of many reasons I'm a big advocate of minimum spends on ad sets in a CBO once you get to a decent level of creative volume and scale. Too easy for creatives to go missing in there and Meta to show bias to things that get strong signals after just a few impressions

I really don't believe Meta can tell whether something is a winner or not after just a few $ spent. If it hits a bad pocket of people in those first few impressions it will likely never see the light of day again. Of course a real winner has a high likelihood of getting strong signals from the very start, but definitely not all the time. Have too many examples where this just wasn't the case
