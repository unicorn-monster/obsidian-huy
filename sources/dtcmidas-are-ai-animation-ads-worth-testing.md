Full AI animation ads are top spenders in multiple campaigns right now.

They’re beating:

- Winners that have been running for months
- Highly produced, expensive videos
- Long VSLs with multiple creators

I don’t particularly like this.

It’s less defensible because competitors can replicate AI ads way easier than real creator content.

But the data doesn’t lie. And I won’t go against data.

The real defensibility here is volume.

It takes time to make these AI animation concepts actually look good. Most brands won’t put in the effort to produce them at scale.

If you haven’t tried this format yet, I highly recommend it.
