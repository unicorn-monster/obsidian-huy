The best ads make you wish you'd thought of them first.

This KitKat ad is one of those.

At first, it looks like a normal photo of people standing around staring at their phones.

Then you look closer and realize they're all holding KitKat bars.

KitKat's whole thing is "Have a break." The funny part is that most of us now spend our breaks staring at another screen.

So they swapped the screen for the break.

It took me two seconds to get, and then I wanted to look at it again.

No other brand could run this exact ad without making you think of KitKat.
