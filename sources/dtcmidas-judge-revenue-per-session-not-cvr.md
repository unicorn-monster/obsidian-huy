Store wide cvr doesn’t say anything. It’s a mix of warm and cold traffic, and even repeat purchasers, so can be very skewed. 

Cvr especially is a metric I don’t really look at. If aov goes up cvr mostly goes down.

Therefore always look at rev per session on either page level or specific funnels.
