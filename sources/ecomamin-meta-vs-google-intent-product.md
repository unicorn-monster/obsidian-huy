i lost money on meta ads for two years straight before i touched google

no dramatic story, no rock bottom moment. i was 17, running stores, watching campaigns work for a week and then die the second i pushed spend

and the reason had nothing to do with my ads

let me be clear about something first: meta is the goat. i'm not one of those guys who found one channel and now pretends the other one is dead

meta prints for the right product. impulse buys, visual products, trend-driven categories, anything where "i want one" fires in three seconds. nothing on the internet does the job better

my problem was i wasn't selling those products

pet health. supplements. categories where the buyer researches, hesitates, compares, and needs to TRUST you before the card comes out

meta gives you 3 seconds to build trust with a stranger, and a considered purchase needs far more runway than 3 seconds. so my CPAs looked fine at $200/day and fell apart at $400

i rebuilt creative, rebuilt targeting, rebuilt offers, and the ceiling stayed exactly where i found it

a friend told me to try google shopping. i set up one campaign, fixed the product titles so the keyword led instead of the brand name, uploaded lifestyle images instead of white background shots

profitable in week one

because the buyer typing "supplement for dog joint pain" into google already HAS the problem, already believes the category works, and already decided to spend money today

i didn't have to manufacture desire. i had to show up

the store did $955k the same year, mostly google

the honest version has nothing to do with google beating meta. i ran an intent product on an attention platform for two years and blamed my creative

if your buyer searches before they buy, you belong on google. if they need to be shown the problem exists first, stay on meta

most brands need both
