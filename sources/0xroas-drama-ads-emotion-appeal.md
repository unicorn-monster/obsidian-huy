Resilia week 1 and week 4 results.

"No one in this world has ever lost money by underestimating the intelligence of the great masses of the plain people."

AI song ads/dramas work because they are engaging and entertaining to watch...

especially when they combine a good story with hero's success in the end:

- the woman got in her best shape
- the woman dumped her husband in the end
- her husband regrets losing her and wants her back.

The idea is that businesses, politicians, advertisers, and media companies often succeed by appealing to people's:

- simplest emotions
- desires
- instincts

rather than relying on their intelligence.
