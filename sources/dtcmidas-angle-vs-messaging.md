Messaging is the specific words, phrases, and language you use to communicate your angle.

Let’s take Huel (meal replacement shake) as an example:

Angle: Convenience for busy professionals

Messaging option 1: get complete nutrition in under 2 minutes

Messaging option 2: save 10 hours of meal prep every week

Same product, same angle (convenience), but different ways to communicate it.
