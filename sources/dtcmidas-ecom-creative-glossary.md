ECOM GLOSSARY

It seems like everyone on here has a different definition of what an angle, a concept or a format is, and some people are still building pre-landers (spoiler: they don't exist)

So let's fix it once and for all, so we can communicate with each other.

Angle: the reason to buy you lead with. It targets one specific problem, desire or belief.

Example: a hair serum leading with "no side effects" vs one leading with "hair back before your wedding".

Format: what the creative looks like. For example an ai animation ad, podcast ad, b-roll vsl, static with a benefit headline callout, etc.

Structure: the order of elements in your script or sales copy. Like hook, problem, failed solution, mechanism, proof, offer, cta.

Concept: angle + format + structure, so basically the full ad. Change any of those 3 and you have a new concept.

Avatar: the audience you're selling to.
Example: men who are losing their hair.

Persona: a specific version of your avatar, with the same problem but different situation and motivation.

Example: the guy getting married next year who wants hair in the photos, or the guy who tried finasteride and quit because of the side effects.

Mechanism: the unique way your product gets them to the promised result. It has 2 parts:

UMP: the underlying reason (root cause) why the problem exists.

UMS: how your product fixes that exact underlying reason.

Awareness level: how much someone knows about their problem and the possible solutions

Sophistication level: how many similar claims the market has already heard.

Funnel stage (tof / mof / bof): how warm someone is to YOUR brand.

Native ad: can be two things

1. A format on meta: long primary text, often advertorial style, with a native / raw / organic looking image.

2. Native ad platforms, like: taboola, outbrain, mediago.

Landing page: any first page someone lands on after clicking the ad.

Pre-lander: does not exist. If it's the first page after the click, it's a landing page.

What most people mean is a pre-sell page: a page that educates and persuades the prospect before showing the pdp / offer page. Usually a listicle, advertorial, quiz, etc.
