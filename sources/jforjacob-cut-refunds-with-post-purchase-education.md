title: "Diagnose when and why refunds happen, then fix it in the post-purchase flow"
---

We managed to reduce our refund percentage from 3.91% in August to 1.04% in October (73.4% decrease)

Refunds in August were too high for our liking. Did an analysis and found the majority of returns were happening 2-3 weeks after delivery and the main reason given was customers having difficulty using the product

We recorded some educational videos and linked to them as part of our welcome flow. In this welcome flow we also set some expectations, saying that most customers find the product can take 2-3 weeks to get used to, but after that it's a breeze etc

Amazing results from this. Simply listening to customers and making some small changes to educate them better. Saving us thousands a month
