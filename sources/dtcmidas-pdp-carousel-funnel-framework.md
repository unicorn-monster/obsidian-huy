This is my current winning pdp image carousel:

1. product + desired outcome headline + awards badges

2. before/after

3. problem/symptom checklist

4. how it works (unique mechanism, key ingredients, features, materials, tech)

5. social proof (screenshots facebook comments/trustpilot/amazon)

6. us vs them

7. how to use (step 1, step 2 etc)

8. timeline (what changes to expect by day x)

9. authority proof

10. social proof #2

11. guarantee

Your image carousel should work like a funnel.

Some people scroll vertically and check your whole product page.

Others only swipe horizontally through your images and decide to buy from that alone.
