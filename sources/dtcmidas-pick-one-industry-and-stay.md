The most successful people I know picked 1 industry and stayed in it for an unreasonably long time.

While everyone else is chasing the next shiny object, they’re becoming the undisputed expert in their space.

They know every player, every trend, every opportunity before it becomes obvious.

They’ve built relationships with suppliers, customers, and partners that take years to develop.

They understand the nuances that outsiders miss.

When a new opportunity pops up in their industry, they see it first and move fastest.

Meanwhile, the industry hoppers are starting from zero every 2-3 years.

Pick your lane.

And stay in it longer than feels comfortable.

If you’re ambitious and work hard for long enough in that industry, you will become successful.
