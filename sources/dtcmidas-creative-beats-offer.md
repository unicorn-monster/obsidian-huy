title: Creative leverage beats offer at scale
---

Creative is higher leverage than offer.

A good offer will only get you so far, it won't get you to real scale without great ads. The other way around, it will.

Imagine 2 scenarios:

1. A very good offer, but you only run headline call-out and basic BOF offer statics.

Meta will find the low-hanging fruit that converts easily with your offer. But then your funnel dries out as you try to scale budgets, because you won't be able to reach new people and fill your top of funnel with your shitty ads.

2. A mediocre offer, but you only produce killer 3, 4, 5, 6+ minute VSLs.

Great ads will improve CVR, improve RPS, and can make a worse offer work.

Every day of the week I'd rather be in the second scenario than the first.
