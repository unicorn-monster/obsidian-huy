Churn down 25%

What did we change?

- Moved to US fulfilment
- Gave customers more delivery frequency flexibility
- Changed factories to get the highest quality (and most expensive) version of our product, a price competitors wouldn't be willing to pay even if they could get the same pricing as us (which they couldn't)
- And some saucy stuff in the customer journey / product experience that I won't share here
