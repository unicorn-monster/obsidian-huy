Email Design Non-Negotiables

1) CTA button above the fold on every single send. The above the fold section is the only part every opener is guaranteed to see. If your button isn't up there, you're banking on them scrolling to find it.

2) Every product gets its own button. Subscribers don't assume the image is clickable. One general "Shop Now" at the bottom loses everyone who wanted a specific SKU.

3) End every email with a "Shop All" CTA. The people who scrolled all the way down without clicking are still warm, they just didn't connect with what you featured. Give them another way in right before the footer.

4) Infographics over walls of copy. Anytime you've written more than 2-3 lines in a row, that's your signal to turn it into a visual. Before and afters, us vs them charts, stat blocks, step-by-steps.

5) Keep the background behind your hero text clean. No faces, no busy product shots, and no detailed scenery. Extend the image with AI or drop a gradient over the top so the headline actually reads.

6) Never run two hero layouts in a row that look the same. The same headline + body + button every send makes your calendar feel templated, and templated calendars stop getting opened.

7) Include category buttons in every footer. It's the one section that repeats across every campaign and flow, so the optimization compounds for the lifetime of the account.
