Things that can actually move the needle for your brand:

- new products

- new landing pages

- new offers

- new channels

- new creatives (mainly new angles unlocking new audiences)

Things that don't

- vibe coding apps that you can pay $100 a month for already

- refining your "brand guide"

- Updating your SOP's

- Changing your meta structure for the 100th time

- email popups

- AI apps

- finding a new way to automate your shitty static ads with AI
