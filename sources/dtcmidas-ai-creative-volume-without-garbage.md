Making more ad-creatives won't save you anymore.

AI is bringing the cost of making creative way down.

Same thing that's happening in tech:

Dev costs dropped, so everyone and their mother started building B2B SaaS and consumer apps.

Now the same thing's happening with ad creative.

I'm always preaching volume because you need to test fast and outlearn your competitors.

That hasn't changed.

Think about it like this

When everyone had to hire expensive video crews, making even a bad video ad cost $3-5K.

So people put thought and effort into each one.

Now you can generate 50 ads in a day.

But so can your competitor.

The feed is flooded with shitty AI-generated content.

And consumers are already getting numb to it.

It's like when stock photos became cheap and accessible.

Suddenly every website looked the same.

The brands that stood out were the ones investing in custom content.

The winning move isn't to make more garbage faster.

It's to use AI to increase your volume while maintaining (or raising) your creative standards.

Most people are using AI as an excuse to get lazy.
 They just put out content for the sake of putting it out. 
And that's exactly your opportunity.

When everyone can make 50 ads a day, be the person making 5 great ones instead of 50 pieces of shit.
