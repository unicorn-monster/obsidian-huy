title: Scaling low AOV brands via unit economics loop
---

how to scale with low aov

low aov, easier first purchase, often lower cac

good product > people buy again
people buy again > ltv goes up
low cac + good ltv = good unit economics

ads and stock need cash now, but repeat purchases bring cash later
scaling can create cashflow problem

good economics make that easier to finance
solve cashflow issue > scale harder
small profit per order x many orders = big total profit
