1 cbo (both for testing and scaling), dump in new creatives in there (1 adset per concept, 3-5 ads per adset), then add a minimum spend on adset level, scale if campaign performance is above target. 

Kill high spenders in that campaign if campaign performance is under target and kill mid/low spenders that have bad performance.
