Depends on the LP

If I’m testing pre-sell pages (eg, advertorials, listicles, long form sales pages…) I normally just test it within the CBO by duping ads and adding a new url to it.

If I test new pdp I mostly will run a a intelligems A/B test.

To start I’d recommend making LPs based on your top performer ads, so that the LP matches the angle of that ad
