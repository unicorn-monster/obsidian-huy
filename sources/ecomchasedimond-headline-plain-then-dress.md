Every founder I know can tell a friend what their business does in one sentence. Then they sit down to write the website or a post, and it comes out as "bespoke lifestyle solutions."

People give you about two seconds. If they can't tell what you do in that time, they're gone, and more words only bury the one thing that would have told them.

So I'd write the plain version first:

What you do and who it's for, in the words a customer would use. You can dress it up later, as long as a stranger can still tell it's a bike shop.

Quick test: read your headline to someone who doesn't know what you do and ask them to say it back. If they can't, it isn't done.
