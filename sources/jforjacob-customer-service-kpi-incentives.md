What's a good KPI to use to introduce some incentives for a customer service team?

If you go by average response time it will end up encouraging rushed responses

If you go by return / refund rate you risk them pissing off a lot of customers by being too resistant to any refund requests

If you go by Trustpilot rating they could end up being really pushy asking customers for reviews or else doing things they shouldn't to overly please customers

If you go by number of tickets answered you could end up in a situation where they don't go into as much depth as they need to solve the issue fully or trying to draw out long interactions with customers

I really want to give some incentives to my team but can't quite figure it out
