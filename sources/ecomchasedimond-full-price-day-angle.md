The biggest sale on earth exists because four college guys in 1993 were sick of being single.

Prime Day was Amazon's birthday party.
Cyber Monday was a press release.

Every shopping holiday on your calendar was made up by someone with a reason and a deadline.

If I got to make one, it'd be Full Price Day. One day a year, your best launch, no discount, and you tell people exactly why it's worth it. If they still buy, you've got a brand. If they don't, you've got a coupon.

My question to you is: what would you call yours?
