How to run your biggest BFCM ever this year:

> Treat it as 5 weeks, not one weekend
> Nov 1-16 kick off with bundles, gift sets and dollar off framing
> Hold your strongest offer back, always have best offer on Black Friday
> Run email and SMS every other day, and alternate them so you're having daily touchpoints with your subscribers
> Nov 17-24 open early access with a softer version of the real offer
> Sell the gated access and beating the shipping cutoff, instead of just a discount
> Ramp email to 5-7x per week through that window
> Nov 25-29 go live with the biggest and simplest offer you'll run all year
> If it takes more than 3 seconds to understand, it's too complicated
> Send 2x a day minimum emails/sms, morning push and an evening reminder
> Nov 30-Dec 3 run Cyber Monday at the same value with a new angle on it
> An extra % off, a free gift or a product drop is enough to make it feel new
> Resend your emails in the evening to get more out of your creative
> Build and schedule these in advance so you aren't getting dragged down during BFCM week
