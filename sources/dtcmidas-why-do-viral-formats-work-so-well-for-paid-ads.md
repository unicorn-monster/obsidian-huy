Why viral formats work so well for paid ads.

When a format goes viral, the best editors/creators jump on it and make great content, because there’s an incentive  for it.

People watch tons of these videos and get conditioned: 

This format = entertaining & worth watching

When you make ads in the same viral format, people hook better and watch longer.

Not because your ad is necessarily better, but because they've been conditioned to expect a payoff from this format.

You're borrowing the success of the format itself.

This is why jumping on viral formats fast is so valuable.

Ofcourse you still need to apply direct response principles to make it works.

But viral formats die out.

Once too many brands flood the format with ads, the conditioning breaks and people start scrolling past them.

So when you see a format going viral, test it immediately, as it works very well.
