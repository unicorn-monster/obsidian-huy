If you find yourself in a situation where you have an ad that is grabbing all spend, ROAS / CPA is not where you need it to be and overall campaign performance is also not there. Please do the following:

Understand that ad that is grabbing all the spend and not converting at your desired rate is likely very engagement heavy and good at getting new and cold people to interact with your brand

By default these people will not convert at your target

If your overall campaign is not performing on top of this it's because you don't have the necessary touch points to serve to these people after initial interaction

What you should do: Analyse your top ad and landing page (use AI if you wish). And literally think of every single question or concern someone could have on that specific journey (from top ad to your landing page, assume that is all they have saw from your brand)

Then create ads that address these directly. It needs to be hyper specific to your specific top ad and landing page

So if your top ad was "omg I'm fitting into my high school clothes after 60 day supplement challenge" people would likely be curious about things like "what was she eating during this period?" "Was she exercising / hitting the gym" "Did she see any negative side effects" "How was her libido during such fast weight loss" "Was she still going out at the weekends"

Then literally just make ads that address / answer these questions: full day of eating during the challenge, my workout routine during my challenge, candid talk about sex life during challenge, comment reply videos directly answering questions, before and after videos

Assume that meta will show these ads in the correct sequence (as it most likely will after some optimisation). If you do this correctly, you will print money
