Do this to minimize risk when starting a new brand.

1. Get quality renders
Spend ~$500 on professional product renders. You need these to look legit in ads and on your landing page.

2. Build a simple funnel
Make a basic but optimized landingpage (either straight to product page or pre-sell page + product page) and checkout page in Shopify.

3. Run $2-5K in ads
Test with static ads using your renders and VSLs made with stock footage + ripped content.

4. Email everyone who bought
Send an apology email explaining the situation. Give them two options: get their money back immediately or be first in line when the product launches.

This way you can validate demand without risking a big inventory order.

This saves you from ordering 1000 units of something nobody wants.
