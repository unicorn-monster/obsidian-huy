google ads quiz funnels are a $10k+/day opportunity for ANY ecom brand...

but they use to take me 20h+ to manually run

mapping ICP segments into question logic. producing MOF search ad copy. fixing the branding

manus AI does EVERYTHING in 1 morning now (for $0)

here's my EXACT 4 step workflow:

step 1: ICP pain point mapping

i give manus one prompt:

"research the top 5 pain point clusters for [product category] buyers. for each cluster, identify the primary frustration, the failed solutions they've tried, and the language they use on reddit, forums, and amazon reviews"

manus browses autonomously. returns a structured map of 5 ICP segments with their exact language and emotional state

this took me 4-6 hours manually. manus does it in 30 minutes. and the depth is better because it pulls from sources i'd never check

step 2: branching question logic

"based on this map, design a 5-question diagnostic quiz. first question has broad appeal. questions 2-4 narrow toward specific segments. question 5 confirms intent level. include the logic tree for which answers map to which result page"

manus outputs the complete architecture. questions, answer options, routing rules. ready to drop into any quiz builder

step 3: segment-specific result pages

"for each result type, write a page: personalised headline referencing their answers, explanation of their root cause using ICP language, the mechanism that addresses it, how [product] implements this mechanism, one testimonial from their segment, soft CTA"

5 distinct result pages. each speaking directly to the segment's specific pain. not generic "you might like this." segment-matched copy

step 4: MOF search ad copy

"write 5 responsive search ad variations targeting non-branded keywords. each speaks to a different ICP pain point. headlines lead with the pain, not the product. descriptions create curiosity about the quiz"

the ads sell the quiz. the quiz sells the product. this two-step indirect sell converts significantly higher on cold google traffic

here's why this matters beyond production speed though:

quiz funnels outperform advertorials for products above £100 AOV. the higher the price, the more the buyer needs to feel ownership over the decision

a quiz creates that through micro-commitments. each answer is an investment. by the time the result page appears, walking away feels like abandoning something they started

and after 30 days of running the quiz, you have data no other source gives you

which pain points resonate most (by completion rate per branch). which segments convert highest (by purchase rate per result). which ad copy drives the best quiz takers (by qualified completion rate)

that data feeds back into everything. your google ads get sharper. your shopping titles match buyer language. your remarketing segments by quiz result, not page visit

the quiz doesn't end at the conversion. the quiz is the beginning of a data loop that makes every campaign in the account better
