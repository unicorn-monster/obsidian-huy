People don't understand that in terms of video retention your hook does 90% of the work

GIVE A PROMISE OF A PAY OFF

I'd guarantee this video has almost 100% watch rate. After the hook nobody is even listening, she's just rambling about stuff nobody cares about. But they are waiting for the payoff (which doesn't even come)

Think about how you can incorporate a promise of a payoff when creating your next ad
