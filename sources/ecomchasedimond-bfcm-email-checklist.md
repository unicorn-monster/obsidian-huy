dear ecommerce brands

you can have your best Black Friday ever, even with a small team

my agency helped 250+ ecommerce brands generate $200+ million. here's the simple playbook we run every year

1. build a VIP list this week. copy this:

"want first dibs on our Black Friday sale? reply YES and you get in before anyone else"

everyone who replies gets early access

2. start sending more now. bump volume slowly through November, starting with people who opened or clicked lately. don't go from 2 emails a week to 2 a day on Black Friday

3. clean your list. stop emailing people who haven't opened or clicked in 6+ months. they hurt your inbox placement for everyone else

4. watch your spam rate. Gmail wants it under 0.1% and you're in real trouble at 0.3%. check Google Postmaster Tools every day of the sale

5. send to your best people first. engaged subscribers get the email a few hours before everyone else

6. keep recent full-price buyers out of the discount emails. send them a thank you or a how-to instead

7. update your automated emails for the sale. new subscribers and abandoned carts should hear about your Black Friday deal

8. texts: send between 8am and 8pm in the customer's time zone (some states are stricter than the federal 9pm), and only to people who opted in

9. send a last chance email before the sale ends to people who clicked but didn't buy. then send buyers a thank you after

10. decide BEFORE the sale if you're extending into Cyber Monday. if an email says "ends tonight" it needs to actually end tonight

bookmark this and go crush it
