title: 6-step BFCM email ops checklist
---

If you're sending Black Friday emails, I'm begging you:

- change your pop-up offer for the sale
- pull the coupons out of your flows
- stop sending sale emails to people who already bought
- push back your review request
- write your "we messed up" email now
- plan what your Black Friday buyers get next

How to do each one:

1. CHANGE YOUR POPUP OFFER (THIS WEEK)

"10% off your first order" looks tiny when the whole site is 30% off, and it gives people no reason to sign up.

For the sale, offer early access instead: "Get our Black Friday deals before everyone else." Then open the sale to that list a few hours early.

2. PULL THE COUPONS OUT OF YOUR FLOWS (BEFORE THE SALE STARTS)

Your welcome, cart and winback flows keep running during the sale. If they still hand out 10% off, either the code stacks on top of the sale and eats your margin, or it looks like a worse deal than what's on your homepage.

For the sale window, swap the code for a line about the sale and exactly when it ends.

3. STOP SENDING SALE EMAILS TO PEOPLE WHO ALREADY BOUGHT (EVERY SEND)

Someone buys on Friday, then gets "LAST CHANCE: 30% off ends tonight" on Monday. At best it's annoying, and at worst they unsubscribe.

Exclude anyone who's ordered since the sale started from the rest of your sale emails. Send them a thank you or a "goes great with what you got" email instead.

4. PUSH BACK YOUR REVIEW REQUEST (BEFORE THANKSGIVING)

A lot of review flows go out a set number of days after the order. In late November, shipping often takes longer, and plenty of those orders are gifts that won't be opened until December.

If your setup allows it, trigger the review request off delivery instead of the order date, and add a few extra days for sale orders.

5. WRITE YOUR "WE MESSED UP" EMAIL NOW (THIS WEEK)

If a code won't apply or a link goes to the wrong page on Black Friday morning, you don't want to be writing from scratch while sales stall.

Draft it now: a subject line that names the fix ("Fixed: your code works now"), two lines of apology, the working link, and the same end time. If something breaks, you can send it within minutes.

6. PLAN WHAT YOUR BLACK FRIDAY BUYERS GET NEXT (BEFORE CYBER MONDAY ENDS)

A lot of your Black Friday buyers will be first-time customers who came for the deal. If the next email they get from you is another discount, you're teaching them to wait for the next one.

Tag everyone whose first order came during the sale. Send them a few emails on how to get the most out of what they bought before you send another offer.
