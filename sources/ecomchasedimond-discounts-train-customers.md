title: Discounts train customers to wait for sale price
---

I'd rather have customers who buy because they want what I sell than customers who only show up for the discount.

A discount works the first time. Run one every week, and people learn that the sale price is the real price, so they stop buying until the next one.

Look at the last ten messages you sent your customers. If most of them were about a discount, that's what they're waiting for now.

A welcome offer for new customers is fine. But for the people who already buy from you, the reason to come back shouldn't always be a lower price.

Give them a new product, a limited run, something that's only around for a few days. Put that in most of the next ten messages instead.
