Curious what adding a free gift on Order #2 does for a subscription brand?

Free Gift Recipients: 77.9% rebill rate on Order 2
Holdout Group: 70.4% rebill rate on Order 2

Given their COGS on the gift, it is well worth it.

(Also, the recipients will rebill more times)
