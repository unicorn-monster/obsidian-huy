The direction you give to creators is equally important as your script you give them

So many people (including me) have fucked up here. Small details matter a lot here, for example:

We don't allow any creators to use a tripod or phone clamp. Phone has to be held in one hand or else set down on a kitchen counter etc while filming. Just even that one small detail gives a much more organic feel to the video and you'll see much better results on average

They also aren't allowed to record in a staged area (against plain wall background etc) they have to be outside on a walk, in a car, walking around the house, lying down in bed or on a couch or doing some task like cooking / prepping food / doing their hair or makeup

take some of your 'losing' scripts, re-record with direction like this and i'd guarantee you see better results
