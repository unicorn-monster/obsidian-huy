"google ads is a remarketing channel"

"google ads is a branded search channel"

"google ads doesn't scale like meta"

bro we took a brand from $57k in ad spend to $1.21M in revenue at 21x ROAS. let me explain what google ads produces when you build the full ecosystem

the misconception comes from how 99% of agencies set up google ads for ecom brands

they run one PMAX campaign, maybe a branded search campaign, capture the lowest-hanging fruit and call the account done. the ROAS looks decent because they're only capturing BOF traffic already going to convert

then they tell the founder "google is a support channel," and the founder believes them because there's been no other experience to compare against

the real google ads is an ecosystem with 8+ campaign types running simultaneously across every layer of the funnel:

demand gen with youtube shorts fills the top of funnel with the same UGC your meta team produces, reaching two billion+ monthly users and filling the remarketing pool with qualified viewers

MOF search with advertorials captures problem-aware buyers at 4-13x ROAS. "why do i wake up at 3am" routed to an advertorial, never a PDP. cold traffic converts because the landing experience matches the buyer's awareness level

comparison page conquesting intercepts competitor traffic at 2-4x higher conversion rates than generic shopping

shopping, segmented by margin with keyword-first titles. the feed drives 80% of shopping performance and nearly every brand has the feed wrong

then remarketing, branded search, PMAX, and bing mirror

the brands treating google as a system are scaling to numbers meta founders don't believe exist:

an automotive brand went from $57k spend to $1.21M revenue at 21x ROAS. a men's fashion brand grew from A$4k spend and A$52k value to A$31k spend and A$169k value in 90 days. a jewelry brand hit 8-figure months with a 42% gift loop repeat rate. one client generated $1.4M in ecosystem revenue from $203k in spend over six months

and the compounding effect is what separates google from meta at scale

meta creative fatigues every 14-21 days. google search and shopping print for months because intent-based traffic rotates naturally with fresh queries. youtube shorts, demand gen, and display fatigue on a similar cycle to meta, so those layers need a rolling refresh while the search and shopping winners compound

the data compounds, quality scores improve, and CPAs drop every week as the algorithm gets sharper on more conversion data across more campaign types

google is the profit engine making the entire business more efficient
