here are the exact prompts i use to automate google ads creatives with GPT images 2.0 and seedance 2.5

shopping statics, display banners, youtube shorts, all of them

prompt 1, the shopping grid static:

"enhance this product photo for a google shopping listing. place the product in a real environment matching [ICP's aspirational world]. include a human element mid-action, natural pose, nobody posing for camera. lighting: [warm for wellness / cool for tech / dramatic for luxury]. add one imperfect environmental detail (a coffee cup, an open notebook, rumpled fabric) so the frame reads lived-in instead of stock. 1080x1080. a candid moment shot on a high-end phone, NOT a studio product shoot"

the last line does the heavy lifting. perfection is the tell on AI images

prompt 2, the display and demand gen banner:

"create a display ad image for [product]. show the RESULT of using the product rather than the product itself. the viewer's state before is [pain]. after is [desired state]. use GPT images 2.0 for text rendering. headline overlay: [copy], bold weight, top-left. output 1200x628 and 1080x1080"

GPT images 2.0 handles text inside images better than anything else right now. reach for the model whenever a headline needs to sit ON the creative

prompt 3, the youtube short from a winning static:

"animate this image with seedance 2.5. 15 seconds, 9:16 vertical, seamless loop. motion: [person picking up and examining product / slow camera push into product in environment / before-after scene transition]. the FIRST frame must be the hook, no build up. warm natural lighting. subtle motion, nothing dramatic"

seedance 2.5 runs 30 seconds in a single pass now, no stitching, and character consistency holds across the whole clip. shipped July 31

prompt 4, the batch variant generator:

"generate 3 more variants of the winning creative. variant A: hold the visual style, change the hook. variant B: hold the hook, change the environment. variant C: hold the angle, change the model demographic"

run the generator weekly against whatever won. one variable changed per variant or you learn nothing

full pipeline cost per batch: around $15-20 in credits

the same scope from an agency: $3-8k
