One of many examples of why you should charge shipping, especially if you're low AOV

Lets say you sell a supplement for $24.99 and your total COGS is $8 and processing costs are at 3%

If you were to charge just $2.99 shipping your margin goes from 65% to 77%

B/E ROAS goes from 1.54 to 1.29

If you charge $3.99 shipping your margin goes from 65% to 80%

Your B/E ROAS goes from 1.54 to 1.24

That can be the difference between loosing money on the front end and being profitable. Could be life or death for some

And nobody that really wants a product is bouncing for $3-4
