We were running our pop-up forms on Klaviyo and kept getting stuck around 8-10% sign-up rate.

Switched to Alia Popups and on the very first couple weeks A/B testing we jumped to 21.7%.

Doubled the brand's email list growth from when they had their Klaviyo form.

Reminder to not just get your brand on Alia and leave it... TEST.

Initially we were getting 12% opt-ins (still a 25-50% increase from Klaviyo)...

But only through testing did we get it to 2x their sign-ups.
