You should not follow the media buying or creative advise from big supplement / subscription brands

90% of these brands are scaling and spending so much not because they are great at creative or running ad accounts

It's because of the way they re-bill people. Some of these brands are re-billing customers at double the price of initial purchase and scaling at like a 0.3 ROAS

the reality is even a basic marketer can scale a brand like that to multiple 5 figures to 6 figures a day in spend. I'd guarantee that if they even just re-billed people at the price they signed up at the brand would die

It does not take much skill to scale a brand the way some of these brands are running things

When looking for creative inspiration or media buying advice imo it's better to look at brands that are scaling but doing it despite having a much worse business model

These are the brands making creatives that really understand and engage their audience as well as getting super cheap CPC's and high CVR's

They fully rely on the front end, it's do or die for them. Not saying needing to rely on the first purchase for profitability is a smart thing but it forges the best marketers, there's no question
