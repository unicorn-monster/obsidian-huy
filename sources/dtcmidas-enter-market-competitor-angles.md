title: Enter market by fixing what competitors do wrong
---

The easiest way to enter a market with existing competitors is to start with the angles already working for them.

Then show why the other solutions don't work or have failed them, and why yours is better, different and will work (eg your unique mechanism)

Go through their reviews and comments and check what people keep complaining about?

Then figure out how to solve that with your product. Here are some simple examples:

True Classic, hide the dad bod angle, but people don't like the polyester. So you can run the same angle, but with a 100% cotton shirt

Magnesium for better sleep, people complain the pills are too big. So you can run the same sleep angle, but yours are liquid drops that can mix into a drink.

You don't need to reinvent the angle, just fix what people don't like about the current product.
