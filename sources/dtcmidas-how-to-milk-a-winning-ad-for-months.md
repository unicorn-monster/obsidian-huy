Ecom & running ads really is a game of running experiments, finding what works and milking it until it stops.

Here's how you squeeze everything out of it when you find a winning ad:

For Video:

- Add new overlay text hooks (new text in first 3 seconds)
- Change the visual hooks (new opening 3 seconds)
- Send the same script to a new creator
- Change the b-roll while keeping the same voiceover
- Add new testimonials or social proof
- Make a greenscreen ad with a creator reacting to the video
- Change the location (car, living room, dog park, etc.)
- Adjust video length (make it shorter/longer)
- Same message, different format (podcast, UGC, AI voiceover + b-roll)
- Turn winning hooks into static ads

For Statics:

- Keep visual, swap headlines
- Keep headline, swap visuals
- Turn image into a GIF
- Add motion to the visual
- Add urgency or social proof elements
- Turn winning headlines into video hooks and scripts

One proven concept can fuel months of profitable campaigns if you systematically iterate instead of chasing the next shiny creative idea.
