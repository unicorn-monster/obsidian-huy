A surprising amount of people don't know this so gonna post it for those affected by the latest pricing updates for shipping to the US from China

You can set completely separate exact pricing and compare at pricing for the USA without affecting the pricing in other markets. Shopify tell you this isn't possible but it is

Make sure you have US set up as it's own market in Shopify

Go to products -> click export. You'll get a CSV of all your products and their pricing and compare at pricing for each market

Set the pricing for US to account for new pricing updates and save the CSV

Then go back to shopify and go to products -> import and upload the new CSV

Your pricing and compare at pricing will now be separate for US and whatever you set it to in the CSV

Also don't forget to charge Shipping for US, I always do and never see any drop-off at checkout if reasonable
