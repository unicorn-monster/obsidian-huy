The easiest way to improve your ads, product- and landing pages? 
Ask people who just bought. 
Post-purchase surveys are one of the most underrated data sources out there.  You're getting insights directly from customers while their buying decision is still fresh. 
Here are some questions you should ask

- What almost stopped you from buying today?

- What questions or doubts did you have while shopping on our site?|

- What was the main reason you decided to buy? 

- What problem are you hoping this will solve? 

- Who are you buying this for?

- How did you compare us to other options before buying? 

Let them give open-ended answers, don't do multiple choice.

The gold is in how customers describe things in their own words. That could be the copy for your next winning ad.

Some tools I suggest to set this up:

- KNO Commerce
- ZigPoll
- Triple Whale Post Purchase

To get more responses, offer store credit or a chance to win a full refund on their order.

Set time weekly to go through responses and actually implement what you learn.

Your performance will improve guaranteed.
