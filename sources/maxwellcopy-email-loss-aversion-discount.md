This is a fire email…

Instead of running a normal "10% off" sale, you tell the customer $10 has already been added to their account and it disappears if they don't use it.

Same discount code under the hood BUT losing $20 you already have psychologically stings way more than skipping $20 off that anybody can claim.

Try it on your next sale.
