Media Buying Decision Making 101

Your ad performance lives in one of these 4 quadrants:

Low CAC + High Volume = Winner

→ Action:  Scale aggresively. This is the sweet spot.

Low CAC + Low Volume = Missed Opportunity

→ Action:  You're being a pussy. Scale up!

High CAC + High Volume = Fixable 

You have demand but need to optimize. 

→ Action: Reduce spend, fix your funnel/ads/offer, then scale again.

High CAC + Low Volume = Problem

You should never allow yourself to be in this position, everything's broken.

→ Action: Pause, figure out what's wrong, then restart small.
