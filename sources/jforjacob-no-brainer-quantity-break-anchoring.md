People don't actually know what a no-brainer deal is. 50% off isn't it

Starts at the ad level, here's an example

Show creators in your ads all using a 3 pack of your product

Get lots of complaints that you don't offer 3 pack

Base offer on site is a 2 pack (inflated the price at 90%+ margins) so if they want same results they need to buy at least 2 packs

Quantity break options are: 2 pack - $39.99, 4 pack - $49.99

Almost nobody will take 2 pack and when they do margins are silly

This will be more effective than just trying to sell either of these options on their own

4 pack seems like great value because of price anchor from 2 pack

Wouldn't be able to sell the 2 pack on its own at such high markups effectively, as perceived value isn't there

Everybody already wants a minimum of 3

No brainer
