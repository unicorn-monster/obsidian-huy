This is what we launched with + how we scaled budgets 

2 avatars - 4 angles each, so 8 angles is total

20 concepts (so 20 adsets): a mix of ugc, “native” ads, AI animation & statics 

We had a pre-sell page and a pdp

Most traffic went to the pre-sell page, some lower funnel statics went directly to pdp

We started at $250 / day

This is how we scaled budgets

$250 > $450 > $650 > $1000 > $1350 > $1850 > $2500 

This happened in 10 days and in 1 CBO

We did ran some ads on the account before launching to warm it up and get the spend limit higher
