Part 2 of my 30-day plan: the emails themselves.

13 fixes, in the order I'd make them, from the inbox to the button.

What people see in the inbox

1. Write the subject line and preview text (the short line people see after it) together, as one thought. Put the strongest words in the first 35 characters, since phones often cut off the rest.

2. Start a sender name test: "Brand Name" against "Sarah from Brand Name" over your next 3 campaigns.

3. Leave the Promotions tab alone. People expect marketing emails there, and tricks to get into Primary tend to backfire.

The copy

4. Copy 10 lines from your 5-star reviews and use 3 of them, word for word, in your next email.

5. Delete the first paragraph of your next email. If it still makes sense, that's where it should have started.

6. Swap "Shop now" for a button that finishes the offer, like "Get my 20% off."

The design

7. Use one column and big buttons, at least 44 pixels tall, so a thumb can hit them.

8. Open each email on your phone with images turned off. If it makes no sense, move the headline and offer out of the image and into real text.

9. Check dark mode on an iPhone, an Android phone, and desktop Gmail before you send. Logos and see-through images are the usual problems.

Testing

10. Judge tests by clicks and sales. Apple Mail opens a lot of emails automatically, so open rates run high.

11. Test one thing at a time, starting with the subject line or the offer. Leave button colors for later.

12. Run your tests on welcome email 1 or cart email 1. A winner there keeps working for every new subscriber and shopper who comes through.

13. Wait at least 24 hours before you pick a winner, because your most active readers open and click first and skew the early numbers.

Most of these take minutes. The tests take the longest, so start those early.
