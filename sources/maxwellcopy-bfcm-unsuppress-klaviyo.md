Do this before Black Friday:

Unsuppress Klaviyo profiles who may have a chance of buying.

Your Klaviyo account probably has thousands of suppressed profiles sitting there. Past buyers and window shoppers you cut off months ago to keep the list clean and the bill down.

But those are people you already paid to acquire. And if anyone is ever going to come back and buy, it's during BFCM.

Here's how to get them back and what to do with them:

1. Build The Unsuppress Segment

> Cannot receive email marketing because they were manually suppressed
> Has ever opened an email, OR browsed the site, OR placed an order in the past 730 days
> AND has never marked you as spam
> Look at the size of it, adjust if needed, then unsuppress

2. Go Wide On Launches And Closes

> Send every offer launch offer to the full list, with both email and SMS
> Same for every last chance email — those are your highest intent moments
> Rotate between the full list and a wide 365 or 730 day engaged segment for the days in between

Then after BFCM, suppress anybody who didn't reengage.

Easy.
