Your Shopify store shouldn't be the only place people can buy from you.

You're losing money to people who only trust and buy from marketplaces like Amazon.

Your DTC site should always be your main focus, especially in the beginning.

But once you're running higher ad spend, you need to think about where else you can capture demand.

Because a lot of people just don't trust sites they've never heard of.

But they do trust the big marketplaces.

This creates what's called the halo effect:

Your ads on one channel drive sales on another channel.

Here's how it works:

Someone sees your Meta ad → clicks to your site → doesn't trust buying from an unknown brand → leaves.

But they remember your brand name → later search it on Google → see your Amazon listing → trust Amazon → buy.

Without that Amazon listing, that sale never happens.

You've already paid to generate that demand with your ads, you're just not capturing it because you're only selling on Shopify.

There's a massive group of people who ONLY shop on Amazon.

They won't buy from your Shopify store no matter how good your funnel is.

They want the trust, the fast shipping, the easy returns.

Plus, Amazon reviews carry way more weight than reviews on your own site.

You can even leverage that social proof back on your Shopify store and in your ads.

You don't need to go all-in on Amazon or make it your priority.

Just having an optimized listing there captures the demand you're currently losing.

Easy win.
