Download the last 60-90 days of invoices from your fulfilment company > compile into a single spreadsheet > upload to chatGPT > ask it to tell you the average shipping cost per country > download last 60-90 days of orders > upload to chatGPT ask it to tell you the AOV & items per order per country > for each country subtract average shipping cost & product cost from AOV > group countries together with similar margin together > create your campaigns based on these groups

You could afford to be spending A LOT more on certain countries and you have no idea. Imagine having a country where you can afford a $50 CPA in the same campaign as a country where you can only afford a $40 CPA. You're leaving so much spend on the table. Enough of this world-wide campaign nonsense
