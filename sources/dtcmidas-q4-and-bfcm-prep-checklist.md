Here's what needs to be done to have a successful Q4:

- Start increasing spend now to fill your top-of-funnel before CPMs spike

- Ensure you have enough stock (scaling hard without inventory will destroy your Q1)

- Set up backup suppliers and shipping options

- Secure sufficient cash flow (get credit lines approved if needed)

- Get your customer support team dialed in and scale headcount if needed

- Finalize all your BFCM offers (also focus on retention offers + aim for higher aov)

- Identify your best-performing content and remake it with bfcm & holiday messaging

- Write your Q4 email campaigns (plan to send more frequently than normal during peak periods)
