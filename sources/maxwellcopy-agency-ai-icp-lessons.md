Just finished a gauntlet of 5 different in-person events from Toronto, to NYC, to Boston, to San Diego, to Cancun.

My 3 main lessons learned:

1. Get on the AI agent wave ASAP
We haven't been using pretty much any AI in our service. Just haven't dedicated time to learn it. It was a waste of time in the past. But now the agents are actually good and there are some apps that make it really easy to build on. Implementing asap.

2. No reason to not be 2x more profitable
I've historically accepted benchmark agency margins for my company. But I'm not trying to be a benchmark company... why would I compare my company to "benchmarks" set by average companies. We should have 2x higher margins. Cut down bloat. Change some of our team structures. Use AI heavier.

3. Your ICP is everything
If we only signed a certain type of client we would be way more profitable. Easier to work with, we generate better results, and they take less resources. Just changing your ICP makes your business as a whole more efficient. All starts with marketing and messaging.
