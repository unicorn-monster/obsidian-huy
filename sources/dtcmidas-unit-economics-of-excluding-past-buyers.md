The “unit economics” of excluding past buyers are very simple.

Acquisition campaigns should drive net-new customers.

If 10–20% of your spend is wasted on people who already converted, your NC CAC inflates and efficiency drops.

“But higher CPMs” 🤡🤡🤡

Excluding small audiences (past purchasers) barely impacts CPMs.

If it does, you probably have another problem, but that’s not the point here.

Even if my CPMs rise, which I don’t care about, my NC CAC is still lower and my NC ROAS is higher when I exclude past purchasers.

And those are the only KPIs I really care about when running ads.
