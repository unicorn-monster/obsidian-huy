One of the most useful things i've found you can do for getting good results with ChatGPT image gen is instead of giving it a reference image and asking it to recreate it:

Find a photo you like and ask ChatGPT to create a prompt that describes the style of photography used

You can then give it any product and use the prompt it gave you as a styling guide

Here's an example prompt I used for a supplement product (results were amazing)

Editorial-Style Flat Lay: Items are arranged in a top-down (or slightly angled) view with thoughtful placement, giving it a curated and intentional composition. It's functional but designed to be visually interesting.

Hard Lighting & Defined Shadows: The use of strong, directional light creates dramatic shadows and sharp contrast. This technique adds energy, a sense of realism, and makes the objects pop. It's often used to give a more tactile, dimensional feel.

Monochromatic Background Integration: The backdrop ties in with the product colours for a cohesive, branded aesthetic. This creates a modern, design-forward feel.

Texture & Light Play: The way the light hits the product creates shine and specular highlights, making the materials feel tangible and premium
