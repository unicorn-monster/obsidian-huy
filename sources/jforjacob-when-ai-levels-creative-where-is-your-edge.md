My prediction for where AI and advertising is going with Meta

I believe creative assets will effectively become like catalog ads

Where Meta knows what products you sell, it can match that to a user based on what it knows about them and then can dynamically generate creative assets based on what style of content that person usually consumes and interacts with

I believe for the most part you won't need to make creatives if you don't want to, Meta will handle it all

If that's the case and Meta can serve the perfect ad to the perfect person at the perfect time and everyone has access to that, how does anyone have an edge? How can anyone actually pull ahead?

It will all come down to distribution, unit economics, supplier relationships, CRO, retention, brand and community

I truly believe creative will become a level playing field for everyone
