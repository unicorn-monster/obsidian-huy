Don't be afraid to test new things in your ad account.

When Meta launches new features, they don't do it randomly.

There's a good reason behind it.

For example, the budget sharing feature they recently added to ABO.

We switched a few of our product campaigns to it and it's crushing.

Also play around with different settings.

- 1-day click vs 7-day click attribution.
- Incremental vs standard attribution.
- Bid/Cost Caps vs Highest Volume.

Online you'll find dozens of people preaching what the best media buying setup is.

But the truth is there's no best setup.

If someone's claiming there is, they're either trying to sell you something  or they're a complete retard.

Every business is different.

What works for someone else might not work for you.

The only way to find out best setup for your account is by testing new things regularly.
