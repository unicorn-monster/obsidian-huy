Please, please, please go sit down and watch this

This explanation highlights the big problem I have and the fundamental flaw with the "1 CBO setup" I see a lot of guys running

The point of a testing campaign for me is not necessarily to be profitable with it. It's about isolating variables, making incremental improvements and identifying patterns in a controlled environment. This is how you not only find real winners but actually understand the specific thing that make them winners

Not only that but you want to find the best version of an ad before putting it to the real test and seeing how it stands up at high budget. If you're just publishing everything into the same CBO, you will never be able to do this

There are so many flaws with the 1 CBO setup. God knows how many potential winners have been sent to the graveyard because they didn't get any spend, you didn't get any data etc because they were competing with an ad with already hundreds of thousands in spend and tons of engagement, so you didn't iterate

Like mentioned before, almost all my big winners have been 'Frankenstein' ads made from incremental changes found through controlled testing in a testing campaign. Ads that would have stood no chance of spend if published and forced to compete immediately with already proven winners
