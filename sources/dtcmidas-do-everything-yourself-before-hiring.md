You should do everything yourself in the beginning.

- Edit your own videos.
- Write your own copy.
- Build your own store.
- Set up your own email flows.
- Run your own ads.

That’s the only way you actually get good at this and understand how it works.

How can you manage someone later if you have zero clue how the work actually gets done?

You can’t evaluate their output, you can’t tell if they’re good at their job, and you can’t provide useful feedback.

When you’ve done it yourself, you know:

- What’s hard vs easy (so you don’t make unreasonable demands)

- What good work looks like (so you can spot mediocrity)

- Where bottlenecks happen (so you can actually solve problems)

- What to prioritize (so you’re not optimizing the wrong things)

Once you understand how the fundamentals work, you can then hire and delegate effectively.

But skipping the learning phase and immediately hiring people to do everything is how you build a fragile business.
