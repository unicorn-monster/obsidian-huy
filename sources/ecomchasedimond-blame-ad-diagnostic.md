title: Before blaming the ad check post-click funnel
---

Before you blame the ad, check what happens after the click.

When customer acquisition costs rise, it's tempting to ask for new creative.

But a sold-out product, an expired offer, or a checkout issue could be part of the problem.

Here's a review you can run before changing the campaign:

1. Follow the click.

Open your ad on your phone.

Is the product in stock? Does the offer match what the ad promises? Can you finish checkout?

Write down anything that needs attention.

2. Ask your attribution tool what changed.

Add your target acquisition cost and any recent changes to promotions or stock, then ask:

"Compare the last 7 completed days with the previous 7. Show ad spend, new-customer orders, acquisition cost, and site conversion where available. Keep metric definitions and attribution settings consistent. Flag the biggest changes, show the evidence, and recommend what to check next. Say what you can't verify."

3. Review the evidence, then make it recurring.

Check how many orders sit behind the change. Make sure the comparison makes sense. Add any context that would be helpful.

For supported actions (based on the recommendations you asked for), choose "Ask every time" so you can review proposed changes before they run.

Once you're happy with the review, automate it to run each week.

That's a useful habit to build before Black Friday: check the buying experience, compare the numbers, and decide what actually needs to change.
