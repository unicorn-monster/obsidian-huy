People wonder why their CVR swings from day to day

Their are several factors in this like: Different ads taking different amounts of spend. Natural weekly, monthly and yearly buying behaviour cycles

But there is one thing people never seem to think about

One thing I'll ask people if they are seeing big swings outside of normal in their CVR is "have you changed anything on your website"

The answer is always no. But 99.9% of the time that is not true

Your reviews section is always changing day to day. One day you might have highly detailed and relevant reviews at the top of your reviews widget, the next day short simple reviews like 'great product'

This is generally the only section on someones landing page that changes content dynamically but people never seem to think about it effecting CVR

recommend pinning your best and most relevant reviews to the top so they never move to make sure they are always visible and you don't get this issue of your PDP's changing every day
