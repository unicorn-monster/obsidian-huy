Quick tip to get better at anything:

Create a 50+ page guide / research paper on it…

Force yourself to sit down every day and crank out 1-2 hours on the topic.

When you make yourself break something down into a 50 page master guide, it forces you to do outside research, find examples, articulate your thoughts, and dumb things down for someone else.

All of that deepens your own understanding more than anything else you could do.

It also takes all your random theories and half-formed ideas you have and turns them into structured lessons.

Recently did this for an ecommerce subscription brand master guide…

It was incredible because I already had all these theories about offers and onboarding and retention, but I didn't have a central framework.

I just had the vague idea that onboarding, offers, etc should probably work a certain way.

I'd never actually structured those thoughts or laid them out in a way someone else or even myself could follow...

But building that guide forced me to go find real examples, break every piece down, and actually connect it all together.

Furthered my understanding more than anything else I've done.

I've done this for welcome flows, cart abandonment, post-purchase, segmentation, copywriting, email design. Absolutely everything.

It's the most effective way I've found to learn something.

Even more effective than learning by doing…

Teaching it >>> consuming it
