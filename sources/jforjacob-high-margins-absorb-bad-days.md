Fluctuations are always gonna happen

That's why it's so important to have high margins and a strong back end

If on normal days your margins are low (sub 20%) you're always gonna get shook on these bad days

But if you have high margins, solid retention from email etc, bad days on the front end don't affect you so much. You're just less profitable on those days
