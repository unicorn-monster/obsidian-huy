How to Diagnose Any Failing Email

Before you change anything, run this 2-minute check:

1. Pull your last 5 campaigns side by side.
2. Check in order: spam rate in Google Postmaster Tools, opens, clicks, orders.
3. Stop at the first one that's below your normal.
4. Fix only that step, then send again.

Treat opens as a rough signal since Apple Mail can inflate them. If you landed in spam, a better subject line won't help yet. If clicks are fine but sales aren't, the email did its job and the page needs the work.
