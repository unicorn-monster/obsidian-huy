title: Which pricing tactic to use per sale email type
---

Quick way to use this: look at your next sale email and use the one that fits your price. Selling a gift? Round number. Selling a subscription? Break it down to a daily cost. Don't try all seven in one email.

And check the red tag before you send. Most of these blow up when the buyer feels tricked.
