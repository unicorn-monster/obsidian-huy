August agency stats and update:

- 16 new brands signed, including multiple high 8 figure and 9 figure brands
- Highest average deal size month ever, prices and deal scopes steadily keep increasing
- Most calls booked in a month ever
- Dropped the ball on hiring / training… couldn't keep up demand and had a 4 week waitlist for brands who signed contract and paid invoice but we couldn't onboard

Interesting month at the agency…

Another strong sales month and one of our highest demand months ever in terms of calls booked, so we've got an incredibly full pipeline.

However we had some key team members start to transition out and accept roles in different industries (excited for them), which put us in a tough spot on fulfillment.

We hadn't forecasted for that kind of constraint, team members leaving at the same time as all these new brands coming in.

So we had to go back to a waitlist to properly shift accounts around and train up new people.

Weird timing too because we'd just brought on another sales strategist to take calls since I anticipated a massive rush and was hoping to bring on more brands ahead of BFCM. And we did… But we became constrained internally way faster than I expected.

So now we're in this weird spot with heavy demand, and we're closing onboardings mid to late October to focus on BFCM for our current clients.

We continue to be constrained on talent and fulfillment. Really fucking pissing me off that we haven't figured that out yet.

But we shall learn and grow.
