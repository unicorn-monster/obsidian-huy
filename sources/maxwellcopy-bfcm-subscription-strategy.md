title: BFCM active subscriber exclusion playbook

If you're running BFCM on a subscription brand, do NOT fully blast your active subscribers with your sitewide offer.

They'll see a better deal than the one they're on and cancel to resub at the promo rate.... which means you just gave back revenue you already had and reset their tenure.

However you don't just want to exclude them fully since they are your best customers...

Here's what to do instead:

- Exclude active subs from MOST of your BFCM campaigns
- Send them a couple of the core offer messages so they're not fully left out. They need to do gifting too and will buy for their friends/family.
- Build them a separate custom bonus offer that adds revenue instead of replacing it
- Never discount their next subscription order
- Offer 50% off a one-time add-on to their next order or to add another subscription
- Use your platform's add-on functionality so it lands in the existing queue

BFCM should add subscription revenue without cannibalizing your current subscription revenue.
