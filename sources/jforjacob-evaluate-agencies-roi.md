Do they make me more money than I pay them?

If yes, keep paying them

If no, stop paying them

It really doesn't need to get more complicated than that

See so many people have crazy and unrealistic expectations for agencies and it's stupid

If there is positive ROI, even if small, keep the train rolling. It's really that simple
