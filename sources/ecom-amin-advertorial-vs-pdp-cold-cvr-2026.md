google ads funnels are PRINTING 3-5x what normal landing pages produce on the same traffic

and 90% of brands are still sending EVERY click to a product page

let me break down why the landing destination matters more than the campaign

someone searches "why do i wake up tired" on google. your ad shows up. they click. they land on a product page for a magnesium supplement

price. features. buy now button

they leave in 3 seconds. not because the product is bad. because the page assumed the buyer already trusts you, already knows the category works, and already has their wallet out

they don't. they were searching a problem. they landed on a checkout

an advertorial fixes this by walking the buyer through four steps BEFORE they ever see a product:

step one, it mirrors the exact pain they searched for. "the real reason you wake up at 3am (and why melatonin makes it worse)." the reader's brain says "this person understands me." rapport built. not brand trust. personal trust

step two, it explains the hidden cause. something new. cortisol cycling between 2am and 4am disrupting deep sleep even when you're "sleeping" for 8 hours. the reader is now cognitively invested. leaving means leaving with an incomplete understanding

step three, it introduces the mechanism. not the product. not the brand. the biological pathway the product addresses. the trust transfers from the writer to the logic itself

step four, the product appears. not as a brand pitch. as the best implementation of a mechanism the reader already believes in

the reader NEVER trusts the brand. they trust the logic. the brand becomes the delivery vehicle

we ran this exact structure on a supplement account. cold search traffic through an advertorial converted at 2.8%. the same traffic sent directly to the PDP converted at 0.9%

same keywords. same budget. same product. the ONLY difference was what page the click landed on

the advertorial is one of six funnel types that work on google right now. advertorials for cold problem-aware traffic. quiz funnels for warm category traffic. comparison pages for conquest campaigns bidding on competitor names. listicles for "best of" ranking searches. long sales copy for high-ticket products. PDPs for branded and shopping ONLY

the right funnel matched to the right traffic temperature is the SINGLE highest leverage change you make in any google ads account
