Here are 6 Kinds of Proof and When Each One Works.

Try this on your next email:

Write down the one doubt a buyer has right before they click.

Then pick the proof that answers that doubt and put it right where the doubt shows up.

Price doubt goes next to the price.

"What if I don't like it" goes right above the button.
