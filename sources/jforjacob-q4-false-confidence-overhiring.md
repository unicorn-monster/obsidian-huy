If your brand is under 1 year old, be careful with hiring new people and making big investments into your brand this time of year

You can get a false sense of confidence that you've cracked the code. You think you've finally nailed your creative or offer and it's all happy scaling from here when in reality it's most likely just the nature of Q4 and the high intent this time of year

I've especially seen this be the case with brands selling highly gift-able products

I've seen friends go from $250k+ a day down to $1k a day after Christmas

Try and stretch the current resources you have as much as possible until after Christmas and see how the dust settles

You don't want to be left standing with your dick in your hands
