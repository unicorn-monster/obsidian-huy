Most brokies give away the only valuable commodity they have and they don't even realise they do it so freely

It's so valuable that multi-billion dollar companies have been built from it and continue to fight over it. Yet the Brokies don't even put up a fight to claim it back

Imagine having a huge pile of cash and everyday you just let someone steal from it. You wouldn't allow it, you'd be outraged, you'd do everything you could to stop it happening. Yet this is exactly what's happening for your attention

There's an invisible war going on and unfortunately you're fighting Goliath. Without conscious intervention, I'm sorry but you are going to get wrecked

Guard your attention at all costs, curate your environment to make it as hard as possible to stray away from the task at hand. Seek out the boring, the un-stimulating and let your mind ruminate. Start small, those 10 minutes in the shower you'd usually listen to music, let your mind wander instead. It's amazing how much more intuition you develop and in-sync you feel when you actually give your mind a chance to day dream and ponder. Ideas come flooding, problems get worked out and creativity blossoms

Boredom has become an extremely rare thing for most people. There's always an infinite pit of hyper stimulating scrolling to occupy you. Understand that this is eating away at the one valuable thing you have

Do something about it
