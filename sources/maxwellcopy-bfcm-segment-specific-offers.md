One interesting thing you can try this BFCM is running segment-specific offers instead of one sitewide discount for everyone.

Which means instead of blasting 30% off to your whole list, you give your VIPs early access, hit your lapsed buyers with a winback angle, and save the straight discount for the people who only ever buy with a code.

Especially useful because the person who's bought five times at full price and the person who's never paid full price shouldn't be seeing the same email.

Here are some segments worth building:

> VIPs: top 10% by revenue over the last 12 months, give them early access 24-48 hours before the sale
> At-risk: bought before but haven't engaged in 6-12 months, best winback window you'll get all year
> Cart abandoners: show them the exact products they left with a deadline
> Price-sensitive: used a code on half their orders, this is who your sitewide discount is actually for
> Full-price buyers: rarely use codes, give them early access or new arrivals instead
> Recent buyers: already bought in your campaign, send recommendations not the same offer again
