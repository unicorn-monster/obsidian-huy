quiz funnels on google cold traffic convert 20-40% higher than advertorials for products above £100 AOV

and the reason is a psychological principle from a casino floor. let me connect the dots

casinos let you pick your own seat at the blackjack table

logically the choice makes zero difference. the cards are the same regardless of where you sit

but the act of choosing creates a sense of control, and a sense of control creates commitment. you picked this seat, so you're invested in this seat

quiz funnels use the exact same mechanism

the buyer answers 4-6 questions about their specific situation. by the time the result page loads, they've invested time, attention, and self-reported data into the process

the recommendation feels like a diagnosis they participated in rather than a pitch

even when 80% of quiz takers end up seeing the same product recommendation

this is cialdini's commitment and consistency principle in action

every quiz answer is a micro-commitment, and answering 4-6 questions creates psychological investment. walking away means abandoning something they started

and humans have a deep compulsion to act consistently with their own statements. if someone answered "yes, i struggle with back pain" and "yes, i've tried three products with no results," their brain expects the next action to align with those answers

the result page feels like the logical next step, not because the quiz sold them, but because their own answers led them there

the higher the AOV, the more this matters

a £30 supplement gets bought on impulse after an advertorial. the price sits low enough for the buyer to skip ownership over the decision

a £300 skincare device or a premium jewelry piece plays differently. the buyer needs to feel like they arrived at the conclusion themselves, and the quiz creates the feeling in 60 seconds

we use quiz funnels for jewelry brands above £100 AOV and the conversion lift vs standard PDPs runs 20-40%. the data from the quiz also feeds back into google ads targeting, remarketing segments, and ad copy

the quiz functions as an intelligence engine sharpening the entire account over time, rather than a plain landing page
