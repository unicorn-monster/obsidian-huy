gruns runs $1.2M a month through google ads and sold to unilever for $1.2 BILLION in just under 3 years

i broke down their entire funnel piece by piece. the AI youtube ads, the branded defense, the shopping domination, the PDP

steal the structure:

the youtube ads first, because they're a masterclass

some of their AI-generated spots sit at 1M+ views. and when you dissect the composition, nothing in them is accidental

lighting and colour: everything swims in greens and yellows. the brand IS the palette. you know it's gruns before their logo appears

avatars: they run separate creatives for separate ICPs. the parent fighting the veggie war with their kid. the adult who gags swallowing pills. the GLP-1 user with nutrient gaps. one product, 6+ ICPs, each with their own ad

word choice: zero supplement-industry language. "escape the veggie wars" instead of "comprehensive micronutrient support." it's the language a mate would use to take the piss out of a dinner table standoff

awareness calibration: these ads target problem-aware viewers (my kid won't eat vegetables, pills make me gag) in a stage 4 sophistication market drowning in greens powders. so the mechanism does the lifting: 60+ ingredients, in a gummy, that tastes like candy

hook and pacing: absurdist AI visuals in the first 2 seconds force a double take. cuts every 2-3 seconds hold it. the weirdness GRABS them by the eyeballs

unique concept: jingles and meme-energy animation. an earworm plays in your head at the grocery store. that's branding doing sales work

now the boring stuff underneath the ads, where the money actually gets made:

branded search locked at the top of their own name, because every viral video creates 1,000s of searches and they're not donating those to AG1

non-branded shopping where they sit #1 on "greens gummies" with keyword-first titles and packaging built to pop in a grid of identical tubs

and a PDP loaded with social proof, subscription-first pricing, and 0 friction. the video sold the feeling, the page removes the doubt

the ads make people curious
branded search collects everyone who googles gruns after an ad
shopping is where they beat everyone still selling powder in a tub
the PDP handles whatever doubt is left

3 years, one product, and a funnel where nothing they paid for leaks out
