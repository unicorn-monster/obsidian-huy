Copywriting Tip:

Make your Calls-to-action specific.

"Buy now" -> "Get instant access + lifetime updates"
"Sign up" -> "Get weekly value-packed emails"
"Join today" -> "Join 11,920 other marketers inside"

More specific = more clicks = more $$$.
