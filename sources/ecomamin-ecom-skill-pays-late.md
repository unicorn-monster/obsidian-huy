mfs quit their ecom store after 3 weeks

then pay $997 for a course on "finding winners"

the product was never the problem

they just never stayed long enough to learn anything

ecom is a skill that pays you late

and most people want to get paid before they've learned it

here's what nobody wants to hear:

- your first product teaches you, it rarely pays you
- you're not selling a product, you're selling a fix for something that's been bugging someone for months
- people buy with emotion and justify with logic. your page has to do both
- if you can't explain why someone needs it in one sentence, neither can your ad
- nobody trusts a store they found 10 seconds ago. reviews, guarantees, a real face, a phone number
- the customer isn't thinking about your brand. they're thinking about themselves
- a bad month is feedback, not a verdict on you
- every store that prints had a stretch where it looked dead

the real skill isn't finding winners

it's sitting in the uncomfortable part long enough to understand your customer better than they understand themselves

that's where the money is

most people leave right before they get there
