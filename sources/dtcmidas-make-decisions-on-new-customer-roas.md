title: "Make every decision on new-customer ROAS or new-customer CPA, and aim for a 3:1 LTV to CAC ratio"
---

Always make your decisions on New Customer (NC) ROAS or NC CPA.

In the beginning you don’t know your cltv, so try to be profitable or at least break even. As your brand progresses you can start lowering your target as you get a better understanding of your cltv.

I do always aim for a 3:1 ltv:cac ratio.
