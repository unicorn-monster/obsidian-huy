I did a few consulting calls this week.

And what created the biggest “aha moment” for brand owners was when I talked about building out full customer experiences with your ads and funnels.

It goes way further than ad to landing page congruency.

Here’s what I mean with building out a full experience.

So for example if you’re running a skincare brand:

Get a dermatologist as an (AI) creator for your ad.

Then make a Facebook page with that dermatologist’s name and photo, run the ad from that page.

Make sure your primary text and headline sound like they’re written by that same dermatologist.

Send traffic to an advertorial that focusseston the same angle as your ad, written like it’s on the dermatologist’s blog.

Then direct them to a product page that feels specifically designed for their exact problem.

So why does this work?

Every touchpoint reinforces the same narrative. 

Your customer doesn’t feel like they’re being sold to, they feel like they’re following a natural path that leads to the perfect solution for their specific situation.

A lot of brands optimize individual elements, but the real leverage is in optimizing the entire journey as one connected experience.
