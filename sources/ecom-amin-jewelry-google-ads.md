while i was co-scaling a jewelry brand past $1M+/mo with google ads...

this was BY FAR our winning product:

a sunflower necklace with "you are my sunshine" engraved on it

let me break down every psychology trigger that made this sell 10 times more than our usual:

(and have an almost 0% refund rate)

trigger 1: emotional permanence

this product is obviously meant to be gifted

the message "you are my sunshine" clearly defines that

and if you were to buy this for a loved one, like a girlfriend for example, you'd be telling her "this is how i feel about you"

now here comes the genius part:

every time your girlfriend would put on this necklace she'd remember you, why you gave it, and the moment she received it

and you don't return captured emotions

the refund rate on this product was 0.8%.
our average on other jewelry: 6.2%

returning it means rejecting someone's love. psychologically impossible for most women

trigger 2: the gift loop

42% of women who received this necklace became recurring customers within 90 days

she wears it. posts it on instagram. her friends ask where it's from. she feels indebted to reciprocate. buys a gift for the person who gave it to her. from the same brand

AOV + LTV maxxing ladies and gents

one customer generates a second without additional ad spend. the acquisition cost per pair drops by half just like that

trigger 3: vague personalisation

"you are my sunshine" feels personal even though it's mass-produced

the buyer thinks "this is perfect for them because i call them sunshine." even if they never used that word before lol

the product creates the relationship dynamic. not reflects it. generic enough to apply to anyone. specific enough to feel intentional

trigger 4: perceived value stacking

two charms: the engraved circle plus the sunflower. total cost to add the second charm: £0.30

customer perception: "they included an extra charm. definitely worth £67." 2 items feel more valuable than 1 even when the second costs pennies

now here's where our google ads ecosystem actually made this print 8 figures

meta was driving volume but the margins were thin. CPMs climbing every quarter. creative team was burning out

google operated on completely different economics. the traffic was intent-based. someone searching "romantic necklace gift for girlfriend" was already 80% sold. the conversion rates were higher and the CPA was lower on every sale google drove

so we segmented by buyer motivation

boyfriends got campaigns emphasising romance and commitment. mothers got campaigns around lasting family bonds. friends got campaigns around thoughtfulness and personalisation

all the same exact product, but with 3 emotional entry points. each converting at different rates

youtube shorts ran UGC-style content: unboxing clips, reaction videos of women receiving it. we just repurposed winning meta creatives here and scaled cold into an untapped channel with cheap CPCs

the result: £30k-£40k days consistently. million dollar months. google didn't replace meta, it made the entire business more profitable. the blended margins improved across everything

and the product got easier to sell over time.

month 1 conversion rate: 2.1%

month 6: 3.8%

reviews compounded. recipients became buyers. word of mouth spread

all our products had creative fatigue. this one had basically infinite creative momentum

the ecosystem amplified every psychological trigger baked into the product. and the compound effect scaled it to 8 figures

secret is to find products that trigger emotional responses. then build the ecosystem around them.
