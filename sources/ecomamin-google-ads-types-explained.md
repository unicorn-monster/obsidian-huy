literally the simplest explanation of google ads campaigns you'll ever read

explained like you're 12:

say google is a giant mall and your store is one of the shops

1. search: someone walks up to the info desk and asks "where can i buy a posture corrector?" and the desk points to you. you only pay when they walk over

2. shopping: your product sits in the front window with the price on it, right where people are already looking. they see what it is and what it costs before they walk in

3. pmax: you hand google all your signs and say "put them wherever the buyers are". info desk, windows, tv screens, mailboxes. google decides everything. great once it knows who buys from you, on a brand new store it's guessing with your money

4. youtube: the ad on the big tv screens around the mall. people didn't come for you, you're interrupting them, but they get to see your product in action

5. demand gen: posters along the walkways people scroll past (youtube, shorts, gmail, discover). the closest thing google has to meta ads

6. display: flyers handed out in thousands of other buildings across town (websites and apps that partner with google)

and remarketing isn't its own campaign. it's chasing people who already walked into your shop and left without buying. you can add it to most of these
