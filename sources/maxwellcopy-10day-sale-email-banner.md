title: 10-day sale email banner offer visibility hack
---

If I was running a 10 day sale and wanted every email to actually get read, I'd throw a small banner at the top of every single one with the offer and code on it.

1) Subscribers see the offer within a second of opening
2) The rest of the email is free to be about anything you want
3) Your content stays fresh across the whole promo instead of getting stale by day three

Each email still does its job of pushing the offer… except now you can send brand story, product education, founder notes, whatever, and the offer never stops being visible.

The problem is most brands run a sale and send the same pitch seven times… but by day four people stop opening because they already know what's inside.

Instead now with a banner ON TOP someone can still see the actual offer… Everything else in the email is pretty much yours to play with.
