here's what i learned by trying to automate singing ads...

- you really need to tell ai what is a good visual
- do not generate images, clips literally
- chorus confuses AI on what to generate
- image-to-video works better
- that's it...

this cut our ai singing ads production time to less than 1 hour for a 5-6 min video with post editing making sure there are no mistakes and small generations.

not bad tbh
