You could fight all day every day getting your website conversion rate from 2% to 2.5%...

OR just increase your marketing output by 25% and get the same result.

You could fight all day every day trying to get your close rate from 20% to 25%...

OR just spend a little extra time every day making better content...

Could be more content, more sends, more offers in front of more people, etc...

Just. Do. More.

Volume negates any leak in your funnel.
