title: 6 Black Friday email deliverability mistakes to avoid
---

6 mistakes that send your Black Friday emails to spam:

1. Mailing people who went cold in spring. Win back or remove anyone inactive 90+ days.

2. Spiking volume overnight. Ramp up in steps, most engaged first.

3. Sending from a brand-new domain. Keep your domain and pass SPF, DKIM, DMARC.

4. Hiding the unsubscribe. Add one-click unsubscribe (Gmail wants it honored within 48 hours).

5. Using link shorteners. Use links on your own domain.

6. Checking complaints too late. Check spam rate daily and stay under 0.1%.

Go over 0.3% and Google won't help with delivery issues until you've stayed under it for 7 straight days.

Black Friday is Nov 27. Start these this week.
