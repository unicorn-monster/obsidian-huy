google's own youtube ads data for ecom brands is INSANE

a paid youtube campaign produced a 10x increase in search volume for 1 brand

plus a 49% lift in ad recall, and 52% incremental conversions, the ones on top of what its other channels were already driving

and the average ecom founder still treats youtube as "the awareness thing we'll try later." here's what you're leaving on the table:

the brand was marley spoon, the meal kit company, and google ran a case study on it to measure what video does to a buying journey

people watched the video ads, got curious, and searched the brand on google, where its search campaigns caught them. so the video ads created the SEARCHES, and the search campaigns closed the sales

10x the search volume means the youtube campaign kept filling those search campaigns with people who already knew the brand

so youtube works as the top of a pipeline you can measure: every dollar on video shows up later as branded search volume you can watch climb week over week

and then google went further

their shoppable video study found that advertisers who added product feeds to video action and demand gen campaigns pulled 60%+ more conversions at lower cost

the feed puts your products directly under the video, so a viewer goes from discovering your product to browsing it to buying it without EVER needing to know your brand beforehand

google has been more than a search engine for years.

youtube, shorts, discover, gmail, shopping, maps and search all run as 1 connected ecosystem, and brands treating google as "just search" are playing 1 instrument in a whole ass orchestra

here's the practical takeaway: your winning meta creatives are already 80% of a youtube campaign. adapt them to the placements, connect your product feed, and let demand gen fill the search campaigns you're already running

unlike a meta ad that's forgotten 3 seconds after it scrolls past, youtube leaves behind searches for your brand name that keep showing up in your dashboard

so before you scale meta ANY further, put your best creatives on youtube and let them fill your search campaigns
