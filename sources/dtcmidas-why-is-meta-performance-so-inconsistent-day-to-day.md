title: "Inconsistency on Meta is normal, so base decisions on longer time frames, not daily swings"
---

You need accept that inconsistency on meta is normal.

Especially at low spend where your daily volume is small, so a few sales can make a big impact on your daily metrics.

One day performs well, you scale, the next day tanks.

But the actual difference in conversions might be tiny.

It just feels massive for you.

If you’re looking at daily performance with low volume, you’re going to see swings that seem catastrophic but are statistically meaningless.

That’s why you need to base your decision making on longer time frames.

If your ROAS over 7 days is hitting your target, daily variance shouldn’t matter.

So stop making decisions based on two or three bad days. 

You’ll cut creatives that were actually working because you panicked by seeing red numbers.

The inconsistency you’re seeing isn’t a bug.

It’s just how the system works.
