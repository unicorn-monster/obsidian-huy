Your email tool and your SMS tool are both messaging the same person about the same abandoned cart, and neither one knows the other exists.

So your customer gets the email, buys, then gets a text 20 minutes later asking them to finish checking out.

You paid for that text message.

It also made you look like nobody at your company is paying attention.

In Omnisend, the channels sit in one automation. Email goes first, and the SMS only fires if the email didn't do the job. Push notifications also live in the same flow.

Fewer tools, and nobody on your list wondering whether your company talks to itself.
