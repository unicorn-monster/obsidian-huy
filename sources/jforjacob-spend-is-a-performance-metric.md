title: "Spend is a performance metric"

One of the main reasons I don't like testing with ABO is because it gives you no idea of Meta's preference for spend

Spend should be treated as a performance metric. If you're manually deciding spend and not giving any flexibility to Meta to where it allocates budget, you're not seeing what is in my opinion the most important thing

I've had an ad at a low budget where the CPA was below the account average and may well have never passed testing in ABO. But as it started to eat spend, the performance just never dropped. It just kept spending more and more until it was the top spender in the account. The overall spend of the account went up as well as revenue & profit

The ad was targeting a huge audience & really engaging. Meta loved spending on it

Something I would never have got to see if it was published in an ABO campaign. It wouldn't have met the CPA target requirement to increase budget

Food for thought
