Changed one of my markets to have free shipping recently

CVR went down, became my worst performing market

Charged shipping again, CVR back up, AOV back up and everything back to normal

CHARGE FOR SHIPPING

There are literally no downsides

Your CVR will not drop

Your AOV will go up

You can leverage it to incentivise higher AOV's

You can drop your front end price, add it on as shipping and ultimately convert more people

You'll get more people in your abandoned cart flows

I'm sorry but "free shipping" is really not incentive for the customer you think it is
