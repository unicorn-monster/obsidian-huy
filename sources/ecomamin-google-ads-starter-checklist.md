if you're starting google ads this year, i'm begging you:

- don't launch 10 campaigns. launch one shopping campaign and let it learn
- don't copy your shopify titles. write what people actually type
- don't touch it every day. 2 weeks before you judge anything
- don't leave search partners and display ticked
- don't run $10 a day and call it a test
- don't sell something nobody searches for
- don't scale the day it works. scale the week it keeps working
- don't let AI pick your product without checking google trends yourself
- don't quit at week 3. that's when most accounts start making sense

google rewards patience more than any platform in ecom

bookmark this for launch day
