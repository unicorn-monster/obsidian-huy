I've found pretty much all my success from being a natural contrarian

Always against the grain

When people zig, I zag

The alpha is always found by doing pretty much the opposite of what the masses do

It's so obvious it's what you need to do to get an edge I've no idea how so many people never see the pattern

How do you expect to get extraordinary results by doing the ordinary?

By definition you need to be doing what others aren't

The creative volume thing is a good example

Everyone preaching how important creative volume is to be able to scale -> everyone starts pumping out as many ads as they can -> 1000 active ads becomes the norm -> you have no edge over anyone else as everyone else is doing the same thing -> ad quality diminished as a result of pursuit of as much volume as possible -> your ads get raped in auctions by people who took the time to research, plan and direct their ads to the best of their ability

This is literally exactly what I did. Heard all the hype and dogma around creative volume, deliberately dialled mine back even more knowing people would start to abandon quality in pursuit of volume. So if i could increase mine even further, the delta between my ads and the competition would get bigger and bigger

Exactly what happened. My 5 ads a week beating out competitors publishing 300+ a week

We scaled a new brand from zero to 7 figs a month in it's 3rd month

It's literally the same with everything

Where the market moves, you need to move in the opposite direction, not follow like a sheep

These people are what is known as exit liquidity
