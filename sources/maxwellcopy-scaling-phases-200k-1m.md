Time to add to this.

Here's what the phases past $200k/mo have looked like:

Phase 7 $300k-500k/mo:
Aggressively scale marketing. Focus on quality, start building marketing team whether it's ads, organic, or whatever, and become a machine for getting leads in the door. Set guardrails on service so clients don't walk over you.

Phase 8 $500k-750k/mo:
Service and offer development, building a senior team around it. Put real initiatives in place to reduce churn and become a stickier service.

Phase 9 $750k-1M/mo:
Build and develop leaders around each department, marketing, sales, and fulfillment, and scale everything at the same time. Making a big bet… another big offer or a different supplementary service.
