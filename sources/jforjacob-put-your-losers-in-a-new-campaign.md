To anyone running a single CBO campaign structure and believes that you should "just trust Meta" to allocate budget the best way possible

Take all your losers that didn't get spend, put them in a new campaign. Watch what happens

This campaign will likely end up being one of your best performers and your overall account spend will go up

It's not always a case of "just make better ads bro"

Meta is super autistic, it will grab onto one or two creatives and not let go for dear life

I've proven this time and time again with my own brands and with countless others I've told to move away from the one campaign setup

You will always end up with better performance and higher spend
