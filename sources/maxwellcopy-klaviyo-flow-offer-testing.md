title: Klaviyo flow offer testing tactics that compound
---

If you only change one thing in your Klaviyo flows, make it your offers...

- Test % off vs $ off in your welcome flow
- Give past buyers a loyalty offer instead of the welcome discount in your abandoned flows
- Run no offer at all in your first two abandonment emails
- Increase the offer by email 3 rather than repeating it
- Try a mystery discount in your cart abandon
- Put a countdown on your checkout abandon
- Test which product you push in your post-purchase PS
- Use a weird number like $7.14 on your winback credit

Testing offers is where the business-changing results come from, so don't sleep on them.

Your whole backend compounds off it...
