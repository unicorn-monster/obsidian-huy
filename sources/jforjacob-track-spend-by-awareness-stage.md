Label every ad in your account with its awareness stage in the title -> create custom reports in Atria or any reporting tool filtered by the awareness stage names -> keep track of spend and returns per awareness stages -> double down on the biggest one -> fill gaps in others with high returns but lower volume -> understand where you're putting your money and ad resources and why -> scale

Amazing how many people are flying completely blind when it comes to this
