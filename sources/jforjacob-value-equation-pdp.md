If you aren't sure of what to put on your PDP, something that I stick to every time is hammering on all 4 points of the value equation (especially above the fold and PDP images)

e.g

Dream Outcome - what benefit are they going to see from your product and how will this change their life?

Perceived likelihood of achievement - Can use stats from studies, testimonials, guarantees here

Time delay - how long to achieve the dream outcome? 1 year vs 1 week is a huge difference in value. Tell them when they should expect results. simple timeline of benefits can work here

Effort and sacrifice - if achieving their dream outcome with your product is going to be a chore, require a lot of discipline or its complicated etc this reduces the value massively. This is exactly why GLP-1's are so valuable. People achieving their weight loss dreams with no effort or sacrifice. If you are in the supplement space using less pills per dose, telling the customer it tastes great etc all reduce the effort and sacrifice on their part

Do this and you can't fail
