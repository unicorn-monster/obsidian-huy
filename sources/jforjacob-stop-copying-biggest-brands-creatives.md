Brands and agencies should stop looking at the biggest brands for creative inspiration

I've seen inside some of these ad accounts and they always have the worst front end metrics (usually below break-even with poor soft metrics too)

They can afford this because of other distribution channels (Amazon, retail etc), large organic presence or high LTV's

Most peoples swipe files are wrongly full of these brands creatives. You are not playing the same game as them

Imo you should be looking at those running large amounts of ads who aren't well known, don't have a large organic presence and other distribution channels. These are the people crushing the front end and profitably

All of the most impressive ad accounts / front end acquisition I've seen have been from dropshippers / hybrid brands (ran by ex-dropshippers) fully reliant on front end profitability. It's do or die in that world & hyper competitive. A break-even CPA just doesn't cut it. You should pay attention to those succeeding

And before you say, the accounts I'm referring to here have been at eye wateringly high daily spends, they aren't sacrificing volume for efficiency

You'd think it would be obvious to study the front end of those fully reliant on their front end for survival, but so few actually apply this common sense
