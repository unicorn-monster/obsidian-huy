title: Welcome flow audit — segment by 0/1/2+ orders
---

I audited a welcome flow and found repeat customers receiving 10% off their "first" order.

Some had already purchased three times.

The flow was triggered by subscription. It did not check whether the subscriber had already purchased.

That sent three very different groups into the same sequence:

- Prospects with no orders
- First-time customers
- Repeat customers

Before rewriting a single email, check who is receiving it.

Here's a simple way to structure the experience:

0 orders: Introduce the brand, feature best sellers, and make the welcome offer.

1 order: Help them use what they bought, recommend the next product, and send replenishment reminders when relevant.

2+ orders: Recognize loyalty, use purchase history, and offer relevant perks or VIP access.

Audit your own welcome flow in five minutes:

1. Open the trigger and flow filters.
2. Preview profiles with 0, 1, and 2+ orders.
3. Check which messages and offers each profile receives.
4. Add a "0 lifetime orders" filter or a conditional split before any first-order offer.

Better copy cannot fix the wrong person receiving the wrong message.

Your welcome flow should know when someone is no longer new.
