Contrary to what most brands believe…

You SHOULD NOT be cooling off on sending emails / sms to people who recently bought from you…

You SHOULD be hitting people hard right after their first order.

We've seen repeat purchases heavily concentrated in that first 1-2 weeks post purchase across our brands, then it drops off fast.

But most brands think they'll annoy people so they back off completely.

Makes no sense.

Customers are the most excited about your brand right now than they may ever be again.

You're the cool new brand they just bought from. They're in the honeymoon phase.

So fuck it… add fuel to the fire.

A nice structure you can use as reference:
- Day 0: add-to-your-order offer
- Day 1: your best complementary product
- Days 2-3: education and social proof
- Days 4-5: a repeat-purchase offer
- Days 6-7: final urgency

And with these make sure you're offering things like $ OFF offers, free gifts, or a limited-time deal to get them to add something before the excitement dies down.

Test this against your standard post-purchase and watch what happens to your repeat rate…
