These are the 5 things I’m focusing on to 4x the business this year.

2025 was our first 8-figure year.

The goal for 2026 is to get closer to 9 figs.

Here's exactly what we're doubling down on.

1. Creative (both quality and volume)

Great creative is the highest leverage to scale efficiently.

Goal is to increase both quality and output this year. Output only goes up if quality is high enough - no point in making more mediocre ads.

2. Retention

Our retention is solid, but I know it can be way better.

We haven't put much brainpower and focus into it yet. We're hiring someone to own this completely.

Better retention means we can increase our target CAC > makes scaling easier and have more cash to reinvest into growth.

3. Launching new markets

The model works. We've proven it in 5 markets.

This year we're replicating it in 5 new markets - fully localized ads, funnels, and customer experience.

4. Expanding B2B

DTC is great, but B2B adds profit margin. We're already in a bunch of local retailers. 

Goal is to expand this into all countries we're active in.

5. Product improvement

We're not launching completely new products this year. 

We did a lot of product launches in 2025. 

This year we're focusing on improving our hero products as we believe this will improve our retention. 
That's the plan for 2026.
 Happy to answer questions on how we execute on any of these.
