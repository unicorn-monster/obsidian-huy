if someone told you your google ads can't scale even more, fire them. took this brand from ca$2.6m to ca$7.1m in 1 year while growing roas from 5 to 7.74x. quick breakdown on what we did: for a brand already doing 7 figures a year, there’s a big challenge: how do you keep pushing spend higher profitably? and it seems like the most viable option is to launch new campaigns. because if the account is already performing, there can't be much left to optimise, right? not really. even the best-performing accounts usually have a heap of opportunities hiding under the surface. before we start adding new campaigns, we fix what's already there first. then we add new prospecting campaigns and strategies to push them further. for this brand, we rebuilt in 4 phases. 1. strengthen the foundation first thing we looked at was conversion tracking a surprising number of brands are feeding the wrong conversion signals. they're counting page views, engagement events or other soft actions alongside purchases. that’s not what you want smart bidding to optimize toward before scaling anything, we cleaned up tracking so the account was optimising toward real purchases. then we turned to branded campaigns. they get a lot of hate, but they play a crucial part in scaling google. when you've got them dialed in: - your brand terms stay protected - you pay less for branded clicks - you make more profits from branded traffic to reinvest into prospecting - branded searches stop creeping into prospecting campaigns and making them harder to scale we cleaned up branded campaigns with: - keyword segmentation - proper exclusions - manual bidding strategy - a dedicated campaign in the market where branded coverage didn't exist low-hanging fruit, but by the end we saw: - cpcs fell by 78% - conversions increased by 144% - - - 2. make the product feed work harder the product feed is your biggest lever in shopping & pmax. we rewrote titles, improved attributes and optimized the feed around the way customers searched for these products. as a result: the campaigns started appearing for more relevant searches while spending similar budgets. - - - 3. let budgets follow performance there’s a common issue among high-spending accounts multiple product categories are shoved into the same campaign. the problem is, you can only control budget at the campaign level which means google decides where the money goes. different products might not receive the budget they deserve we wanted more control than that. especially when different product margins can have a massive impact on how aggressive your roas targets can be so we added custom labels to see which categories were doing well. then we built dedicated campaigns around the highest-performing product categories. that way we had full control over how we wanted to scale them - - - 4. expand the funnel next, we started looking for new ways to acquire customers from the top of the funnel. we noticed people who purchased sample products were more likely to place a larger order later. so we launched demand gen campaigns focused on samples across youtube. it played a big role in our growth. it shortened the buying journey and built a pipeline of new customers. on paper, last-click roas undersold what these campaigns actually did. there was downstream revenue this drove that never showed up as attributed conversions. but that wasn't the point. their job was to bring qualified people into the funnel through a low-friction offer. from there, the brand could maximize customer value over time. at the same time, we continued scaling the campaigns that were already working. after 12 months working together: ad spend: CA$521k to CA$922k revenue: CA$2.6m to CA$7.1m Roas: 5.05x to 7.74x. a lot of brands think scaling starts by launching something new. it can help but… the fastest path to growth is usually fixing the parts of the account that are consuming budget. especially in bigger accounts where you've already got loads of data and campaigns to work with. because there’s a high chance they’re not set up properly and you're nowhere near getting everything out of them. once you've sorted that out, adding new campaigns will scale your account a lot faster.
