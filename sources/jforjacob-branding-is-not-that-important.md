I don't care what anyone says

Branding is not that important, especially when starting out

The reality is people do not think about you or your brand nearly as much as you think

I've seen people spend silly money and time perfecting their "branding" before launching only to get out-scaled by someone who made all their images with AI and Shrine theme

If you have a product that can solve a problem for someone and you can convey that in a clear and convincing way that's what's important, not that you have pretty packaging

People will say you need "branding" in order for people to keep coming back. Again, I know this isn't true

I started a "brand" in June which I spent literally $0 on "branding". GPT images, website that was built in 2 days which also cost me $0 and yet we're about to hit $1m in MRR, with what I'm told are very healthy retention rates

So, what did I spend my time on? Customer research. Competitor research. Product quality. Copywriting. Post purchase education and habit forming resources. Ads, ads, ads, ads

If you know you're customers, you know their issues intimately, you have a quality product that can help solve their issue, and you know how to convey this effectively with your ads and funnels, you will have no problem with acquisition or retention

Nobody gives a shit about how pretty your products look. Sure it's not a bad thing to be aesthetic but it won't help you scale your business (unless in a category like jewellery or fashion ofc)
