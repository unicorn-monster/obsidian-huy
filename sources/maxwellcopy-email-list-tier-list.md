Tier list for scaling the fuck out of your email list:

S TIER
- Testing your offer (biggest lever by a mile)
- Full page pop-up instead of a small modal
- Engage with customer first step before asking for email (quiz, scratch off, etc)
- Using @AliaPopups for your pop-ups

A TIER
- 2-6 second delay, nothing longer
- One input per step
- Making the offer the biggest thing on the screen
- Close button placement testing

B TIER
- SMS as its own step after email
- Add second pop-up with longer delay so you get a second shot at people who closed the first one
- Running actual A/B tests instead of set and forget

C TIER
- Cutting the "join our community" fluff copy

Alia handles this all for you, you should use them.
