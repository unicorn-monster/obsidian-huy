AI Agents Can Now Buy From Your Shopify Store:

5 Things to Set Up Before BFCM

Shopify's Fall '26 Edition is out. Shop Pay checkout now works inside AI agents like Muse from Meta, Claude Duet, and Instinct, and those orders land in your admin like any other order.

If you only do one thing today, open Sales channels > Agentic > Knowledge base. Shopify already drafted your store facts from your site, but agents can't see the ones it scanned until you publish them.
