title: "High CPA doesn’t equal bad ad"
---

If looking at an ads CPA is the only thing that matters ask yourself this question

If I had a proven winning ad, someone rips it and sends it to a different landing page / offer and they see a $100 CPA whereas mine is $20. Does this mean the ad is suddenly a bad ad? Same if I take a winning ad and put a bad / irrelevant hook on it?

This is where only looking at CPA is problematic, it doesn't tell the full story

You can think because your ads are getting a high CPA that means they're bad ads, not true. I could do the above and all my winning ads would suddenly become 'losers' overnight by that way of thinking

This is why looking at soft metrics and site metrics is very useful and informative. It tells you what you need to do

In the example with the hook, I could see that hold rate, CTR etc all look really strong but hook rate is just bad. I could then try to make a better / more relevant hook and all of a sudden it becomes a 'winner'

Something you'd never even test if you only look at CPA

High CPA doesn't = bad ad. It could just need a few tweaks somewhere
