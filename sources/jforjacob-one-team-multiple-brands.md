The highest leverage thing you can do in ecom is build a reliable team, and yes this can include trusted agency partners

If you crack the formula with one brand it's so easy to leverage that existing team across multiple

I'm currently running 3 brands and the goal for 2025 is to increase this to 7

I'll be able to use the existing infrastructure I have in place for this with the exception of hiring some extra VA's for customer service when needed

It's basically like treating your team as an agency for your brands. Agencies have no problem handling multiple clients (in fact they depend on it for the most part) and this is how it should be for your team

I think it's a myth that it's better to laser focus on one. At a certain point there comes diminishing returns

Instead of banging your head against a wall trying to come up with the 47th angle to unlock new audiences for your current brand there comes a point when it just makes sense just to start a new one

Much easier to get an extra £200-£300k per month starting something new than trying to add that to an existing brand that you've already pushed hard with
