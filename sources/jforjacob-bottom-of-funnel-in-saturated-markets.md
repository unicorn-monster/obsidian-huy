I see so many people in crowded markets making the most generic problem / solution ads

If you believe your market is "saturated" all your efforts should be towards differentiation, unique mechanisms, why you are the better choice NOT on trying to educate people on a problem or solution they are already well aware of

I actually think the easiest thing you can do if you're just starting out and struggling is just go full on bottom of funnel. From your ads, to your product images and landing page copy.

Suck up the sales from the bigger players that have already done all the expensive education marketing for you. Literally just be a little feeder fish

Once you get some level of scale you can worry about getting more sophisticated and moving up awareness levels in your marketing
