the highest converting placement in google ads right now is not search

it's the native ad that looks like an article, sits in a gmail inbox or a discover feed, and lands on a page that doesn't look like it belongs to your brand

the buyer never processes any of it as advertising, and that's the entire mechanism

standard display is dead for a documented reason. banner blindness. the eye physically skips rectangular ad units because the brain filed that shape under "not content" around 2005

native placements sidestep it by looking like whatever surrounds them

in discover, it reads as a recommended article. in gmail, a promotional email. on MSN, a news story

so engagement happens BEFORE the scepticism reflex activates. CTRs run 2-5x standard display and CPCs often come in lower because google reads the engagement as quality

what to run on each placement

discover: editorial lifestyle imagery, curiosity headline written like an article title. "the surprising reason most sleep supplements don't work" beats "shop our sleep formula" by an order of magnitude

gmail: the subject line IS the ad. write it like a newsletter, not a promo blast. "your cortisol is probably spiking at 3am" over "SALE: 30% off"

MSN: news-style headlines with a specific data point. "australian study finds 73% of adults over 40 are deficient in this mineral"

now the part that decides whether any of it converts

where the click lands

send native traffic to a product page and you've wasted the entire setup. they clicked because they were curious about an ARTICLE. they need to arrive at one

host the advertorial on a separate domain. not yourbrand.com. editorial layout, no store navigation, no logo in the header, no product image above the fold

structure is hook, agitation, mechanism, solution, proof, soft CTA

spend 40% of the word count on the mechanism. that's where the reader stops trusting the writer and starts trusting the LOGIC. the product then inherits that trust automatically

the moment they see your brand URL, the disguise breaks and the whole thing collapses into an ad

then import the campaigns to bing and run the identical funnel at 20-40% lower CPCs
