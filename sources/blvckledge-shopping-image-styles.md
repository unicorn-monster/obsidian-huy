don’t know why more ecom brands aren’t testing this

you can get pretty crazy with what you put in your shopping ads images

lifestyle & product shots is NOT the only option there

google has become much more flexible with shopping images.

now you've got far more freedom on how to present your product.

look at the shopping results on the right.

Musely is running plenty of different image styles.

some creatives use a male model. others use a female model.

some images show the whole face. others zoom in where the product gets used.

half of them don't even show the product.

there are 2 major benefits to doing this.

first, your ads stand out.

scroll through the shopping results and it's the same boring product shot over and over.

when you show something different, your listing instantly grabs attention

more eyeballs means more clicks.

second, it opens up way more angles.

when the image is built around a person, a transformation or an outcome, you've got far more ways to sell the product.

in this example, musely has separate creatives for men and women.

each one can send people to a landing page written for that audience.

their before and after images can also send people to a page focused on the result or transformation.

here are a few image ideas you can test in your account:

\- product + props

\- product + packaging

\- product + key ingredients

\- before-and-after (for some verticals)

\- close-up application shot

\- different avatars interacting with the products

obviously you can't upload whatever image you feel like.

google still has rules around image formats, things like promo text across the image.

but you've got way more room to test than most people realize.

and from what i've seen

the brands willing to test those limits are often rewarded with much better results.
