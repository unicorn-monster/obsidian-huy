Sorry but I have to jump in here

The flaming Brian is getting in the comments here is wild

Look, @herrmanndigital is a legend in the space, no doubt about that

But I think people are reading this post completely wrong

I believe David mostly is involved in media buying (correct me if I'm wrong)

Brian is rightfully criticising the style of ads a lot of incumbent brands are running these days, that quite frankly just don't work well anymore. Not criticising how David runs his clients accounts

And yes, David gets to see a lot of spend so has a very good idea of the general landscape on how meta is performing right now

But does that mean that other people can't have cracked Meta right now? That not everything they are doing is working? No

And in fact, it's probably more useful to listen to the people that are swimming upstream, going against the current performance trends on meta and trying to understand what they are doing that's letting them scale while others are stagnant or declining, rather than just accepting that most brands are down right now

Pretty much all the alpha I've got in Ecom has been from the few, not the many

I'd far rather be asking questions to someone who is at say $1m a month growing 200% MOM than someone doing $20m a month and declining

Blindly dismissing someone because they challenge someone with a reputation or manages more spend than them is dangerous, that's how ideas become stagnant and progress halts

Gotta question everything, no matter who it comes from, keep adapting, keep finding the new alpha, challenge everything, test it yourself

Like, go look through Brian's posts. You will find an incredible amount of valuable content that I guarantee if you implement you will grow

Listen to everyone. Take the meat and leave the bones. That's what's always worked for me
