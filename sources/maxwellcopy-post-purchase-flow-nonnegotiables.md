Post Purchase Flow Non-Negotiables

1) Send an immediate plain text founder thank you with no delay after the order.

Makes the customer feel good about buying and reduces buyer's remorse. We see on average 1.8-2.7% placed order rates on these alone versus 0.3% on a branded post purchase email.

2) Split the thank you and post purchase experience from email based on how many times they've purchased.

A first time buyer should get a different message than someone on their fourth order.

3) Look at your time between orders data before you build anything.

4) Don't exclude recent purchasers from all your campaigns.

This is the exact window where repeat orders happen. If your post purchase flow is thin then your campaigns need to cover it.

5) Onboard before you sell.

Days 2-4 should be how to use the product and what to expect.

6) Set long term expectations early.

If someone expects results in 7 days they cancel at week 2. Use testimonials from 6 month customers instead of the short term ones.

7) Save the discount for after the repeat window falls off.

No point giving an offer on day 3 to people who were going to buy anyway.
