took a gadget brand from CA$34,196 to CA$106,910 in conversion value in four weeks

spend went from CA$3,748 to CA$12,816

ROAS "dropped" from 9.12 to 6.92, and the drop is the most misunderstood number in the whole case study

what we walked into:

the previous agency had built a branded-only account. branded search, basic shopping, nothing above them. no MOF, no TOF, zero demand generation

on paper the account looked healthy, because 9.12 ROAS reads great in a monthly report

but 9.12 on CA$3,748 is CA$34k in revenue, and the account had already absorbed every bit of branded demand available

they were capturing people already on their way to buy and calling the capture performance

the ceiling was structural. more budget into a demand-capture-only setup is water poured into a full glass

what we implemented:

a full build across all three funnel stages, our bread and butter. BOF branded search protecting the name, MOF non-branded category and problem search pointed at proper landing pages, TOF demand gen sitting above everything

then we leveraged the conversion history already sitting in the account instead of starting the algorithm from zero. the fastest asset in the build, and the previous setup had never used the history as signal

then demand gen with youtube shorts and image ads for display

impressions went to 194,715, up from 12,881

those impressions carry the whole story. they put the brand in front of people with no prior exposure, who searched the brand or the category days later and got closed by the search and shopping campaigns underneath

back to the ROAS

6.92 on CA$12,816 is CA$106,910. the incremental CA$72k came from audiences the old setup never touched

blended number fell while total profit roughly tripled

scaling reads like this. optimising a ceiling reads like the old account

if your account is branded-only and the ROAS looks amazing, you're capped rather than efficient
