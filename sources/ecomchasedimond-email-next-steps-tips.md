Once your email basics are set up, here's what I'd do next:

A few extra tips...

- Define "engaged" as opened or clicked in the last 90 days, and start there.

- Keep SMS short and send it after the first email, never before.

- Base cross-sells on what people actually buy together in your store.

- When you test, change one thing at a time so you know what worked.
