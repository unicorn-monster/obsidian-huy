Reflecting on yesterday not just for my own brands but others that I've talked to

The person I know who did the most revenue out of anyone I've heard is a friend that also owns and operates multiple brands and owns 100% of them

More revenue and profit than most of these brands on Twitter people cream over. And again, owns 100% of the pie. Genuinely few people can say this. Even if he had of done half the rev he did, still banking more in his own pocket than your favourite 9 figure brands CEO

I think again this strengthens my objection to just focusing on one thing. People say you will divert your attention and end up dropping the ball

Yet this guy on each single brand continues to absolutely crush it and put up eye watering numbers

If you build the right team you can basically leverage that team as an agency for your brands (no agency just runs with one brand, why should you?)

You can leverage learnings from one brand to the other. You unlock one win for one brand, roll it out across several and compound it

You diversify your risk. One brand gets sued, loses a processor or just shits the bed. No panic you've got multiple other brands running

It's accepted that investing in S&P is better than picking a single stock, why do people take the opposite approach to their own business assets?

Also you can fill gaps with one brand that the other leaves open. Like if one brand is considered a value brand, why not go capture another portion of the market with a more premium version? You've got the supply chain, operations, team, market insights already in place. Just spin it up

I had been conflicted on this topic for the past 6 months or so, but solidly back in the multiple brand camp

It just makes so much sense
