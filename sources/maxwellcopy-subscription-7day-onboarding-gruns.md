To win as a subscription brand in 2027, you need to fix what happens in the first 7 days after someone subscribes.

I subscribed to Grüns and documented their entire onboarding sequence to see what a $100M brand actually does differently.

Here are the 3 moves that make them one of the most successful subscription brands on the market.

1. They send every single day

Grüns is in your inbox every day for the first week. They do NOT have a fear of unsubscribes. Instead they're capturing attention while the lead is at its warmest. Compare that to most brands that send one email and then nothing until the rebill reminder. Day 1 through day 7 is the lowest risk real estate you have and they use all of it.

2. They show long-term benefits

Every email reinforces what the product does over the next 30, 60 and 90 days. Expectations get set early so nobody bails in month 2 when results feel slow. Instead of promising instant results they build buy-in for the long game. This is what kills the "I don't see results" cancellations before they happen.

3. They remind you to take it every day

Habit stacking is the whole game and they run it from day one. Every email reminds you to take your Grüns. They tie it to a specific moment like morning coffee or before the gym. Once the product becomes a habit, retention takes care of itself.

The first week is the only time someone is actually excited about what they bought. Every day you don't use it, the less of that excitement is left.
