---

Unaware
Mindset - "Everything feels normal. I don't think I have a problem."
Belief shift needed - "There's a hidden problem affecting my life and it's worth paying attention to."

Problem-Aware
Mindset - "I know something's wrong, but I don't know how to fix it."
Belief shift needed - "There's a specific type of solution designed for this exact problem."

Solution-Aware
Mindset - "I've heard of solutions like this, but I don't know what actually works."
Belief shift needed - "Most options aren't effective, but this one works because of a unique advantage or mechanism."

Product-Aware
Mindset - "I'm considering this product/service, but I'm not fully convinced."
Belief shift needed - "This is the best choice because it's proven, trustworthy, and delivers real value."

Most Aware
Mindset - "I know the brand. Should I buy now?"
Belief shift needed - "Now is the right time to act because I'll get more value, or I might miss out."
