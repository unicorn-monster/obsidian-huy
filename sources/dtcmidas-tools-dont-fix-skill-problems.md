title: "Tools do not solve skill problems"

Stop looking for tools to solve skill problems.

Everyone's looking for the tool that will finally fix their problem.

But if you don't have the underlying skill, buying software won't solve your problem.
 An example, I've heard this many times:

"I can't find good creators that want to work with me" or "My creators don't perform."

Then they get this advice: "Just use Billo or Insense or whatever UGC sourcing platform."

This is BAD advice.

Going to Billo won't fix your performance.

The real problem is you don't know how to make creators excited about your product, write clear briefs, and negotiate good rates.

What you actually need is to learn how to source and sell creators on your brand and put in the effort to onboard them properly and make them excited about your product.

Most of you won't do it because it's boring, takes a lot of time, and you don't see success immediately.

Another example:

 "My landing pages aren't converting, which tool should I use?"

Advice: "Just use Replo, they have good templates."

Getting a Replo subscription won't fix your landing page.

You don't understand customer psychology, which objections to handle, or how to structure a value proposition that drives conversions.

You could technically sell any product with just a google doc if your copy is good.

What you actually need is to spend time educating yourself and getting better at copywriting.

Look at Comfrt

They scaled to $500M with thousands of creators.

NOT because they used some new tool.

But because they built relationships with creators for years, aligned incentives when structuring deals, trained creators in-house, and made the product and mission something creators wanted to talk about.

They put a LOT of effort and time into it.

That's why it worked.

You keep buying tools hoping they'll fix your problems.

They won't.

If you don't build the skill, the tool is useless.
