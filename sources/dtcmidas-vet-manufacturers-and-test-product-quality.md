Here’s how to find manufacturers and test if products actually work.

Start with Alibaba, but don’t just message random suppliers.

Look for Gold Suppliers with high ratings and trade assurance.

Check their product portfolio, you want manufacturers who already make similar products to yours.

Message 10-15 suppliers, ask for samples, and compare quality.

Don’t just go with the cheapest.

Look for responsive communication, good English, and willingness to do small MOQs for testing.

Or go the easy route and get a good sourcing agent > this will make the process easier and decrease the chance you get fucked.

Testing if a product works well?

Read this post how to validate demand: https://t.co/lW9T9ypCR6…

But for actual product quality testing, order samples from multiple manufacturers.

Use the product yourself for weeks.

Give samples to people in your environment that are in your target demographic.

Ask them specific questions:

- Does it solve the problem it claims to?

- How does it compare to alternatives they’ve used?

- Would they actually buy this at the price point?

If people are genuinely excited about the sample, you’ve got something.

If they’re just being polite and saying “it’s nice,” it’s probably mediocre.
