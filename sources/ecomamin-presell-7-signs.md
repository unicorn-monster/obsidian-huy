title: 7 signs your store needs presell page not more spend
---

signs your store needs a better PAGE, not more ad spend:

1. people click your ads but almost nobody buys
the ad did its job. the page didn't

2. lots of add to carts, very few checkouts
they wanted it. something on the way made them doubt it

3. customers keep emailing the same questions before they order
every question is something your page didn't answer

4. your product needs explaining
if you have to tell people why it's different, a photo and a price won't do it

5. next to a competitor, you look more expensive
if all they see is the price, they'll pick on price

6. your buyer is older, or buying it as a gift
these people read before they pay. give them something to read

7. you sell a fix for a problem
someone searching a problem wants proof it works, not a buy button

if 2 or more of these sound like your store, more budget just buys more of the same result

the fix is a page that sells BEFORE the product page:

- a listicle: the reasons to buy, one by one
- an advertorial: the story of the problem and how your product solves it
- a pre-sell page: answers every doubt before they see the checkout

send your google traffic there first

then the product page only has to close
