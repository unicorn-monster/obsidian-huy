title: Doomscrolling to find new winning ad formats
---

Doomscrolling should be part of your job.

Once you know which angles work for your product, there's a huge upside in being first to bring a new format into your market.

There are only so many angles for your product, but new formats are emerging every day.

I've brought new formats into our market many times and saw huge growth during those periods.

The easiest way to do this is to either intentionally doomscroll content your audience watches organically, or take a format from another niche or market and bring it into yours before everyone else starts doing it.

Here's how to approach it:

1. Look outside your own market

If you only look at competitors, you're mostly finding things your audience has already seen.

Look at what brands in other categories are doing too.

A format that's been used a lot somewhere else can still be new to your market.

2. Consume content your customers actually watch

On YouTube, search for the problems, interests and routines around your product and find creators making content for your audience.

On TikTok, search the phrases your customers use when talking about their problem.

Open relevant vids, read the comments and click through to the creators and scroll through the vids.

Same for IG.

3. Build a swipe file

When building one you also need to be able to answer which of your winning angles could work with this format (there needs to be angle <> format fit).

4. Be the first one to test

Don't wait until all your competitors are running it before trying it yourself.

Being first doesn't mean the ad will work. You still need to execute it well.
