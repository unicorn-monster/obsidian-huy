Running email for a brand in 2020:

- Blast 20% off to the whole list
- Judge everything by open rate
- Write for the inbox

Running email for a brand in 2026:

- Send to the people who actually click
- Judge everything by revenue per recipient
- Write for the inbox, and for the AI that reads it first
