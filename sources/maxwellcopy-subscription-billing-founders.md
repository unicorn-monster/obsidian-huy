Lessons I've learned from extremely cracked ecom founders:

- They understand monthly billing makes a customer re-make the decision of staying subscribed 12 times per year... compared to quarterly billing being only 4 times per year

- They know what a one month subscriber is worth vs a quarterly subscriber

- They ALWAYS price the longer term as a discount and push it to get more cash upfront

- They ask for commitment at the moment of highest intent (the purchase)

- Treat the renewal emails as risks

- Don't focus on win-back discounts to fix what is actually an offer problem

I have personally seen the one-month subscription -> three-month subscription upsell on 9-fig brands absolutely rip.

More cash upfront for you as the brand... stronger retention... higher LTV

Yes, this works, and it crushes.
