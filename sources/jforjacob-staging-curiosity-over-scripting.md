I think many people focus too much on copywriting

Of course it's hugely important but the reality is the platforms we advertise on today are hugely visual. I don't see enough talk about staging, direction, scene, environment etc

You ever see those videos where it's just somebody randomly assembling, crafting or cooking something, no talking, no captions & no context? I always find myself getting sucked into these kind of videos because I automatically think the end result is gonna be something really cool or bizarre. As the video goes on I'm trying to figure out wtf it is, mf's could literally have me for 5+ mins if they wanted

I think videos like this are far better than starting off with someone saying something like "watch how I make a DIY pet feeder" for example. I don't really care and I'll just skip. But because of the lack of context and natural curiosity it creates I'll end up watching the whole thing to get the payoff at the end

Then there is stuff you can do like planting some mistake, controversy or something the viewer believes they weren't meant to see in the video, I've talked a few times about how we've saw this drive absolutely insane engagement. Viewers feel the need to jump into your comments and correct you, argue with each other or mention the thing 'they weren't meant to see' etc

Figuring out ways you can apply this to your advertising is such a hack. Of course your ad should still be relevant and copywriting is the bread and butter, but by doing the things mentioned above you can create ads with massive hook, retention and engagement rates. Leading to lower CPM's, more eyeballs on your business and more sales for less money.

Meta likes content that engages and retains users. If your content does this they will reward you. I think more people should be thinking about this kind of stuff rather than just putting all your effort into scripting
