title: launch rough first version get real feedback
---

The first version of whatever you're launching is allowed to be rough. It just has to get in front of real people.

Once a few of them have seen it, you know things you couldn't have known at your desk. What they clicked, what they asked about, what they ignored. Version 2 gets built on that, and it's better than anything you could have made on your own.

So before the big launch, send it to a couple dozen of your customers and ask them what's unclear. Fix what comes back, then send it to everyone.

The polishing still happens. It just happens after customers have seen it.
