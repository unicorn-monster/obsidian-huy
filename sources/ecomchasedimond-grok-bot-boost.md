I built a bot in Grok that finds my best X post every day and puts ad spend behind it.

The idea is simple: the post people are already engaging with, sharing, and bookmarking gets in front of a lot more people.

My first boost is live right now. The bot picked the winning post, then set up the whole campaign itself: the targeting, the budget, everything. All I did was hit approve.

I'll share the results next week.
