You can make a ‘zombie’ campaign with low budget > put a cost cap on it > and let it rip. 

Will probably spend efficiently on a few ads that didn’t make it to your scaling campaign.

PSA: I don’t do this as I’m forcing spend to all my creatives, but I’ve multiple friends doing this and it works for them.
