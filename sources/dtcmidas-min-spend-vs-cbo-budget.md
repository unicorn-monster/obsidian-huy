title: "Keep total min spends at max 50% of the CBO budget"

I always try to have my min spends beeing max 50% of my cbo budget.

So for example:

If cbo budget is 300 and I have 3 adsets with 50 min spend and I want to add a new adset, I’d up the cbo budget to 400 and add the new adset with 50 min spend
