Easy abandoned checkout flow hack:

Split your checkout abandonment into 4 paths instead of sending everyone the same email.

Only takes two splits.

> Has this person bought before...
> Is their cart above or below your free shipping threshold...

That gives you four versions:

1) New buyer below the threshold gets the welcome offer plus how much more they need for free shipping

2) New buyer above it gets the welcome offer plus free shipping already unlocked

3) Returning buyer below gets the exact amount they're short

4) Returning buyer above gets free shipping unlocked and a nudge to go finish

Shipping cost at checkout is one of the biggest reasons people drop off, so telling them exactly where they stand does most of the work for you.

And anyone sitting below the threshold usually goes and adds another item, so your AOV goes up on top of the recovery.
