title: "Get around people better than you, and when the chance comes to help them, refuse payment"

Anytime I think I know a lot I end up talking with people doing things I wouldn't have even considered in the realms of possibility

I know I've said this before and it is very fluffy but the best thing you can do is just get around people that are better than you

If you ever have the opportunity to work with / do a favour for / build a relationship with people that are ahead of you do whatever it takes

So many people unknowingly miss out on these opportunities because they try to make everything transactional

Can't tell you how many times someone has offered me something in return for something else, I decline it and say happy to help, no need to pay me etc and then that ends up turning into something massively valuable

Stop trying to extract things out of every interaction. Give, deposit good will, don't ask for anything in return. You will reap what you sow
