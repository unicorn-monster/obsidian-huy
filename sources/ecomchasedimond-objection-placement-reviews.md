title: Place top objection next to where doubt hits on page
---

Look at your last 20 replies, reviews, or chat questions. Whichever objection shows up most is the only row you need.

Put that line where the doubt hits: next to the price, by the size picker, or right above the button. One real objection beat six half-answers every time.

And only use a line if it's true for you. Avoid fake guarantees, fake policies, fake stories.
