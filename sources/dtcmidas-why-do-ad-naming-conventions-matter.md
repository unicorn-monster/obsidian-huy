Start using naming conventions for your ads.

I know - it seems useless and a waste of time.

I thought the same thing.

But now that I'm launching hundreds ads a month, I regret not setting it up earlier.

Without proper naming conventions you can't:

Analyze patterns properly.

When all your ads are named "Video 1" or "Static 2", it's nearly impossible to see which angles, hooks, or formats are actually working at scale.

Track who's creating winners.

You need to know which creative strategists, editors, and designers are making your best ads so you can give them more work and pay them better.

Find specific ads quickly.

"Which ads did Emily make in the last 2 weeks?" becomes impossible to answer when you have 800+ ads running.

Collaborate as a team.

When everyone uses different naming systems or none at all, communication breaks down and people waste time asking basic questions.

Set up a naming convention system, stick to it religiously and your future self will thank you.
