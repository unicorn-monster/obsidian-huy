Underused Klaviyo feature...

You can drop a personalized offer block into any regular campaign and have it only show for specific people.

The block sits at the top of the email and only subscribers with a certain profile property see it, while everyone else gets the campaign like normal.

That means every campaign you send from that point is doing two jobs at once...

The main email promotes whatever you want to the whole list, and the block serves the right offer to the people most likely to take it.

A few ways we've used it:

> Welcome offer for anyone on the list who's never purchased
> Stronger winback offer for customers who haven't bought in 90+ days
> Reactivation offer for canceled subscribers with a dynamic link back to their account

Setup takes a bit of work... but once it's live the offer follows them across every send until they take the action instead of you hoping one email lands in the right inbox at the right time.

Worth building if you've got the volume!
