Split up campaigns by product category or by margins. 

Run to collection pages. Make sure to have a banner with the offer on top of the collection page.

Make sure that the products that are shown in the ads are on top of the collection page (there are apps and utm rules for this)

I wouldn’t diversify channels until you’re at atleast 100k month. Each channel costs time and recourses to manage and moves your focus from things that are already working.
