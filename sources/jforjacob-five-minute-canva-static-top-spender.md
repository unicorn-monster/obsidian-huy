That ad is still running today. It took roughly 5 minutes and has no messaging other than displaying an offer

I've had some brilliant ads made by very talented people since then that have performed well and spent a lot, but nothing that has ever topped that shitty little static made by a newbie

To this day, I have no idea why it works so well
