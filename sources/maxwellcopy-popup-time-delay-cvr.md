Yes a shitty, misplaced pop-up will def hurt your 7-day CVR.

But...

A well made, and properly placed pop-up will increase your 7-day site CVR.

If you are struggling:
- Try different formats on @AliaPopups
- Increase time delays on your pop-up trigger (never use scroll amount, just do time delays)

Feel free to NOT fire pop-ups on all of your primary ad traffic pages... but would recommend at least having one on your general home page.
