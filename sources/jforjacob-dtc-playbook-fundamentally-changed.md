title: DTC playbook fundamentally changed past 3 years
---

I think everyone pretty much knows now that the DTC playbook has fundamentally changed in the past couple years.

The founders who built their brands in the golden age of Facebook ads are not worth listening to anymore. Their knowledge and playbooks aren't effective anymore. Reality is that timing was on their side not skill.

With how competitive things are now, and how much higher the standards are for creatives, offers and landing pages I genuinely don't think any of these guys would have a hope in hell of starting and scaling a brand from scratch with no funding or significant starting capital.

Genuinely if someone hasn't bootstrapped a successful brand in the last 2-3 years I really don't care about anything they say.
