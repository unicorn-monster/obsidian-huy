People seem to completely miss the point of testing ads

The goal is not just to publish something to get the lowest CPA possible, sure that's nice but what good is it if you can't draw a conclusion or know why that specific thing worked? That's what will enable you to keep your CPA's low over a longer time horizon. Clear knowledge of what specific messaging, headlines, hooks, imagery, colours, styles etc drive better performance

Seems like very few people actually do this though. I get people asking me all the time how I'm able to do the things I do and how I'm able to scale things so fast, but when I tell them they just shrug their shoulders

Well, there's your answer. Don't be lazy, do the work, don't just publish stuff and be happy it's CPA is low. Unless you can draw meaningful learning from everything you publish you will be in a constant battle with the rising CAC monster
