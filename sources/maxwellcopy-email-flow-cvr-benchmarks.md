title: Email flow conversion rate benchmarks
---

Conversion rate benchmarks your email flows should be hitting:

1. Welcome flow: 10%

These people just gave you their email and they're sitting there with an unused discount.

How to fix: better offer framing, send 4-6 emails daily, use more urgency.

2. Checkout abandon: 6-8%

They had their card out, but folded. Low-hanging fruit to fix this up.

How to fix: send your first email 30 minutes after the action, wait 1 day between emails, remind non-buyers of their welcome discount.

3. Cart abandon: 4-6%

Lower intent than checkout but the same principles apply.

How to fix: same as checkout. But don't be afraid to add an extra offer in here.

4. Browse abandon: 3-4%

Lowest intent of the abandonment flows so the bar is lower. At volume though, going from 2 to 3% conversions can be huge.

How to fix: give custom emails to non-buyers reminding of their welcome offer, layer in a "mystery discount", and send 4 emails daily.

5. Post-purchase: 3%+ placed order rate

This one everyone ignores. Most brands treat this as a thank you note and run it at 0.3%. It should be pushing a second order while they're still excited about the first one.

How to fix: send an immediate upsell, suggest more products when their order delivers, don't be afraid to send more.

Before you go compare these against your account: know that each of these run on a 5 day open and 5 day click window, which is the default in Klaviyo.

So go check your attribution, then pull all five and see which one is furthest off.

Also important to note: every brand is contextual and has a different audience and different numbers to shoot for but this in general is what is standard.
