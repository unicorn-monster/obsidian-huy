How to build abandonment emails that actually convert.

Put the abandoned product block at the very top.
Never bury it halfway down the email.
Show them the exact item they left behind.
Kill the "forgetting something?" headline every brand uses.
Write something like "your order is ready to ship" instead.
Make it feel like they're one step from finishing what they started.
Split buyers from non-buyers with a conditional split.
Remind non-buyers about the welcome discount they never used.
Put real education below the hero section.
Hit the benefits, the objections, the reviews.
Show them what actually makes you different.
