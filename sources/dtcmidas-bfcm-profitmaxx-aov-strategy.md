During BFCM you need to profitmaxx.

That doesn't mean no discounts.

I'm okay giving away more discount, but only if they buy more. Bigger basket = more absolute margin, so bfcm is the one time of year I really aov maxx.

You get the ltv up front, at a discount, but you can reinvest that cash right away (or pull dividends), so it's a good deal for one month of the year.

Now the catch…

Especially for subs / mrr / retention-heavy brands:

The cohort you acquire during this bfcm period is generally your weakest.

They bought at a discount, so they're less likely to ever buy again at full price - for subs that means higher churn.

Day 1 blended can look great, but really check that cohort and you can be losing money on them if you're not first order profitable.

And if you're a health brand, q5 is where the real scaling opportunities are.

So during BFCM:

- hold the line on nc roas / cac (adjusted for your discounted gross margin)
- make decisions on actual contribution margin
- but keep pushing budgets as long as you stay on target
