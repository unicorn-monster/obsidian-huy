google ads scale as hard as meta and i'm tired of pretending they don't

the amount of operators quietly pushing $1M/mo through youtube ads alone should have ended this debate a year ago

let me explain what changed

meta is still the goat for speed. faster feedback loops, tighter audience precision, better raw scale ceiling. nobody sane argues against that

but google spent the last two years building a SECOND meta inside youtube and almost nobody in ecom noticed

youtube shorts is a full social feed now. same swipe behaviour. same creative logic. same hook rules. same UGC aesthetic winning over polished brand video

which means the creative you already validated on meta transfers over with maybe 20 minutes of reformatting work

that alone kills the "we don't have creative for google" objection dead

here's the part that makes this compound though

everything lives inside ONE ecosystem. your youtube viewers, your display visitors, your shopping clickers, your search traffic, all of it feeds the same audience pool

then PMax and demand gen pull from that pool automatically

so a buyer who watched 50% of your youtube short gets picked up by shopping three days later when they finally search the product category

and the meta traffic you paid for? it lands on your site, gets tagged, and becomes a retargeting audience google reuses across every placement it owns

meta creates the demand. google captures it AND recycles it

running them as rivals is the mistake. they were designed to compound

the brands doing $100k+/mo on google right now are running the exact same creative angles they run on meta, pointed at youtube shorts and demand gen, with search and shopping catching everything downstream

if you're on meta only, you built half a machine
