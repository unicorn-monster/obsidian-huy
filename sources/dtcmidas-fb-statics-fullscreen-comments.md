Facebook statics now take up your whole screen if comments are expanded.

So you can almost build a full landing page as an ad.

Including headlines, hero, social proof and a cta.

It also shows how important getting good comments is.

They're one of the best forms of social proof, because people can click the profiles and see real fb users behind them.
