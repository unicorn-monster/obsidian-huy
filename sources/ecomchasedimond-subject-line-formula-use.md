title: Subject line formula use one not three avoid clickbait
---

Easiest way to use this: write your subject line like you normally would, then look at this list. If it doesn't hit any of these, swap in one formula. Don't cram three into one line, or it reads like clickbait.

And make sure the email actually delivers what the subject line teased. If it doesn't, people learn to stop opening.
