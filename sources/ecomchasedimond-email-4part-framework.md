title: 4-part email template hook to CTA
---

Stuck on an email? Fill in these 4 parts, in order:

1. Hook: "[Their problem]? Here's why."
2. Why care: "That means [what it costs them today]."
3. Offer: "[Product] [does what they want]. [Deal] until [date]."
4. One CTA: "[Verb] the [product]" (use the same link 2 or 3 times)

Write it in that order and you have a full email.
