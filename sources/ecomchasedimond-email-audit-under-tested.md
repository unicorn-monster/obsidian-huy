Their flows were built properly, calendar full, nothing obviously broken.

So I asked what they'd tested in the last quarter...

They said 2 subject lines on just one campaign.

Everything else in those emails had been decided exactly once. The hero, the offer, the order of the sections, the copy under the button. Someone picked all of it on a Thursday 18 months ago, and it's been running that way ever since.

And to be honest, that's most email programs I look at.

Most brands are just happy they have flows running at all, but that's not a truly optimized program...

And it's losing you money every single day it runs untouched.

Then it builds each email per subscriber out of that library and keeps score of what's landing across everyone moving through.
