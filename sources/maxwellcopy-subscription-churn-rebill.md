title: Reframe rebill reminder to cut subscription churn
---

Another subscription brand that we dramatically cut churn for in the books.

They were losing 10-12% of their subscribers in the 3 days right before their rebill.

So we rolled out a bunch of different optimizations on their billing reminder by reframing it from "you're about to be charged" to "get excited, here's what's coming" and removed other messages they were sending to customers around the date they were going to get rebilled.

Got it down to around 4%.

Massive increase in subscription charges coming through.
