If you fulfill from China, run worldwide campaigns.

They're still crushing it and of course very scalable.

One hypothesis I have on why they work so well - not 100% sure if it's true, but it's something I've noticed:

When you advertise across different timezones, Meta is able to show your ads during optimal times for each country.

Normally when you advertise in one timezone, you get peaks at the same times every day.

With worldwide campaigns, the algorithm spends optimally across each country's best timezone.

When I wake up, Australia and New Zealand are performing best.

Few hours later, Japan and South Korea pick up.

Around lunch, Saudi Arabia and UAE start converting.

In the evening, most sales come from US, Canada, Mexico, and LATAM.

It's like having your ads running during peak hours 24/7 instead of just during your home market's active hours.

This could be one of the reasons these campaigns scale so well - more timezone coverage = more opportunities to spend at optimal times.

If you're fulfilling from China anyway, there's no downside to going worldwide.

Test it if you haven't already.
