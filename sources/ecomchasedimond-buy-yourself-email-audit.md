If you run an online store, I'm begging you:

Buy from yourself like a stranger would. Grab your phone and an email your brand has never seen.

1. Sign up for your list

Did a welcome email hit your inbox within minutes? If not, set up your sending domain and turn it on.

2. Read the welcome email

Does it tell you who you are, why you're different, and what to buy first? One story, one best seller, one button.

3. Add to cart and leave

Did anyone remind you within a day? Turn on cart reminders that show the exact item.

4. Buy it on your phone

Any zooming, hunting for a code, or typing things twice? Turn on express checkout and cut extra fields.

5. Read your order confirmation

Does it say when it ships and what happens next? Add a clear timeline and one product tip.

6. Reply to it

Did a real person answer? Send from an inbox someone actually reads.

7. Wait for it to ship

Did tracking show up without you asking? Send a shipping email with a real delivery window.

8. Write down everything that confused or annoyed you

Fix the first one this week.

This is the quickest and cheapest audit you'll ever run. Your customers go through all of it every day.
