title: Creative email formats to break inbox pattern
---

Creative emails should make up 15-25% of your calendar.

Email is the one channel where people have already decided whether to engage before they read a word.

They see the shape of the email and know exactly what it is...

Problem is most brands send the same shape every single time.

Which is why you need to break your format on purpose...

The best concepts:

> Airdrop notification where the product sits where the sender name goes
> Notes app screenshot with your product on a checklist
> Fake group chat where friends are talking about the product
> Receipt from a competitor with every overpriced line circled
> Boarding pass with your bestsellers as the itinerary
> Error message or system alert
> Text message thread with the founder

Where to pull them from:

> Subscribe to 20 brands and swipe everything creative
> Break down why each one worked
> Steal concepts from packaging, memes, viral tweets, app interfaces
> Take your next send and ask yourself what's the most unexpected way to deliver that same message

Easiest way to wake up a list that stopped paying attention.
