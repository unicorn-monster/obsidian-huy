title: Localization arbitrage playbook for non-US brands
---

If you're from europe, asia or south-america, localization arbitrage is your unfair advantage.

Find a US ecom brand that's scaling and localize it for the market you're from.

Localizing doesn't mean just translating everything. You have to:

- use the local language and phrasing, not a literal translation
- adapt to the right awareness and sophistication stage of that market
- make use of local proof (review websites, awards, badges, magazines)
- localize your full funnel
- offer the payment methods people there actually use
- offer the delivery options they expect

The goal is that it feels like they're buying from a local company not some big international brand.

These markets are way less competitive than the US and cheaper to advertise in, so you can profit quicker.

I know a guy doing nine figures with this model and he only sells in europe with 1 brand and 3 products.

Build it out locally first, once you have the cash and understand your product better, launch in the US where the market is just bigger but also more competitive.

The Samwer Brothers did a version of this with rocket internet and became billionaires in the early 2000s.

For dtc brands the playbook is still wide open, but the arbitrage window is closing.

As ai gets better and supply chains get more global, US brands can sell worldwide with fully localized ads and funnels.

So be quick!
