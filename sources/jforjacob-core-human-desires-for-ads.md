There only are a handful of core human desires. See too many people listing stuff like: "The desire to look good in front of my ex boyfriend so that he regrets the decision to leave me"

That's not very useful. That's more just a summary of your ad, an afterthought

The point of understanding desires is so you can work out how to map them to your audience. Some smart guy once wrote "desire can't be created, it can only be channelled and directed toward your product" or some shit like that

If you know the things that really drive people at the core, it's going to give you a lot more clarity and direction with your creative strategy. You should know exactly what core desire you are trying to hit with every ad you make

Pick from this list and ask yourself how can I create ads that appeal to one of these:

To survive; Avoid illness, harm, death, danger. Basis of health, safety, and risk-avoidance messaging

To avoid pain; Physical, emotional, or social pain. Includes discomfort, frustration, shame, or fear

To be free; Freedom from constraints, limitations, or control. Applies to health, lifestyle, finances, time, authority

To feel safe; Security, stability, predictability. From uncertainty, misinformation, or harm

To protect what matters; Family, identity, health, values, investments. Often a "guardian" or "caretaker" motivation

To preserve the self/body; Longevity, youth, healthspan, mental clarity. Avoiding deterioration, injury, or burnout

To perform at one's peak; Strength, power, endurance, mental sharpness. Includes optimization, biohacking, achievement

To be in control; Over body, choices, schedule, destiny. Often linked with empowerment and independence

To belong; Social validation, community, identity alignment. Feeling seen, understood, and connected

To live with integrity; Acting in line with personal values. Includes ethical consumption, clean living

To live with meaning; Purpose, contribution, self-actualization. Higher calling, long-term legacy

To experience joy or vitality; Energy, pleasure, happiness. Feeling alive and well, not just functional
