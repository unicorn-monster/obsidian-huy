title: "When a native static wins, split it to find whether the image or the copy carries the weight"
---

Whenever I find a winning native static, I split it up to see what's actually carries more weight in driving performance - the image or the copy.

So I make 10 new ads with the winning image + 10 different copies, and 10 new ads with the winning copy + 10 different images.

Both go in separate ad sets.

Most of the time it's the copy (especially if it's long form).

So then I just go and test a bunch of different native images with that same copy.

When you find a winner in those iterations, you can go through the same process again.
