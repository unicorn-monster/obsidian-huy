Here are my requirements for a brilliant ecom product:

- Solves a real problem

- Consumable or expandable: Either they need to reorder regularly or you can build a product line around it

- Big TAM: Large addressable market, not a tiny niche

- Easy and cheap to ship: Small, lightweight products that don’t kill your margins with shipping costs

- 70%+ margins: Anything less makes scaling ads profitably very hard
