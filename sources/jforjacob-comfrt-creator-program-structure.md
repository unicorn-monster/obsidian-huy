I got so much backlash from the creator community about this post (predictably)

The main thing they were saying "you're broke, you can't afford good creators" and "if you pay the big money you'll get better content"

First, not true. There is zero correlation to the price I've paid creators to the quality I've received

But just watched an interview with the founder of Comfrt, who undoubtedly have had the most successful creator program of all time. Their founder basically mirrored what I had said in this post

When they bring on creators they start them on a very small fee ($1k retainer for 30 videos a month, works out at $33 per video) then bring them on a performance only basis

Literally said that they only pay for creative that works, if it doesn't work they don't pay
