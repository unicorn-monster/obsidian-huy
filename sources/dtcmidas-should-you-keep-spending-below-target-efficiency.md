title: "Stop spending when you’re far below target efficiency, even if LTV would eventually recoup it"
---

You shouldn’t keep spending when you’re far below your target efficiency.

If you have your unit economics dialed in, you should know how much you can afford to spend without running into cash flow issues.

In less performing periods, I scale down. Lower spend usually means efficiency goes up.

Even if I could probably recoup it over time due to LTV, lower efficiency causes other problems.
