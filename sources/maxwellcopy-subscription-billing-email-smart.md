title: Smart vs basic subscription billing email

Here's the difference between a basic billing reminder and one that actually is churn proof:

BASIC SUBSCRIPTION BRAND:

- "Your order is processing"
- Still firing out of your subscription platform on the default template
- Manage subscription button sitting at the top of the email, pretty much asking people to cancel
- Same generic reminder sent to every subscriber regardless of order number
- No mention of what's actually coming in the box
- Reads like a bank notification

SMART SUBSCRIPTION BRAND:

- "Here are the benefits you are getting"
- Rebuilt in Klaviyo so they control the whole thing
- Manage subscription button buried at the very bottom
- Split by order number, because someone on order 2 needs a completely different message than someone on order 6
- Free gift front and center in the hero
- A visual gift roadmap showing what unlocks at order 3, 4, 5
- Reads like a reward

Same email... but a COMPLETELY different customer reaction.

Optimize this one email correctly and you'll see your churn drop dramatically.
