title: BFCM popup and welcome flow 4-point checklist
---

Before you finalize your BFCM ad budget, sign up for your own email list.

Then check what needs to change before your sale goes live.

If your sale banner says 30% off, your popup offers 10%, and your welcome email sends 15%, shoppers shouldn't have to guess which offer applies or whether they combine.

You have time to catch that now. Here are four things I'd check:

1. Will your popup make sense alongside the sale?

Plan your popup updates alongside your ads, site banners, and sale emails.

If signing up will get shoppers an additional discount, explain how it works with the sale. Make the terms clear before they reach checkout.

2. What does signing up get them before BFCM?

If you're offering early access, be specific.

"Get the Black Friday sale link 24 hours before everyone else" tells shoppers more than "Join our VIP list."

Your welcome email should confirm that promise and explain what happens next.

3. Will the entire welcome flow deliver the right offer?

Review subject lines, body copy, and discount codes throughout the sequence.

Updating the first email isn't enough if email three still promotes an old offer.

Also plan for someone who signs up before the sale but receives the next email after it starts.

4. Will the offer work on mobile?

Once your sale setup is ready to test, follow the full path: submit the popup, open the email, tap the link, and add a product to your cart.

Check minimum spend, exclusions, expiration dates, and the final price.
