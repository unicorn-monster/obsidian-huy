title: "Always dangle the carrot of a long-term partnership when negotiating with creators"
---

When negotiating with creators you should always be dangling the carrot, showing them the opportunity that could be in front of them if they work with you. It'll amaze you the deals you can get when this is done correctly

Just had a massive creator in my niche (600k+ followers) make 3 videos for us (which are all great btw), post to her story and come and comment on our videos. Paid her $250 and some free product. But more importantly showed her that there was an opportunity to work with us long term, explained our creator / ambassador program and how she could progress through it

Most creators (even the big ones) are not as well off as you think, and even when they do get money it's usually very inconsistent. The idea of steady money and a brand partnership can be very appealing to them. Use this to your advantage. Learn the art of the rizz, stop paying UGC creators, find people that actually know how to make content with an audience you can also leverage. This is the way
