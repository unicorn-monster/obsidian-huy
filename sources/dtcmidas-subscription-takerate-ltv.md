title: Subscription take rate and LTV CAC optimization
---

To increase your sub take rate, make subscription the default and hide one-time purchase, or make it a tiny text link you have to click.

In most cases this will increase your CAC tho, so figure out if it's worth it for you.

In the end you should be optimizing for LTV:CAC anyway (CM2 LTV, so margin before ad spend).

Then 0 day churn.

This is most of the time an acquisition problem.

Either it's a bad offer: too big of a discount on the sub pulls in people who just wanted the discount and were never going to be subs (most times better to give gifts than big discount).

Or it's the wrong messaging: you're attracting people who expect results after one use.

Make it clear they need to use it for a longer time before they see results, so they get why they need a sub.

In both cases you're attracting the wrong audience.
