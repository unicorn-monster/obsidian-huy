title: "Set your free shipping threshold just above your most common order value, not your AOV"
---

When setting free shipping thresholds, most people look at their AOV and base the threshold on this

I.e AOV is $50 so they set the threshold to $55

This isn't the best way to do things

You should set your threshold based on your most common order value

So in this case AOV may be $50 but the most common order value may actually be $30

Setting your free shipping threshold at $35 here would have a greater impact on your AOV than setting it at $55

It was actually @jordanhill11 that pointed this out to me when having a drink a couple weeks ago. Had never thought of this before but seems so obvious in retrospect

I implemented it on one of my stores and it's having the desired effect
