I used to find that ASC hyper focused budget on 1-2 creatives whereas CBO would distribute it more amongst ad sets and things would get a better chance to spend

Seeing the exact opposite now. Seems like once a CBO starts spending on something it latches on really hard

When I drop those same creatives into an ASC they will pick up a good share of spend vs existing ads

I have dozens of ad sets that will get zero spend in CBO. Drop those same ones into an existing ASC and they're picking up spend and performing well

Getting closer and closer to being completely done with any manual sales campaigns

If they would just give us ad sets for ASC or allow breakdown for flexible ads I would never use manual again
