In the age of AI and vibe coding

This is exactly what apps and SAAS companies should be doing

Using AI to make your current subscription better bang for your buck by adding more features

Either doing that or reducing the cost of your subscription

In this scenario I'm a fan of just letting the builders build, leave the coding to them

Yeah you can go vibe code your own stuff but when the ROI on the money you pay for the app is just so ridiculous it makes no sense to spend your own money, time and resources to build your own, it just makes no sense

Plus you get friendly UI and support from apps

This is the new moat for apps to safeguard against vibe coding
