The first week structure, the documentation that saves you months of repeating yourself, and how to train someone without doing the work for them.

Our full project management setup, per person views, and the campaign workflow from brief to send.

The exact meeting cadences we use internally, our async rules, and the bonus structures that actually keep good people around.
