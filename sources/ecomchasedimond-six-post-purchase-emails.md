The 6 emails I'd send after someone buys:

Plus a few tips to make them work even better:

- Put one useful product tip in the order confirmation. People open it to check their order, so make it count.

- Send the founder note from a real inbox and read the replies. "Why'd you pick us?" answers are some of the best ad copy you'll find.

- If shipping is slow, say so early. A heads up cuts down on "where's my order?" emails.

- Hold the cross-sell until after it arrives. Let them love the first thing before you pitch the second.

- Split out repeat buyers. Skip the founder note for them and send a short thank-you for coming back instead.
