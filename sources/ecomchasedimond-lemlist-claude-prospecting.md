Five minutes after I gave lemlist 30 companies to pitch for sponsorships, the campaign was loaded. I still had to read it before putting my name on it.

I sell newsletter placements and LinkedIn/X posts to ecommerce and B2B SaaS brands, prospecting to fill gaps in my calendar.

I typed this in Claude through lemlist's MCP:

"Find the person who runs partnerships and the most senior marketing leader at these 30 companies. Verify every email. Tell me which companies have neither role so I can drop them, then build a 4-step email sequence for the rest and show me the preview. Don't send anything."

Results:
- 16 companies had neither role in lemlist's database, so I dropped them
- 23 people across 14 companies
- 22 of 23 emails found and run through verification

Reading every row, I found three contacts with wrong-company domains or outdated jobs, plus one missing email. Removing those contacts cut three companies; kept one contact per company, leaving 11. Nothing reaches a real inbox unread.

Sent only the first email to those 11 people, with follow-ups skipped. All 11 delivered, one person replied positively.
