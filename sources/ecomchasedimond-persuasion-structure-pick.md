title: Pick persuasion structure from reviews
type: clip

Easy way to pick the right one: read your last 20 reviews and support emails.

- If people keep asking the same question (sizing, shipping, "will it work for me?"), start with Objection Answer.

- If they rave about the result, lead with Before-After.

- If they mention specs, use Feature to Benefit.

Your customers already told you which structure to use.
