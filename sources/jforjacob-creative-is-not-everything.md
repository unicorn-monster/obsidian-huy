Sorry but someone needs to say it

Creative IS NOT everything

You could give me the best ads in the world and I could still fuck it up by:

- having a shit offer
- having shit margins
- having a shit landing page
- having a shit supply chain
- having shit distribution
- having a shit product
- having shit product images
- running the ad account like a retard

The list goes on

In fact, the majority of the biggest scalers I know have mediocre ads at best.

First and foremost the product comes first and is by far the most important thing. I've seen complete newbs hit $100k a day because they landed on an absolute banger, not knowing wtf they were doing with creative or anything else

Offer and funnel I would say come next, then creative (Maybe)
