title: 5 jobs email onboarding flow must do
---

Your email onboarding flow to new subscribers is your most impactful flow in your account.

Every email in those two weeks should be doing one specific thing that gets them closer to orders 4+.

5 jobs your onboarding has to do:

1. Get them excited: The second someone subscribes, buyer's remorse kicks in. Email 1 should hype the decision and pack in social proof from subscribers who've been around 6+ months.

2. Set proper expectations: "Life-changing in 7 days" usually leads them to cancel at week 2 if the results don't show up on time. Instead, tell them results take 90+ days, use 6-month testimonials, and give them a week by week timeline.

3. Drive consumption: Over half of cancels come down to "I still have too much product left." Push how to use it, when to use it, and how to pair it with something they already do daily. Habit formed means the will be subscription be retained.

4. Build buy-in: People don't cancel on things they feel part of. So push founder stories, the mission, and other subscribers who use the product. Identity framing is much more effective than a regular discount.

5. Show the benefits of staying: Never let them forget what they'd lose. Highlight the discounts, free gifts, the early access, and the convenience of never running out as long as they stay. Every email should reinforce that subscribed is the better deal.

Do these five things properly and you'll never be sitting there wondering:

"why does everyone cancel on their second order?"
