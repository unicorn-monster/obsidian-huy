Cool promo inspo from Dr. Squatch.

They picked a random 5-day window, gave it a branded name, and released a new free gift every single day.

Great because it does 3 things:

1) Every day is a new drop so people open every email that week just to see what's next. So anticipation goes through the roof for your subscribers.

2) Brand value. A branded 5-day event with its own name feels like something really special. If you brand this right, this can be a huge customer event.

3) It adds sales / novelty in a dead period. You can run this literally whenever you want. Doesn't have to be seasonal or anything. Great way to make a dead period special.
