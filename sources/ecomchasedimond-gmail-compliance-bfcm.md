How To Check Your Gmail Compliance Before Black Friday.

---

A few things worth knowing before you open it:

1. Your domain has to be added and verified in Postmaster Tools first, or the dashboards stay empty.

2. Use the new Postmaster Tools (v2). That's where the Compliance status dashboard lives.

3. Status shows for your main domain, but it counts mail from your subdomains too.

4. Send 5,000+ a day to Gmail even once, and you're a bulk sender for good, so rows 6 to 8 apply to you.

5. Click "Deliverability analysis" under Compliance status for Google's own recommendations.

6. Data runs about a day behind, and fixes can take up to 7 days to show.

Check it this week, so you have time to fix anything before your Black Friday sends.
