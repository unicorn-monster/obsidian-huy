title: Pull FB ad comments as social proof on PDP at scale

If your customers are 40+ and you run a lot of facebook placements, one of the easiest ways to increase cvr is to add fb comments as proof on your pdp and pre-sell pages.

This is how to do it at scale, without manually finding and screenshotting every comment:

Connect claude or codex to meta ads mcp and apify mcp - meta finds your ad url's, apify scrapes the comments.
Give it your ad account and landing page url, then tell it which product or angle to focus on.
Have it shortlist the strongest social proof: people sharing results, overcoming objections, or explaining why they bought again.
Pick the ones you want and ask it to build a comment section using 1-on-1 fb ui - give it a screenshot for reference.
Add it to your pages and see cvr go up.
