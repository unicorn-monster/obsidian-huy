title: "The “cost controls can’t grow a brand” argument falls apart on five fronts"
---

Something that baffles me is the argument that you can't grow a brand using cost controls. They say because you're only focused on profitable spend, that over time you aren't able to drive awareness. "The bad days create the good days" etc etc

1. This happens with cost controls anyway. I regularly have days when I'll spend a lot over my target, only for the next few days to come in under target. With target CPA your CPA averages out to your target over your chosen attribution window. So there are natural cycles of heavier "prospecting"

2. People act like the cap can't be changed lmao. If you want to have a period where you spend more at less efficiency to "drive more awareness" just up your cap. At least in this way you still have control over your margins

3. With lowest cost you have absolutely no idea if you could have spent more. Budget adjustments are always reactive. Ok so you had a good couple of days and upped budget 20% and it went well. How do you know you couldn't have doubled budget? Also, decreases in budget are always in reaction to bad performance I.e you've already lost money. The hubris it takes to think you know better than Meta how much to spend in a given day is mind blowing

4. Using cost controls forces you to solve for spend, which in turn forces you to put effort into the things that matter. If you're not spending as much as you'd like, you know you need to focus on creatives, offer, CRO etc, rather than just deciding your gonna force more spend in the hopes that your performance will just magically increase

5. Time. Not talked about enough. Imagine having to check your ad account several times a day to check if things are ok or if you need to adjust budgets. Not what a founder should be doing. Leaving your spend in the hands of Meta frees up your time and mental real estate to focus on things that truly matter. Plus, like mentioned Meta is far better than any human at this so makes complete sense to outsource it to them. It's like having an employee that's better than anyone else on earth, works 24 hours a day and doesn't want paid and you say "Nah, I'm all good thanks, I've got this" actually insane
