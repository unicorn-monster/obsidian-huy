This is our exact creative testing feedback loop that helped us generate new winners consistently.

Most brands test ads randomly and pray something works.

We turned it into a systematic process that eliminates guesswork.

Here’s our exact framework 👇

Weekly Creative Review Meeting

Every week, my team and I sit down and dissect every single ad we ran.

We start with the winners first.

For each winning creative, we analyze:

• Angle
• Hook (what stopped the scroll?)
• Promise
• Avatar (who exactly was this speaking to?)
• Awareness level
• Pacing (how fast/slow did we reveal information?)
• Desire
• Format (VSL, UGC, static, etc…)

Then we do the same thing with our losers (even more important, cause most likely you’ve more losers than winners)

The goal isn’t to celebrate wins or cry about losses.

It’s to understand WHY something worked or didn’t work.

We’re literally paying Meta to test our hypotheses and extract learnings.

The Iteration Process

Once we identify what made a creative win, we iterate:

For videos:
• Same video, new hooks
• Same script, different creators
• New winning hook + old winning video
• Same script, new B-roll

For statics:
• Same headline, new visuals
• Same visual, new headlines

How We Track Everything

We log every test in a spreadsheet with:

• Status
• Batch number
• Angle
• Hook
• Core desire
• Persona/avatar
• Format
• Testing hypothesis
• Results (high spend + good cpa, poor spend + good cpa, poor spend + poor cpa, good spend + poor cpa)

Why This Matters

The better you understand why people buy your product, the more money you make.

It sounds boring, but it’s necessary work.

Imagine doing this every week for a year.

You’ll develop such a deep understanding of your audience that you’ll never run out of winning concepts again.

That’s the difference between a 6 figs and a 7+ figs / month brand.
