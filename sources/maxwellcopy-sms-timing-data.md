A study of 294 million SMS sends on the best times to text.

Some interesting findings...

Best day for conversions: Friday
Best single hour for revenue: Friday 9 AM (0.238% conversion rate)
Best day for clicks: Saturday (13.76%)
Best time for clicks: 7 AM
Worst day for conversions across the board: Tuesday (0.095%)

Wild thing here is Tuesday is one of the best days for email and it's the worst day for SMS.

So if you're just copying your email schedule over to your texts you're sending on the wrong day.

Also, don't send your email and SMS at the same time...

Email converts best at 7-8 AM, SMS at 8-9 AM, so stagger them and you cover more of the morning instead of competing with your own campaign.
