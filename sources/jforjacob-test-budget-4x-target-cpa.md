Will depend a lot on the price. If the product I'm testing. Usually around 4x the target AOV per day with a minimum of $100

If my target CPA was $20 I would still test at $100 a day even though that's more than 4x

If my target CPA was $50 I would test at around $200 per day

You just apply a "test at $100" a day to everything, despite what you may hear. Doesn't make sense for higher AOV products
