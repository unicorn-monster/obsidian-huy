The real secret to building quiz funnels that print MILLIONS

The first deliverable can be shit... Where we stand out is our ability to create feedback loops and test new hypothesis until we crack the funnel.

Here is our exact Iteration checklist:

Run this on your winning ad and your quiz funnel. Write both columns. Fix the gaps.

1. What is the desire?

The end-state they're buying. Does the funnel promise the same outcome the ad sold -- or a watered-down version of it?

2. Who is the avatar?

Experiences -- what they've tried and failed
Emotions -- what they feel about the problem right now
Behaviours -- what they're doing (or avoiding)
Demographics -- the surface stats
Is the funnel talking to the same person the ad stopped?

3. What is the angle? (the problem we're solving)

The specific problem framing. If the ad's angle is sharp and the funnel's is fuzzy, sharpen the funnel.

If there's no clear problem in the funnel, add one.

4. What is the mechanism?

The named "why this works / why nothing else did."

If the ad teaches a mechanism and the funnel skips it, build the interstitial.

Mechanism is usually the biggest missing piece in a funnel.

5. What is the authority building trust?

Doctor? Founder? Peer? Made-up holistic character?

Whatever earns belief in the ad should carry through the funnel.

Mismatched authority breaks the story mid-funnel.

6. What is the market awareness level?

Unaware > Problem > Solution > Product > Most aware.

The funnel must start where the ad landed them.

A solution-aware funnel behind a problem-aware ad loses people in the first two steps.

7. What is the emotion of the ad? (Valence + Intensity)

Positive or negative, and how hot.

The funnel's opening should meet them at the emotional temperature the ad created -- not reset to neutral.

8. What are we doing that's different? (positioning)

The wedge against every competitor and every past attempt.

Is the funnel defending the same unique position the ad claimed?
