One of the biggest mistakes I see before Black Friday is brands suddenly sending way more email than usual.

Sending more during BFCM is normal, since more emails can mean more sales.

But you can't go from 0 to 100 overnight. Google's own guidelines warn that a sudden jump can get your emails delayed, blocked, or sent to spam.

Here's how I'd ramp up instead:

Now to Oct 31:

1. Check your spam rate in Google Postmaster Tools. It's free, and all you need to do is verify your domain. You want it under 0.10%, and never let it hit 0.30%.

2. Find people who haven't opened or clicked in 90+ days. Ask if they still want your emails, and remove anyone who doesn't respond within 14 days.

Nov 1 to Nov 20:

3. Add one extra send a week, and send it to the people who open and click most (your most active subscribers). If you send 2 a week now, go to 3, then 4, then 5 by Nov 20.

Nov 21 to Nov 30:

4. Send your main sale emails (Black Friday and Cyber Monday) to your most active subscribers first. Then send to everyone else a few hours later, once you've checked that unsubscribes and spam complaints look normal in your email platform.

5. Use real deadlines. If the sale ends Monday at 11:59 PM, end it then.

Start this week, and your Black Friday emails will have a much better shot at the inbox.
