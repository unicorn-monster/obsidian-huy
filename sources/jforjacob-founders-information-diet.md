I believe one of the best things you can do as a founder is go on a full on information diet

Podcasts, Youtube videos, Twitter (except mine)

It's mostly all bs, distracts you and causes analysis paralysis

There only really is a few core things that really move the needle in any business in my experience and you probably already know what they are. Just focus on getting the reps in on those few things

Theres no point overloading yourself with new info if you haven't internalised the stuff you already know and mastered the basics
