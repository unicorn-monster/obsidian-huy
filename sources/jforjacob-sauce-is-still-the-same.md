The sauce is still the same as it always was

- Know your customer
- Write ads that speak to them
- Produce ads in formats native to platform
- Have a cold friendly offer
- Optimise your business for LTV
- Scale your ads according to your LTV not your day one revenue

There is too much noise, too many new toys to play with and I honestly have abdicated from most of it

And funny enough we're doing better than ever just sticking with the basics and not getting distracted

Whenever I see something new being hyped I just think about the above and if it will actually help me get better in any of these areas, if not I just don't bother

Everyone's found a new software that is a "game changer", a new prompt that will "change your life" etc

Yet I haven't seen many people really scale with all this stuff

In fact, the timeline looks down bad from what I see

The cost of operating is lower than ever, the speed to ship is faster than ever yet your revenue is flat.

Maybe the bottleneck was never the speed to get stuff out the door or get it done cheaper?

Maybe it's always been quality and everything you're putting your focus into right now does not increase quality?
