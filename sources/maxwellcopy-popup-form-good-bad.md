Bad Pop-Up vs Good Pop-Up Form:

Bad:
- Offer is buried instead of being the first thing you see
- Unnecessary copy nobody is reading
- Asks a question but the setup behind it is wrong
- Nothing branded about it

Good:
- Offer is front and center, impossible to miss
- Low barrier question first, then asks for the email
- Collects data that lets you personalize email content after
- Branded and fun enough that people actually engage
