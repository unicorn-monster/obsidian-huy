for subscription brands running post purchase upsells

You should not try to upsell non subscribers onto a subscription

Instead you should be upselling them a 6 month or 12 month supply upfront at a discount

1. Will likely be worth more than the LTV of your average subscriber

2. You get that money collected on day 1 instead of having to wait for up to a year for a subscriber to pay you that

3. Take rate can be surprisingly high on these if framed correctly

Do not fall into the trap of automatically thinking that subscription is always where the money is
