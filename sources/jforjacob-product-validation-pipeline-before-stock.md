Identify high monthly search volume with exploding topics or Keyword Planner > Cross reference Amazon US & UK & TT shop monthly sales > Search Meta ad library for brands selling the product (how long for, amount of active ads, which countries etc) > cross reference with Similarweb / Semrush for traffic volume and source > Go through reviews on Amazon, Trustpilot & Reddit to identify dislikes / issues > Identify gaps to exploit based on your research (open markets, pricing / offer, addressing flaws etc) > create website & ads based on this > run campaigns without stock or drop-ship to get proof of concept > refund any sales made if without stock > If profitable order MOQ's & customise / modify as per your research > fulfil from Chinese 3pl giving you operating / margin advantage over competitors
