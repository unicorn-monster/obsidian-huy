title: Churn-proof subscription billing reminder email
type: clip

If you're running a subscription brand, you should constantly be churn-proofing your billing reminder.

Your customer is deciding whether to stay the second that email lands.

So give them the obvious reasons to not cancel:

- What's actually shipping to them
- A free gift coming in this order
- How much they've saved by staying subscribed
- Where they are in their results journey
- Testimonials from people further along than them
- The perks they'd lose by leaving
- What's waiting for them in the next order

You're just reminding them what they already bought into...

So simply stack it above the manage subscription button so they scroll past all of it first.

Think about it from the customer's POV... they just got an email telling them money is about to leave their account.

If the first thing they see is a cancel button, you've made it easy for them bounce.

So bury it at the very bottom so everything good comes first.

People are looking for a reason to decide either way.

So make sure the reason to stay is the only thing in front of them.
