title: Seedance 2.5 + Opus 5 YouTube ads 40-minute workflow
---

seedance 2.5 generates 30-second youtube ads with multi-shot narratives and character consistency in a single prompt

and connected to opus 5 through higgsfield MCP, the entire creative production pipeline runs in one chat session.

step 1: research the buyer (20 min)

paste this into opus 5:

"research the top 5 pain point clusters for [product category] buyers. for each: primary frustration in buyer language, failed solutions, exact phrases from reddit and amazon, emotional state, trigger event. ranked table"

replaces 4-6 hours of manual forum crawling. the output comes back in the buyer's own words instead of marketing speak

step 2: write the script (10 min)

"write a 15-second youtube shorts ad script for [product]. 4-part structure: hook (0-3s, name the pain from the research), agitation (3-8s, name 3 failed solutions), mechanism (8-12s, explain the cause), reveal (12-15s, product as best implementation, soft CTA). conversational tone. include camera directions for seedance 2.5"

the 4-part structure separates ads printing revenue from ads burning budget. the hook stops the scroll, the agitation validates the buyer's frustration, the mechanism builds trust through education, and the product inherits the trust the mechanism built

step 3: generate the video (5 min)

"generate a 15-second vertical 9:16 video. person talking to camera in a bathroom, phone-camera quality. they open mid-sentence: '[hook from script]'. cut to close-up of hands holding [product]. cut back to face, smiling: '[resolution from script]'. natural lighting. native audio"

seedance 2.5 through higgsfield MCP produces the video directly inside the opus 5 chat, with no tab-switching, no export/import, and no manual file management

step 4: generate the static companion (3 min)

"generate a lifestyle product image of [product] on [surface]. dramatic rim lighting. 1:1 for shopping. no text overlay. no watermark"

GPT images 2.0 through the same higgsfield MCP produces the shopping companion to the youtube short

total: under 40 minutes for a video ad plus shopping image grounded in real buyer psychology

the traditional version runs 2-3 weeks, $5-15k, and a team of 3-5 people

youtube ad fatigue is real. CTR decays after 3-4 weeks on the same hook because the audience pool is narrower than search intent. AI production makes the refresh cycle economically viable at speed instead of a monthly production bottleneck

build 5 ads in one afternoon, test them, and once you find the winning hook, spin 4 fresh variations every few weeks to keep the format alive without rebuilding from scratch
