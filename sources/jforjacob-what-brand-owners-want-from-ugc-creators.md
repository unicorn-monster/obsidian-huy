There is a reason everybody wants AI creators to succeed

It's because 99% of real "UGC creators" are utter dog shit. If you are a UGC creator and don't want to be replaced by AI please read this

First, pricing. I know you've been through some UGC course teaching you how to get the most money possible out of brands but believe me, it's killing you. Charging $250 per video plus usage rights will not get you very far long term. Sure you might get a deal here and there but nobody, and I mean nobody wants to work with creator on that basis. They will be actively looking to replace you

A brand will be very lucky to have 10% of ads be profitable. If you charge $250 per video, $250 x 10 = $2500. Not to mention the fact they probably will have spent around $2,500 just testing the other 9 ads that didn't work

The average ecommerce brand has an EBITA of 10%. Let's say it took them paying $2,500 and another $2,500 lost testing the other ads that didn't work to finally have something that is profitable, then you tell them you want 10% usage rights on top of this. At that point you are a burden to the brand, not an asset

I think any creator that just charged a reasonable fee ($50-$100) per 30 second video would clean up. Brands would be paying you again and again and again (assuming you did good work)

I also think, any creator that was smart enough to just charge on performance would have so many brands after them they wouldn't be able to cope. No upfront fee, you just get a % of the amount the ad spends. Less upfront risk on the brands part, more upside for you if the ad performs. Brand is more than happy to pay you that % of the creative if it is performing. You could be earning money on a creative every month for a year if you weren't so short sighted. Think of it like TikTok shop affiliate payouts but you'd be getting paid far longer as ads on Meta have a far longer lifespan than any video on TT

Next, is quality of work and punctuality. Can't tell you the amount of times I've had creators reach out to me, begging for a chance to make content for us, we send them the product and then no content gets made - or it takes around 2 months. When it does get delivered it's usually terrible. No research done on the creators part to even understand how the product works or how to use it properly

Almost every brand owner I know is in the same position. Seems like everyone and their mother is a UGC creator these days yet it's almost impossible to find a single one that is good and charges reasonable prices

The bar is so incredibly low and it would be so easy to clean up as a creator if you listened to the above advice

I am a brand owner telling you exactly what brand owners want, please stop listening to the other creators and courses teaching you how to squeeze as much out of brands as possible, it's not sustainable for the brands

If things don't change, you all will be replaced by AI incredibly soon
