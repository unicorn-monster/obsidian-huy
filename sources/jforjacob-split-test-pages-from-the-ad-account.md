title: "Split test pages from the ad account, not with on-site tools"

Something to think about when testing new pages that I see nobody talk about

Anyone who runs more than one page from their ad account will know that you can run the exact same ads at the exact same budget to two different pages and one will have higher, CPM's and CPC's

When you split test pages using split testing tools this isn't accounted for

Ok great, you had a 10% lift in rev per visitor but then when you roll it out across your ad account your CPM goes up 20%

This is just one of many reasons I believe split testing pages from the ad account is the best way to do things
