Contrary to popular belief this store with a relatively high AOV has been the hardest to scale store I've ever ran, but finally taking off

Maybe now we've cracked the creative and offer the high contribution margin will enable us to scale this further than any others so far, but definitely was more difficult to get initial traction with

Lower AOV's are great as they usually don't take much consideration from customers and high levels of trust don't need to be there etc so easy to see initial success

Really interested to see how far we can push this one now
