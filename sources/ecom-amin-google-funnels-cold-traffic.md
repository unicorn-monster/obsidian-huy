title: Funnels for cold Google traffic beat PDPs consistently

"advertorials and quiz funnels are a meta thing. google traffic goes to product pages"

every ecom founder believes this. and it's costing them the single biggest opportunity on google right now

the belief comes from a real place. google IS mostly demand capture and meta IS mostly demand generation. google catches people mid-purchase, meta chases people mid-scroll

so founders assume the funnel arsenal (advertorials, listicles, comparison pages, VSLs, quizzes) belongs on meta, where the traffic is cold and needs convincing

wrong conclusion

because google isn't 1 temperature. you can run:

- non-branded search
- non-branded shopping
- demand gen
- PMax

and every one of these hits audiences who are NOT ready to buy your product yet

someone searching their problem with no clue your solution exists. someone comparing 5 options. someone who half-remembers a product from a video. these people land on your PDP, see a price for something they don't understand yet, and leave

that's where the funnels come in

- an advertorial amplifies the problem and explains the solution before the pitch
- a listicle compares products side by side and does the shopping FOR them
- a quiz diagnoses people who don't even know what their problem is
- a comparison page catches the "X vs Y" searcher at peak intent

across every account we run, funnels beat straight PDPs on this colder google traffic. not sometimes. consistently

and here's the wilder part: even on pure PDP traffic, adding a bit of sales copy to the page almost never hurts conversion. some buyers typing an exact product name are STILL not fully sure. light persuasion catches them, and it puts you ahead of 99% of your market because nobody else on google bothers

meta's sophistication arrived on google and almost nobody showed up for it

google's future is funnels. youtube ads and demand gen are only making the cold layers bigger

the brands building this now will own the channel while everyone else keeps wondering why their PDP "doesn't convert cold traffic"
