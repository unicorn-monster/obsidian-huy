Here's how to test offers before Q4.

We run weekend promos from Friday to Sunday. Always 3 days.

Why 3 days?

Most people buy on day 1 (excitement) and the last day (FOMO).

That way we capture peak buying behavior without discounting for too long.

We'll test different offers across several weekends.  Here are a few offers to test:

- Buy X, Get Y Free
- X% OFF Everything
- X% OFF + Free Shipping
- Spend X get free gift
- Spend X get Y% OFF, Spend Z, get A% OFF, etc…

Each promo weekend we track:

- Total revenue
- Conversion rate
- Average order value
- Profit margins
- Customer acquisition cost

We're not trying to maximize revenue during these tests.

We're just collecting data.

By the time BFCM hits, we already know exactly which offer will perform the best.
