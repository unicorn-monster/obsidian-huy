1. Keyword-rich product title:

Include search terms people actually use when looking for your product.

Instead of: “SuperGlow Pro”

Use: “Anti-Aging Vitamin C Serum for Dark Spots & Wrinkles”

The second version includes keywords like “anti-aging,” “vitamin C serum,” “dark spots,” and “wrinkles” that people search for.

You want benefits + what the product actually is + primary use case.

2. Difference between the two descriptions:

Section 3 short description: This goes right under your title/reviews.

It’s a quick 1-2 line summary of what the product does.

Example: “Clinically proven vitamin C serum that fades dark spots and reduces fine lines in 4 weeks.”

Section 8 short benefit text: This is in your main outcome section with the lifestyle image.

It’s more aspirational and outcome-focused.

Example: “Wake up to radiant, youthful skin that makes you feel confident without makeup.”
