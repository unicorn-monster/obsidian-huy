title: Cut subscription rebill day cancel rate 38%

We decrease this 9-figure brand's rebill day cancel rate by 38%.

They came to us with a churn problem… They wanted fewer people to cancel.

So we pulled their data first and found that day 30 was their biggest spike cancellation day by far...

The second someone got rebilled on their subscription, a chunk of them would cancel on the spot.

We took a look at their setup and saw that they were sending multiple subscription confirmation emails and essentially inviting people to cancel…

So what did we do?

We rebuilt the order confirmation email to be less focused on how much the customer was charged and more focused on the ongoing benefits they're getting.

Then, we killed one of their Recharge emails that was firing at the same time and muddying the whole thing up.

We can see the clear decrease in same-day cancellations.

Massive for them.
