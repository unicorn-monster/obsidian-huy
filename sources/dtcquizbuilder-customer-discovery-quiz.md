Letting customers discover the solution converts better than telling them the solution.

Same product, two ways to sell it. A gut health supplement:

Path 1: the ad says "bloated? our probiotic fixes it"

Path 2: you ask first. Do you get bloated after meals? Do you crash around 2pm? Do you sleep badly after a heavy dinner? By the end they've landed on it themselves: it's my gut.

In path 1 they're comparing your claim to their life and looking for where it doesn't fit.

In path 2 they're tying it back to their own experiences instead of being told what their experience is supposed to be.

The conclusion is theirs, so they believe it a lot more, and the product is just the next step.
