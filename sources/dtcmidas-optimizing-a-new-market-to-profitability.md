You're usually just a few optimizations away from being profitable and able to scale.

Here's how optimizing actually looks like in practice.

We launched in a new market a bit over a month ago.

From the start CAC was high, conversion was shit, and we were burning money.

So here's what we did:

First, we changed the offer and dropped pricing.

Price drop usually decreases your CAC (but also your margins).

This got us more volume which gave us more data to work with. 

Because you can't optimize without data.

Our winning ads from our main market didn't work in this new market.

So we went back to the research table and started testing new angles and messaging specifically for this market.

After testing 25+ concepts, one finally showed potential.

Once we found traction, we rebuilt the entire funnel around that angle.

New landing pages, rebuilt pdp and changed product positioning.

Everything aligned to reinforce this new message.

Now everything started picking up.

Then we made more ads in different formats iterating on that winning angle and messaging.

Finally, we raised prices back to the original level.

Our margins weren't sustainable long-term at the lower price point.

After price increase sales held up because the messaging was dialed in.

Now we're first-order profitable in this market and able to scale.

Next step is assessing retention for this market to see if we can increase our target CAC over time to scale harder.
