"I almost had a heart attack on a Caribbean cruise."

Metabolae is ripping with this native static ad right now

They run it from a persona page instead of the brand handle

The creative is a candid photo of a woman who is "64 years old" on vacation

The copy hooks you with a trip saved for "almost three years"

On "Day four" she has a health scare walking cobblestone streets

A doctor "from Switzerland" asks "ONE question" that makes her doctor look "15 years behind"

I love this setup {the villain is outdated advice, not the patient}

The click lands on an advertorial listicle under a "HEALTH-INSIDER" banner

Headline flips to "Why I Stopped Buying Every Other Blood Sugar Supplement"

Right under it, the copy gives instant absolution:
"Usually, it wasn't cinnamon that failed them."

It blames cheap cinnamon and introduces Ceylon cinnamon with MCT oil for absorption

Just "one softgel a day"

Older buyers in this market have already tried natural remedies that did not work

If you pitch another bottle cold, they bounce

Showing WHY past attempts failed kills that objection before the pitch

By the offer, buying the softgels feels like taking the right version instead of starting over
