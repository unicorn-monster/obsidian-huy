We just added an unsubscribe analysis tab to WellTV to really understand WHO is unsubscribing from your emails.

Are they high value people? Does your send cadence actually influence it?

I hear constantly from brand owners who freak out about their unsubscribe rate and want to know if they should send less…

But we found the rate on its own tells you almost nothing without knowing who it's made up of.

For this brand specifically, 89% of unsubscribes are non-customers. That means they were most likely pop-up signups who never bought anything.

Honestly, I don't give a shit if those people unsubscribe…

It's fine to pull people into your funnel, hit them with a ton of emails, and have some of them leave. That means they were never interested in the first place and weren't going to buy.

What I actually care about though is BUYERS. Here, only 1.9% of unsubscribes were repeat buyers. Those are the ones worth protecting at $454 in lifetime value each.

You can further see in the campaigns tab that returning buyers are also driving serious revenue on nearly every send, which backs up exactly why that group matters much much more lol.

So how do you actually use this?

Just track your unsubscribes from your non-buyers and repeat buyers separately…

If repeat buyer unsubs start climbing as you push frequency, that's your ceiling and you should ease off for that segment only.

Everything else doesn't matter.
