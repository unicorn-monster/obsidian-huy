This is not how the math works.

If you’re acquiring at $60 CAC with $40 AOV, you’re losing way more than $20 upfront.

You still need to deduct COGS, shipping, handling, fulfillment, payment processing, etc…

Realistically, you’re probably losing $30 on the first order, not $20.

The bigger issue is cash flow.

This model sounds great on paper, but it’s not feasible for most brands because you need actual cash to fund the losses upfront.

You can’t just “outspend competitors” without either:

- Significant outside financing
- An already established base of retention orders generating cash
- Deep pockets to sustain months of negative cash flow

If you have strong retention AND the cash to fund it, yes - this is how you scale supplements & consumables.

But you need bulletproof unit economics and cash flow modeling before attempting this strategy.

Most brands that try this approach run out of cash before they see the LTV materialize.
