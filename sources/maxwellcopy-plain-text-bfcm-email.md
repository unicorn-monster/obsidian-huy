title: Plain text email wins BFCM over designed banners

You should be plain text maxxing your emails this BFCM.

Last year these crushed, and they will again this year.

We had brands where over half of all their sends were plain text and those were the ones carrying most of the revenue.

Everyone is getting spammed with promo banners and holiday graphics… the text-based emails stand out through simplicity.

A few quick tips on how to set these up right:

1. Send it from the founder or someone on the team rather than the brand name. That personal touch goes a long way.

2. Bold your offer and the key info. Blocks of copy never get fully read… so make it skimmable in three seconds.

3. One clear CTA… try not to stack five buttons in there because you will overwhelm the user.

Design has its place, but make sure you are plain text maxxing best results.

Also you can write these in like ten minutes lol.
