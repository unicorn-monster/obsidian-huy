Big hack that's stupidly simple that has helped us a lot with "hit rate"

If you test an ad and see good soft metrics but CPA isn't good. just throw up a quick lander dedicated for that specific ad

We've been doing this a lot for statics with low CPC's (typical click-baity kind of stuff)

People will tell you not to run these type of ads but don't listen to them. Usually people who say this have never done what i'm talking about. Those that have, know

Publish them, see what's picking up spend and getting low CPC's THEN make an advertorial or listicle with a headline that follows on from whatever you've baited them with and then go full Sugarman

Doesn't take long if you've already got listicle / advertorial formats built out, really making a big difference for us
