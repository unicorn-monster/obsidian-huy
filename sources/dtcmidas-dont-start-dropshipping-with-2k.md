Don’t start dropshipping with only $2K to your name.

You’ll most likely lose it.

Here’s the better path:

Learn one valuable skill that ecom brands actually need: 

- Media buying
- Creative strategy
- Email marketing
- Building landing pages
- CRO

Get a job at a brand or agency first.

This gives you:

- Real experience seeing what actually works
- A salary while you learn
- Time to save capital for your own store
- Network of people who can help later

Once you have 6-12 months of experience and $10K+ saved, then consider starting your own thing.
