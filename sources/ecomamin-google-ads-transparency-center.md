google shows you every ad your competitors are running. for free

search, shopping, youtube with the full video, and which countries they're in

it's called google ads transparency center

how to use it:

1. type in a brand doing 7-8 figures
2. filter to youtube
3. set region to germany (or any EU country) to see the date each ad was first shown. works if they also advertise in the EU

an ad running 6+ months is printing

pick 3 brands. study what they're running

don't clone it. find the angle and improve it for your offer
