Been experimenting a lot with flexible ads the last couple of months and finding that Meta has a strong preference for this format

Example: I had an existing ASC running with a shit ton of spend behind it, took the top 10 performing ads from it, put them all into a flexible ad and launched it in the same campaign

Meta started spending heavily on the flexible ad from day one. Around a month later now and that flexible ad is spending more than double the amount of all the other ads in that campaign combined with overall campaign spend going up

I found it surprising that the flexible ad immediately got so much spend considering it was just using existing ads that were already running in the campaign which had a lot of purchases and data already behind them

I still think flexible ads are next to useless for testing so here's what I'm now doing and it seems to be working well:

Test new creatives as normal (separate testing campaign, 3-5 ads per concept, each concept gets its own ad set)

Once I've got spend and data on the tests I'll extract out any variations from the concept that we're getting spend and hitting CPA targets and launch them as a flexible ad in my scaling campaign (cost controlled ASC)

This way I get to benefit from Meta's preference for flexible ads but still get to have a useful testing framework that my creative team can break down, isolate variables and learn from

Worth experimenting with for others I think
