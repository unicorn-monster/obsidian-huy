9 Black Friday / Cyber Monday 2026 non-negotiables:

1. Start Messaging in September, not November.

26% of shoppers begin holiday buying by end of September, and that's when ad attention is cheapest. Run brand awareness and grow the list in September, save the aggressive email push for November when the buying actually happens.

2. Cycle your offers.

Most brands pick one discount and run it from Black Friday through Cyber Monday. Run separate offers for early access, Black Friday, Cyber Monday, and post-Cyber gifting instead. Each new offer resets urgency and reactivates people who ignored the last one.

3. Send 2-4x per day on peak days.

Inbox competition is brutal and one email a day WILL get buried. Send two to three on Black Friday and Cyber Monday, and up to four on your biggest days. You'll get unsubscribes and that's fine.

4. Open the floodgates on segmentation.

Every launch and last chance email should go to your full list. Mid-campaign sends can rotate between the full list and a wide 365 or 730 day engaged segment... and make sure to unsuppress anyone who's ever opened, browsed or bought and hasn't complained.

5. Resend the same emails.

A great BFCM email gets 50% opens, so half your list never saw it. Send the same email with a new subject line, and preview text. Nobody will remember or get mad.

6. Use plain text emails from the founder.

While everyone's blasting flashy graphics, the ones that will break through in 2026 look like they came from a friend. It's faster to write and easier to ship too.

7. Keep graphic emails dead simple.

Have a bold hero with the offer in the biggest font on the page, and one high-contrast CTA above the fold. This is not the time for storytelling season...

8. Update your pop-ups for every offer period.

Your traffic peaks during BFCM, so turning the pop-up off wastes every visitor who leaves without buying. Match it to whatever offer is live that week.

9. Update your flows with quick delays.

Have one email for welcome and site abandon, two for browse, cart and checkout... all with a two hours time delay between sends.
