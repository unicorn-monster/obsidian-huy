The problem is that most people nowadays consume information through short form content and majority of people who have the time to dedicate to consistent output of content aren't actually the experts in the things they talk about

If they were, they wouldn't be doing short form content. They'd be doing the thing they're talking about

It's why there is such a big difference in the stuff the "thought leaders" say to do and the actual alpha that works

Everything I hear from these people is either old, rinsed, needlessly made to sound complicated or just flat out doesn't work

The people talking about the thing are usually just larping, the people doing the thing are too busy doing it

Why just trying to do anything to work for / help someone who is actively doing the business model you're interested in (and is doing it well) is usually the best path for education
