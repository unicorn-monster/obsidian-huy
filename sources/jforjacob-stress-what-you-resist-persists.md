title: "Actively trying to get rid of stress strengthens it"

I know a lot of people, especially those running a business that struggle with this and it's something that I can honestly say I'm good with and it's probably my biggest strength

The thing I see with people who aren't good at handling stress is they are always trying to 'get rid of it' or 'calm themselves down' etc

Stress is fear based. The foundation of fear is avoidance. By actively trying to rid yourself of stress you actually strengthen it. You're literally making it worse

Even if you find temporary relief in some of things you do to get rid of it or avoid it, you are strengthening the positive feedback cycle of fear and avoidance

My best advice is when you find yourself stressed or afraid, is to do the opposite. Actively try to prolong it or make it stronger and let it go away on its own. This breaks the positive feedback cycle. Stress and fear can only exist when there is avoidance

A good thing to remember is 'what you resist, will persist'
