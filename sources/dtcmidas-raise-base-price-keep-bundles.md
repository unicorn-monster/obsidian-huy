title: "Test increasing your 1 piece base price while keeping bundle prices the same"
---

If you run bundle offers, test increasing your base price (the 1 piece price) and keeping the bundle prices the same.

This does a few things:

- Your bundles look like a better deal, because the discount vs 1 piece gets bigger

- More people pick a bundle, because buying just 1 piece is now less attractive

- Your AOV goes up, so you capture more of your LTV on day 1

-  You attract better customers - our data shows retention is better with customers who bought more on day 1.

This is because most repeat purchase products take some time to show results and/or to become a habit.

The odds of one or both happening are higher when someone has enough stock. If someone buys 1 piece and runs out before seeing results, the chance they come back is much lower.

Increasing baseprice has some downsides too, but it helped us improve unit economics by a lot lately.
