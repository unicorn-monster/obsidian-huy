"adding google will cannibalise our meta revenue"

i hear this from every meta-first founder before we show them the data. here's what happens in practice

a clothing brand doing over $50k a month on meta, numbers working, no google presence to speak of

we added a google ecosystem alongside their existing meta setup: remarketing, branded search, shopping, MOF search

google spend of $3,845 produced $61,783 in conversion value at 14.83x ROAS

meta revenue didn't decrease by a single dollar. blended profitability climbed across both platforms

here's why

when someone sees your meta ad and doesn't buy (95-97% of first-time visitors), they spend the next 72 hours browsing the internet

without google remarketing, your buyer sees your meta retargeting ad one more time on one platform

with google remarketing, the same buyer sees the jacket they browsed appear in their gmail promotion tab, on the news site they read at lunch, and before the youtube video they watch at night, across two million+ publisher sites

by touchpoint five across three different platforms, the brand feels established and the purchase feels inevitable

meta creates the demand and google closes the sale

the conversion lands in your google dashboard, but the demand came from meta. google captured a sale meta had created but couldn't close alone

and here's the feedback loop nobody expects:

google remarketing makes meta retargeting more effective. buyers hit by google touchpoints convert at higher rates on the meta retargeting ad, click-through rate rises, cost per acquisition drops, and the meta team changed nothing

the two channels compound

$41k a month from google didn't reduce this brand's meta revenue. blended profitability improved across both platforms because the remarketing pools got bigger

honest caveat: without a geo-holdout, some share of those google conversions would've closed through organic or direct type-in anyway. even discounting 25-30% for overlap, the incremental gain still lands in the five figures a month

9 out of 10 meta-first brands i audit share the same blind spot: strong meta operation, dead google operation

the gap represents $20-100k a month in revenue nobody is collecting

google validates the demand meta already generated
