There isn't a single product page template on Replo that is as good as a standard Shopify theme template. Wondering what is the purpose of them?

Don't know who is curating the templates but I don't see any that don't look like they've came from a 2010's Wordpress site

Why would you have a GymShark pdp template in there? Brand aside, the page is below average at best and could literally be achieved by a 13 year old newby to Shopify with default sections

Surely the point of having pre-made templates is to quickly achieve things that you couldn't do in Shopify, or things that would take a long time or require a lot of custom coding? There's not a single template in there I would use

I get they take them from big brands, but I find a lot of the times they have the worst websites. GymShark being a prime example

Like, look at the 'CRO Top Pick' product pages. Surely someone is having a laugh here? The Baggu product page? Come on. It's literally terrible
