I think one of the most valuable things you can be doing as a brand owner (or any business owner tbh) is learning how to use tools like N8N or have someone else do it

You will be able to replace a lot of the SAAS tools you're paying hundreds if not thousands every month for

Quick example: I was annoyed there didn't seem to be a good or reasonably priced tool for automatically launching facebook ads. They all either had big limitations or cost more money than I pay a VA to do it

Was able to get it built in N8N, exactly how I wanted it with no limitations and is basically free

Ads get uploaded to a google drive file named 'to upload' they get automatically launched to facebook as structured in that folder. Sub folders are ad sets, the ads within those are the ads. Ad sets inherit the folder name, ads inherit the file name

Then once they upload to facebook they get automatically moved into another folder in my drive called 'uploaded' I then get a message in Slack telling me that ads have been uploaded along with their details

Will next build an image gen automation to tack on to this so it can also launch a bunch of AI generated ads on autopilot without paying silly subscriptions. Will be able to connect it with a custom gpt that knows everything about my brand, scrape ad libraries, reviews, reddit threads etc for context. Will likely take around a week and have almost no cost other than open AI credits
