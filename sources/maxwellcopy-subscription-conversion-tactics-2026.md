Want more customers to subscribe to your product?

- your welcome offer should be subscription only, and push that offer in every welcome email and sms.
- use your abandonment emails to sell the subscription offer. most brands give 30-50% OFF subscription but never mention that banger offer in their abandonments… why?
- show the subscribe price right next to the one-time price in your emails so the gap is obvious

Want to turn your one-time buyers into subscribers?

- do not do generic "thanks for your order" emails, hit them with subscription upgrade dedicated emails
- give them an offer they can only get by subscribing
- check your data for when people actually reorder and send your messages right before that (usually day 20 is good)
- keep your emails going for a few weeks. One email won't do it.

Want your campaigns converting people to subscription?

- lead with your subscription offer in every send
- mix in campaigns on why subscribing is the smarter move
- for sitewide sales, don't just say 20% off sale when you also give 20% off on subscription… advertise it as 40% off subscription and 20% off one-time
- stop optimizing your PDP for subscription and then forgetting it exists everywhere else
