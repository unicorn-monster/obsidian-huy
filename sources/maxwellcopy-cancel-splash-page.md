title: Splash page tactic to save subscription cancellations
---

The most effective time to save cancellations as an ecom brand is right at the point of cancellation.

Step 1: Add a splash page that fires the second someone clicks cancel.
Step 2: Put a founder video on it thanking them and addressing the cancellation directly.
Step 3: Give them a delay or skip option right there instead of making them cancel outright.

Simple as it gets and it catches people before they've even committed to leaving.
