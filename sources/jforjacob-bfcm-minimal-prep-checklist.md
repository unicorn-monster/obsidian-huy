title: BFCM minimal prep most brands overthink it
---

For most brands Black Friday is seriously overhyped and so much unnecessary time is spent on preparation

All you really need is:

- Banner on website (optional countdown timer)

- Add BFCM offer to hero image on PDP

- Launch a bunch of offer statics

- Take best evergreen video ads and put BFCM banner on them

- Plan a few more emails over that period

All in all takes like 2 or 3 days max and even the above stuff is optional

Only exceptions here were a serious amount of time and planning needs to go into it are brands that rely heavily on gifting

Most brand would be better off just focusing on their evergreen efforts and launching new products etc
