MAKE SURE YOU CREATE CUSTOM BLACK FRIDAY POP-UPS

Some brands will keep their regular pop-up form live during the month of November or, worse, turn it off...

But you're going to be getting a shit ton of traffic here that you want to be sure you capture.

Update the pop-up offer so it matches what's running sitewide, and ideally swap the background and creative to your Black Friday look.

Yes, the customer doesn't need to opt in in order to access the sale offer... but people still opt-in. Your messaging in the welcome flow just goes to "discount automatically applied"

AliaPopups just dropped a ton of templates within their pop-up editor we're using across our brands.
