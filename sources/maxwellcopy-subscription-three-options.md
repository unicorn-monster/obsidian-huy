title: Three subscription options beat two via anchoring
---

One of the easiest ways to increase your take rate move is giving people three subscription options instead of two.

> A monthly subscription
> A quarterly subscription
> A one-time purchase.

The reason three beats two is that two of your three options are now subscription, so subscribing becomes the default frame...

The quarterly option anchors monthly as the middle, safe choice....

And one-time becomes the obvious worst value sitting there next to both of them....

Most people end up subscribing simply because it's two out of three options on the page.

Surprised by how many brands actually structure it this way lol.
