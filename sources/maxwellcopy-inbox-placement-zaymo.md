This actually changes the game.

Landing in Promotions instead of Primary is the quickest way to cap how much your email channel can make you.

The problem was that fixing it meant weeks of warming segments and trying to restore your reputation, and there was no clean way to even tell if any of it was working…

Zaymo's Inbox Booster has now come to the rescue.

You can now get into Primary without touching your sending schedule or migrating anything.

All you do is drop a block onto the emails you're already sending.

It varies the text on every send so each one reads uniquely to Gmail and Yahoo's filters, and every send runs as a live A/B test against a control so you see the actual lift.

The idea itself isn't completely new… there are other softwares that have been doing versions of this for a while.
