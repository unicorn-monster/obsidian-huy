Hiro Analytics always has a new dashboard I end up finding just by poking around...

The AB tests tab is elite.

We've been testing a ton of subject lines lately on plain text emails, and normally I'd have to go back through each campaign report one at a time to remember what we even ran...

But Hiro pulls it all in one clean view, the actual creative for each variation, and which one won.

Sounds simple but it means your testing history actually lives somewhere instead of getting lost the second the campaign is over.
