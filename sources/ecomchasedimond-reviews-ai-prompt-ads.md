Your customers already wrote your best ads. The AI prompt that finds them is in the reply.

A few tips before you run it:

- Support emails and post-purchase survey answers work too. Paste them in with the reviews.
- Take out names and email addresses before you paste anything in.
- Spot-check every quote against the original review before you use it.
- Use what you find everywhere: ads, product pages, landing pages, and your homepage.

---

Here's the full prompt to copy:

1. Here are customer reviews for [product]. Use only their words. Don't invent anything.

2. List the phrases they repeat about the problem they had before buying.

3. List the exact words they use to describe the result. Quote them word for word.

4. List every worry they mention, and what changed their mind.

5. Write 5 headlines and 5 ad hooks built from their exact phrases.

6. For each line, show the review it came from. Flag any claim we'd need to prove.

Example:

Review: "I finally sleep through the night without waking up sweaty"

Headline: Finally sleep through the night.

[paste reviews here]

Tip: gather 50 to 100 reviews from every star rating. 5-star reviews give you the result words. 3-star reviews show the doubts and what changed their mind. 1 to 2-star reviews show the objections your ads need to answer.
