Two sale email ideas that beat the normal 10% off:

1) Golden ticket

Instead of blasting the same discount, you frame it like they got picked. "Here's your golden ticket for $15 credit."

2) Digital gift card

Same code, but presented inside a gift card graphic instead of a coupon banner. "A $10 digital gift card, from us to you."

Exact same discount either way but now it changes how it feels on the other end.

Human psychology at it's finest...

Slightly different framing scratches a completely different itch in the brain.
