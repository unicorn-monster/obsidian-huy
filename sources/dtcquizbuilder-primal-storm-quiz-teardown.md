Primal Storm went from est. ~59k to ~878k visits/mo in 2 months.

I checked their ads: brand ads dropped to 79. Persona page "The Perf Hub" scaled to 2,191 instead.

Why it works: pitching ED pills is cooked. They hook with "I got scammed" & sell a free 7-day plan.

---

Once in, they ask the hard question: rating erection strength down to "Too soft to perform."

Then instant absolution:

"YOU ARE NOT ALONE! 66.5% of men feel the same way."

Remove the shame before any pitch. That is how you sell to a sensitive market.

---

The single move to copy:

Primal Storm cloned one quiz skeleton across 5 segment variants (e.g. the diabetic men version).

How to apply it: build one working quiz backbone. Then clone it for your best niches instead of forcing one hook on everyone.
