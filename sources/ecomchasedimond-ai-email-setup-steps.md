This is a good way to use AI for email. Here's how I'd set it up for your brand this week:

1. Pick your 10 best emails ever (highest revenue or clicks) and give them to the AI with this prompt:

"Study these emails. Write a one-page guide to how this brand writes: tone, length, words we use, words we never use, and how we sell. I'll use it for every future email."

Save that guide and paste it in every time you ask for a new email.

2. Give it a folder of real product photos, and tell it to only use those

3. Give it a list of what it can never say: fake discounts, made-up reviews, or health and results claims

4. If your AI connects to your email platform, have it build each email as a draft there. If not, drop its copy into one of your saved templates. Either way, you edit one line instead of redoing the whole email

5. Before you send, spend 5 minutes on 4 checks: right audience, right offer, right send time, links working

Once a month, show it your best and worst emails so it learns what your customers respond to.

Start with one campaign a week. Once the drafts need almost no edits, give it more.
