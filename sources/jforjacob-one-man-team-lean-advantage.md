I can't comprehend how lucky we are to be living in this current moment

There has never been a time where it's so easy for a one man team to genuinely compete with & disrupt big players

it's unfathomable how far you can get with an internet connection, AI & some freelancers

Anything you want can be done at lightning speed and to high standards

Most big brands are bloated, paying too much for everything, move slow & operate on pathetic margins surviving on the hope of being bought one day. The thought of profit is an abstract one

Yet the majority of people are looking to these brands to try and model themselves on

Personally, I'm far more interested in talking to the 19 year old kids doing $50k a day from their parents house with no employees.

I genuinely think if most people running these type of legacy brands knew what was possible they'd jump ship. Anytime I've talked to these people they are always mind blown when they are shown behind the curtain

An unfair advantage has been created for those that can leverage it. Stay lean, stay profitable and stop romanticising the idea of building a big, bloated company. The opportunity lies in doing the opposite
