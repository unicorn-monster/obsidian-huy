quiz funnels on google cold traffic convert 20 to 40% higher than advertorials for products above £100 AOV

and the reason has nothing to do with the quiz itself. this is a psychological hack most ecom brands have never thought about. let me connect the dots

casinos let you pick your own seat at the blackjack table for a reason. logically, seat choice makes zero difference, and the cards are the same regardless of where you sit

psychologically, the act of choosing creates a sense of control. and a sense of control creates commitment

quiz funnels weaponise the identical mechanism. the buyer picks their answers, they chose their path, and they invested their time and attention into 4 to 6 questions about their specific situation

by the time the result page appears, they have TOLD you what they need. the recommendation reads as a diagnosis they participated in, never as a pitch. even when 80% of quiz takers end up seeing the same product recommendation

the illusion of personalisation runs deeper than real personalisation. the product does not need to be different, because the buyer FEELS like the process respected their individuality

this is cialdini's commitment and consistency principle weaponised for ecom. commitment: every quiz answer is a small action, and answering 4 to 6 questions creates psychological investment. walking away means abandoning something they started, so the sunk-cost bias kicks in

consistency: humans have a deep compulsion to act consistently with their previous statements. someone who answered "yes, i struggle with back pain" and "yes, i tried three products with no results" has a brain expecting the next action to align with those answers. viewing the product recommendation feels like the logical next step

the quiz pre-qualifies the buyer in their OWN words. they have told you what they need, and the result page shows them a product matched to what they said. no pitch feel, only diagnosis feel

and the data from the quiz feeds back into targeting. you learn which pain points resonate, which segments convert, and which product variants match which buyer type

after 30 days of running a quiz funnel you have data no other source gives you. which pain points resonate most (completion rate per branch), which ICP segments convert highest (purchase rate per result page), and which ad copy drives the highest-quality quiz takers (qualified completion rate per campaign)

the data feeds back into your google ads copy, your shopping titles, your remarketing segments, and your meta creative

the quiz functions as an intelligence engine more than a conversion tool. every campaign in the account gets sharper over time

and the higher the AOV, the more the quiz outperforms. a £30 supplement gets bought on impulse after reading an advertorial. a £300 device needs the buyer to feel like the decision was THEIRS, and the quiz creates the feeling in 60 seconds
