A creative strategist is basically the brain & manager behind your ads.

They’re responsible for:

- Using customer research to figure out what angles, formats, & messaging will convert

- Analyzing winning ads to create iterations that scale further

- Running creative meetings and managing timelines

- Allocating resources (budget, team, tools)

- Writing scripts

- Managing designers and video editors

- Writing ad copy

Basically, they’re the person who makes sure you’re not just throwing random shit at the wall and that you put our new creatives consistently.

A good cs is worth their weight in gold.
