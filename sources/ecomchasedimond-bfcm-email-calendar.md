Black Friday is 6 weeks out. If your email calendar isn't locked yet, this will save you a weekend.

I turned the way I plan BFCM emails into a free bot template.

It asks for your store, your offer, your sale dates, and last year's numbers if you have them. Then it gives you:

1/ a full calendar from warm-up to your holiday shipping cutoff
2/ who gets each send and who to leave out
3/ the offer angle for each email
4/ subject lines and preview text for every send
5/ what to change in your automated emails during the sale
6/ a deliverability checklist before you ramp up volume

My agency helped 250+ ecommerce brands generate $200M. This is built on that BFCM playbook.

The two things it'll push back on most:

- mailing your whole list on Black Friday
- sending urgency emails to people who already bought

Share last year's best and worst sends, and the plan gets a lot sharper.
