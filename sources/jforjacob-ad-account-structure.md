I'm seeing so many people down bad on Meta right now

We are spending more than ever at better CPA's than ever and have not been effected by any recent issues

Sharing what we do in our ad accounts, literally just as an observation and maybe why we haven't been affected

Of course creative and post click experience is far more important than ad account structure but just find it interesting we have had no issues lately

ABO testing campaign: All markets combined. All new concepts go in here starting at $100 a day. Losers get cut early and will often look at leading indicators to do so (CPATC etc). Winners will continue to be scaled here as well as duplicated into scaling campaigns

CBO scaling campaigns: Lowest cost bidding. Campaign per market. Winners from testing stacked in here. Will create a new ad set each month. Eg "January winners" "February winners"

Graveyard campaign: Bid cap. All markets combined. All losers from testing and past winners that are no longer spending go in here. Self explanatory

Attribution for all campaigns is 7DC 1DEV

Exclusions for all campaigns are synced Klaviyo list of existing customers and website purchasers 180 days
