title: Search ads capture demand created by Meta ads
---

70% of online purchasers used search at some point in their buying journey

which means you can spend all day generating demand on tiktok, meta, wherever you want... and a massive chunk of your buyers will STILL walk through google before they pay anyone

google and nielsen ran the clickstream research on actual purchase journeys. not surveys, not vibes. real browsing behaviour from real buyers

7 out of 10 people who bought something online touched search on the way there

and buyers were 17% more likely to click a sponsored search result than non-buyers

read that again. the people MOST likely to click your search ad are the ones with their wallet already half open

this is the part meta-first founders keep missing

your meta ad does its job. it plants the product in someone's head. but almost nobody buys off the first impression, because that's not how humans spend money

they think about it. they talk themselves in and out of it. and then, days later, they type it into google to make the final call

that google search IS the purchase moment. everything before it was foreplay

so when you run meta with zero google presence, you're doing the hardest part of the sale (creating desire from nothing) and then leaving the room right before the close

whoever shows up on that SERP inherits your work. a competitor. amazon. a review site with affiliate links to someone else's store

the demand you PAID to create, captured by whoever bothered to be present

and the fix costs a fraction of what created the demand. branded search runs on pennies. shopping listings cost nothing to exist. the buyer is already sold, you're removing the last step

your ad account doesn't have a demand problem. it has a presence problem at the exact moment demand becomes revenue
