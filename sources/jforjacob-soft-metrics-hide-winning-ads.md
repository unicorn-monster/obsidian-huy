The idea that you should judge an ad purely based on its CPA / ROAS or spend is ridiculous

I could take any "winning" ad and make it a "looser" instantly by changing the offer to something shit or sending to a bad landing page

Did that winning ad all of a sudden become bad? No, absolutely not

For those that don't look at the soft metrics on your ads you are missing so many opportunities it's crazy

The reality is hook rate, hold rate, average watch time, CPC matter a lot if you know what to do with that data. That's what actually indicates a piece of content is good

You likely have many great ads in your account that you deemed as a bad ad because the CPA wasn't at target etc

When in reality all it might need is a different landing page / funnel. If people are engaging with the content but not converting at your desired rate, it's your job to figure out the post click experience
