The difference in percent of new visitors that come from your ads has nothing to do with your campaign type and ultimately just comes down to spend

Seen so many people bash ASC since it's release as they say 'it just re-targets' something I know first hand to be completely false

Set up a column in your triple whale attribution dashboard for 'new visitor percentage' Take any 2 campaign types, put the same ads in them and target the same location. Set one at double the budget of the other. You'll see a huge difference in % of new visitors between the two, with the higher spending campaign getting far more new visitors

Then swap the budgets of the campaigns and you will see the % of new visitors flip as well

I think why so many people said ASC was just doing a lot of re-targeting was because they were going with the Meta guideline of '30% of your budget should go to ASC' when it was first released

But it doesn't matter what campaign type you use, if you set 30% of your budget to one campaign and 70% to another, the one with 30% will always have significantly less new visitors

This is also why account consolidation is so important imo. If you have a bunch of different campaigns, targeting the same markets, selling the same product it just muddies the waters of attribution even further. Makes reading data so much harder as you'll be dealing with a lot of cross-over, different % of new visitors between the campaigns, shit ton of false signals from last click attribution etc

Just keep it simple
