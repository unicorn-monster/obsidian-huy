From my experience, those that come from a fashion dropshipping background are at a disadvantage when trying to build an actual brand

They are full of bad habits, read data incorrectly and have no patience. I've helped out quite a few that come from this background and I often get messages like: "My ads spent $15 and CPC is above $1 so I killed it"

Sorry, but who tf is teaching this kind of stuff? If I had went by those kind of rules I wouldn't have a single brand or any success in e-commerce

These kind of rules give no weight to your product price, the market you're in, the quality of your audience etc, not that I think you should really care much at all about your CPC

If you want cheap CPC's go run ads in India, see how that turns out
