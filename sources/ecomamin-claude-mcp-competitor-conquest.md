i use opus 5 connected to the google ads MCP to reverse-engineer my competitors' ENTIRE weakness profile

and then turn those weaknesses into ad copy, comparison pages, and conquesting campaigns. let me show you how

the google ads MCP connects opus 5 directly to your live google ads account data, reading your campaigns, your search terms, and your auction insights in real time

here is the prompt:

"pull my auction insights data for the last 30 days. identify the top 5 competitors by impression share in my non-branded campaigns. then for each of the top 3, research: their homepage positioning and primary offer, their active google ads from the transparency center, and their review sentiment on amazon and trustpilot. identify the gap between their product promise and actual delivery. compile into a conquest weakness file with specific attack angles"

opus 5 reads your live auction data, identifies who is stealing your impression share, then goes outside the account and researches their entire business

pulls their homepage claims, their ad copy, and their review sentiment

here is where the process gets surgical: every competitor has reviews revealing unmet buyer expectations, and this prompt finds them

for a supplement brand, the competitor teardown identified the top competitor had 4.2 stars on amazon but 73% of their 1-star reviews mentioned the same issue: the capsules were too large to swallow comfortably

the competitor's product page never addressed the size problem. their ads never mentioned capsule size

so we led with the angle

our ad copy: "the smallest capsule in the category"

our advertorial hook: "if you have ever gagged on a horse pill supplement, you understand why capsule size matters more than dosage"

our comparison page made capsule size one of the three evaluation criteria

the angle did not come from brainstorming. the angle came from the competitor's OWN review data. their customers told the internet what was wrong, opus 5 extracted the pattern, and we turned the pattern into our headline

this is the genghis khan principle applied to digital marketing. he did not conquer by having the biggest army, he conquered by studying his enemies' weaknesses before the battle started, knowing which flank was poorly defended and which supply lines were vulnerable

the conquest weakness file is the reconnaissance. the ad copy is the battle plan

and opus 5 with MCP access compiles the entire thing from live account data and external research in one session

the old way: 3 to 4 hours of manual research per competitor, reading through 100s of reviews and cross-referencing forum threads

the new way: one prompt, 30 minutes, and the AI catches patterns a human researcher would miss because the model cross-references 100s of reviews simultaneously

every competitor has a flaw their customers are publicly complaining about. the question is whether you find their flaw first or your competitor finds yours first
