This is how you can use Muse if you're in Europe, the UK or anywhere else where it's not available.

And not only in your browser, but also in the mobile app.

First you need to make a muse account.

To do this turn on a US vpn, go to https://t.co/hjVU4NMt0C and sign up.

Now you can already start using it in your browser if you want.

Just make sure to turn off your vpn before you start using it, otherwise your account gets flagged and you'll be locked out.

Next up, the mobile app.

This is how it works on iPhone:

First you need to switch your app store country to the U.S.

1. Go to your iPhone setting
2. Go to media & purchases
3. Tap country/region and select the United States
4. Fill in a random US address and phone number (they won't send a 2fa, just make sure it's a real number tho)
5. Accept and you're in the US app store.

Now just go to the App Store and you'll be able to download the muse app.

Don't open the app right away after downloading.

Enable your US vpn again and only then open muse and log in (I wasn't able to log in without the vpn)

Once you're logged in, don't start using muse right away.

Go back and disable your vpn first.

Now you can go back to Muse and start using it.

No idea how it works on Android btw (and I never will)
