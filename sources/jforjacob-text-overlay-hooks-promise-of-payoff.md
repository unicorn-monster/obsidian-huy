Excellent use of text overlay hook

This is another thing I see people do wrong. The reason this is so good is that it implies something is about to go down

If this clip didn't have that text overlay you have no incentive to keep watching. I have zero interest in go-karts or racing and no way in hell I would have stopped to watch that video, never mind all of it without that text at the start

Without that, there is no promise of a payoff, no incentive to stick around. Video was around a minute long and I watched all of it, literally just to see why "he should get banned"

Probably could have had me for 2-3 mins tbh. You can dramatically increase the thumb stop and retention on your videos with a well thought out text overlay like this

Understanding the promise of a payoff is the key to get people to actually watch your ads

And yes, you should be testing multiple hooks per video. Despite the recent dogma to make every asset substantially different. This is some of the worst advice I've seen in DTC
