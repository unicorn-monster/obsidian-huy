google ads are dead

google ads funnels killed it

let me enlighten you on the new google ads meta:

i'll start with an example: someone searches "why do i wake up at 3am" on google. they land on your product page for a magnesium supplement. price, features, buy now button

they leave in 3 seconds

it's not that your product sucks, but that the landing experience skipped 3 psychological steps the buyer hasn't taken yet

the awareness ladder has 5 stages:

unaware, problem-aware, solution-aware, product-aware, most-aware

a PDP works on the last two ONLY. the buyer already knows you, already trusts the category, already has their card out

but 80% of google traffic is problem-aware and solution-aware.

they know the problem. they know solutions exist. they don't know you. they don't trust you. and they're not buying from a brand they discovered 3 seconds ago

sending solution-aware traffic to a PDP is walking up to someone at a party and immediately asking them to sign a contract

a google ads advertorial funnel fixes this with 4 psychological steps:

first it validates the reader's struggle so precisely their brain says "this person understands me." that's rapport. not brand trust, personal trust

then it explains the hidden cause behind the problem. something new. the reader is now cognitively invested. leaving means leaving with an incomplete understanding

then it introduces the mechanism, not the product. it explains how the problem gets fixed at a biological or structural level. the trust transfers from the author to the logic

only then does the product appear. not as a brand asking for money. as the best implementation of a mechanism the reader already believes in

the reader doesn't trust the brand. they trust the logic. and the brand becomes the vehicle

this is why we took a brand from $57k in spend to $1.21M in revenue at 21x ROAS. same keywords any agency would target. the difference was the landing page matched the buyer's psychological position

and google ads quiz funnels take this even deeper

each question is a micro-commitment. by the time someone answers 4-6 questions about their specific situation, they're invested. walking away feels like abandoning something they started

the result page shows a recommendation matched to their own answers. it doesn't feel like a pitch. it feels like a diagnosis

brands running quiz funnels on google cold traffic are seeing 20-40% higher conversion rates than straight advertorials for products above $100 AOV

the higher the price, the more the buyer needs to feel like the decision was theirs. the quiz creates that feeling

your ads aren't the problem.

the psychology of the landing experience is
