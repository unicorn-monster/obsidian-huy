What do you think is easier:

Learning to juggle starting with 8 balls or starting with 2 balls and working your way up to 8 after your master the foundation?

Of course it's easier with 2, then 3, then 4 etc

If you try starting with 8 there is a good chance you will never learn to juggle at all as you won't be able to get any foundations in place as it's too overwhelming and you won't be able to make any progress

It's the exact same thing when it comes to creative systems

You need to understand and master quality first then buildup to volume

Starting with volume is fucking retarded

If you start with volume, you've got too much going on and it becomes really difficult to learn quality when you've got all those balls you're trying to juggle

Start with quality, learn the foundations, build your creative systems around that and then scale the volume.

You need to build your house upon the rock, not the sand

If there is a single piece of advice you could take away from all my tweets it would be the above

As someone who has been personally involved directly in the launch of more brands than I'd say 99.9% of people in Ecom, with a success rate far above average I can promise you this is the best approach
