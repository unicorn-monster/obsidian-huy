title: 90-second incognito audit reveals your Google growth ceiling

open an incognito window right now and search your own brand name

i'll wait

what you find in the next 90 seconds tells you more about your google setup than any dashboard will

start with your brand name. exact spelling. then the common misspelling. then "your brand reviews." then "your brand vs" and see what google autocompletes

are you the first result? or is there a competitor ad sitting above your own listing, bidding on the name you spent six figures building?

now search your product category. not your brand. the thing you sell. "minimalist leather wallet." "magnesium for sleep." "cooling dog bed"

scroll the shopping row. are you in it? or are four competitors sitting there while you're invisible?

next one hurts more

search the PROBLEM your product solves. "why do i wake up at 3am." "how to stop my dog overheating." whatever the buyer types before they know your category exists

that search has volume. real volume. and it's the cheapest cold traffic in ecom because almost nobody bids on problem queries

now do the exact same four searches for your top competitor

compare the two lists side by side

most brand owners doing this exercise for the first time find the same three things

they own their brand name but nothing else. their competitor owns the category terms. and neither of them shows up on the problem queries, which means that entire pool is sitting unclaimed

that gap between "searches happening in my category" and "searches i appear for" is your actual growth ceiling. not your budget. not your creative

and you found it in 90 seconds with a browser window

screenshot what you find. that's your roadmap for the next 30 days
