claude fable 5.1 dropped and the coding jump IMMIDIATELY changes what a KILLER ecom funnel costs to build

before this you'd use AI to write the copy and wait 3+ weeks on a dev to ship a simple asf landing page

now it reasons through the funnel logic AND writes a deployable page in the same session

so here's every prompt we use to build a full google ads funnel stack: advertorial, quiz, comparison page, long-form PDP

steal all of it

FIRST, THE BRAND BRAIN

everything downstream is only as good as what you load here. open a project and feed it product pages, best sellers, every review you can export, objection docs, competitor URLs, your winning meta creatives, search terms reports, and your current landing pages

then:

"act as the funnel architect for this brand. read everything uploaded and build a working brand profile: ICP segments, the pain points in the customer's own words, buying triggers, objections in order of how often they appear, the proof assets we already have, and where the current funnel leaks. do not recommend anything yet. summarise what you found and tell me what context is missing"

that last line matters most. it stops the model guessing and hands you a list of what to go find

THEN THE AWARENESS MAP

this decides which funnel you build. overlook it and you'll build the wrong page for the right keyword

"pull our search terms report. sort every query into problem-aware, solution-aware, product-aware, and brand-aware. for each cluster: the emotional state behind the search, what the buyer already tried, what they need to believe before purchase, and which funnel type converts them best. flag any cluster we're currently sending to a product page"

that flag at the end is where the money is hiding in nearly every account i audit

BUILD 1: THE ADVERTORIAL

traffic: problem-aware symptom queries and cold youtube viewers

fed by: TOF/MOF search on complaint-style queries, demand gen with 9:16 shorts

"write a long-form advertorial for [PRODUCT] targeting people searching [SYMPTOM QUERY]. structure: open by describing the symptom so precisely the reader thinks we've been watching them, using their exact language from the research. then name 3 solutions they've already tried and why each one failed. then the hidden cause nobody told them about. then the mechanism that fixes it at a structural level. only then introduce the product as the implementation of that mechanism. close with proof and a soft CTA. editorial tone throughout, zero brand voice until the product appears"

the reader never trusts your brand, they trust the mechanism, and your brand is the vehicle

BUILD 2: THE QUIZ FUNNEL

traffic: solution-aware buyers on anything above ~$100 AOV or highly personal products

fed by: MOF search on "best [category] for [situation]" queries, demand gen

"build a 5-question quiz funnel for [PRODUCT LINE]. each question should feel diagnostic, not like data collection. question order must escalate commitment. map every answer combination to a recommended product and a result page that explains WHY this recommendation fits the answers they gave. write the result page so the buyer feels like they arrived at the conclusion themselves. then code the whole thing as a single working page with the logic built in"

every answer is a micro-commitment. by the result page they're confirming their own reasoning instead of evaluating your pitch

BUILD 3: THE COMPARISON PAGE

traffic: product-aware buyers googling your competitors

fed by: competitor conquesting search, "[competitor] alternative" and "[competitor] vs" queries

"build a comparison page for [OUR PRODUCT] vs [COMPETITOR]. research both from real reviews and their own site. set 7 evaluation criteria where our genuine strengths carry the most weight. let the competitor win 1 or 2 criteria honestly. write it in third-party editorial voice. handle the 3 objections a buyer would have about switching. code it as a responsive comparison table page"

the moment a page reads like you win everything, the buyer's guard goes up and they bounce. losing 1 or 2 is what makes the rest believable

BUILD 4: THE LONG-FORM PDP

traffic: brand-aware and bottom-funnel

fed by: branded search, branded shopping, display and youtube remarketing

"rewrite our PDP for [PRODUCT] as a long-form sales page. sequence: hero matching what a brand-aware searcher expects, proof bar, the transformation before the spec sheet, mechanism, ingredient or material breakdown framed as reasons to believe, review wall organised by objection, comparison against the 2 alternatives they're considering, FAQ built from real support tickets, risk reversal, then the offer. code it as a shopify-ready section"

your PDP is the only page in this stack that gets warm traffic. treat the length as a trust asset instead of friction

THEN OPTIMISE

"take the last 14 days of landing page data and cross-reference it against the query clusters feeding each page. tell me which pages are receiving traffic they were not built for, then rewrite the section where each page is losing them"

your ads are doing their job and sending 4 different types of buyer to 1 page built for 1 of them

your competitors are building their 2nd funnel page right now
