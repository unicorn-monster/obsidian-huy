We took a ecom brand from $0 to $4.65M on google ads in 8 months.

The account had zero google data. Spend went up 82x from feb to aug. ROAS never went under 4 in a single month.

So I wrote down EVERY move, in the order we made it:

The day 1 split

Branded and non-branded kept apart from the start. Branded ran at 16.2 ROAS vs 3.99 for non-branded, so mixing them hides everything.

YouTube in february, not "later"

The test, candidate, scale rotation every new video goes through before it touches real budget.

The shopping title rewrite

Keywords first, brand last, straight from the search terms report. Roughly doubled ROAS and the test hit 18x.

Why the winner moved from pmax to standard shopping

Spend was leaking into display. After the move it held the #1 shopping impression share against 53 other advertisers.

Demand gen with plain static images

One image prospecting campaign did $165.6K at 3.02 ROAS in 30 days.

The "super scaler" campaigns

Every winning video gets its own campaign and budget. Spend went $81.6K to $250K in one month and conversion value went $590K to $1.15M.

The month by month numbers

jan $1.77K → $7.33K. mar $19.9K → $198K. jun $81.6K → $590K. aug $411K → $1.82M.

The 10-point checklist to run it on your own account.
