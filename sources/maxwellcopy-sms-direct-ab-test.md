Another AB test proving SMS is all about being direct

The results:

Variation 1: 6.4% clicks and $45.7k

"SMS FLASH SALE. Today only, claim your exclusive offer:"

Variation 2: 3.9% clicks and $32,700

"You did the browsing. We did the saving. Your exclusive offer is waiting… act now before it expires:"

Nearly 65% more clicks and 50% more revenue from the direct version on a browse abandonment SMS.

There was no extra discount on it… traffic led back to regular PDP page with an always available markdown.

Takes notes!
