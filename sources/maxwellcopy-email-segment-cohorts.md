title: Split engaged list into 5 buyer cohorts
---

Easy win on your campaign reporting is splitting your engaged segment into cohorts.

Let's say you normally send to one giant 160 day engaged list...

Instead of selecting that as one segment, break it into:

> Engaged non-buyers
> Engaged one-time buyers
> Engaged repeat buyers
> Engaged active subscribers
> Engaged churned subscribers.

With the same campaign, you can see exactly which group opened, clicked, and bought in Klaviyo's audience breakdown.

Maybe your churned subscribers went crazy for a specific offer...

Maybe your non-buyers ignored an educational email that your repeat buyers crushed....

That's information you'd never get from one blended number from an engaged group.

And after a month of doing this you know which concepts land with which cohort, and which tells you what to build your actual segmentation and offer strategy around.
