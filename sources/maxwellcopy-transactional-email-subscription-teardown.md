To win as a subscription brand in 2027, you'll need to customize your transactional emails.

Many brands treat their transactional emails (order confirmation, shipped, delivered) as plain Shopify receipts...

That's why I subscribed to Grüns, Create, and Everyday Dose to see how the top subscription brands handle them, and all three use that real estate to educate customers brand and get them bought in.

Here's what that looks like:

1. Create, on the order confirmation

- opens with "Welcome to Create" so it reads like a brand welcome instead of a receipt
- shows a Week 1 / Week 2-3 / Week 4-6 timeline to set 90 day expectations immediately
- includes a full section on how creatine works in the body to build belief before the box even ships
- stacks testimonials to reinforce the purchase and kill buyer's remorse

2. Grüns, on the order shipped

- puts the Track Your Order button front and center, then again further down
- leaves the amount charged off the email completely, because reminding someone what they spent gives them a reason to rethink about it
- shows a "Spoilers" section full of customer reviews that turns the shipping wait into brand building time
- backs it all up with 4.8 stars, 1M+ members and lab testing so nobody second guesses the order

3. Everyday Dose, on the order delivered

- frames the delivery as "Better Starts Now" so it's the start of the journey instead of the end of the purchase
- highlights a gamified Dose Boosts program that gives subscribers a reason to engage every morning and unlocks a free Productivity Planner
- habit stacking built right in with Scoop, Froth, Sip so nobody has to guess how to use it
- bakes in an SMS opt-in for daily reminders so they never miss a dose

Transactional emails pull 60%+ open rates...

Nothing else in your program comes close, so take the time to build these out properly.
