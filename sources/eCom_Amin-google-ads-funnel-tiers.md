google ads funnel tier list, D through S, ranked by what they do to cold traffic

i've built every one of these across supplements, pet, jewelry, gadgets and fashion

the gap between D tier and S tier on identical traffic is roughly 4x conversion rate

D TIER: raw product page on cold traffic

the buyer searched a problem, landed on a checkout, and left in 3 seconds. the page assumed trust that was never built. this is still the default in most accounts and it's the most expensive mistake in ecom

C TIER: collection or category page

marginally better because it lets the buyer browse instead of committing. still zero education, zero mechanism, zero reason to believe you over the four other tabs they have open

B TIER: listicle

now we're doing something. "top 5 X for Y" as editorial content, your product at #1 with the deepest writeup. works because it meets the buyer in evaluation mode. include a genuine downside for your own product or the whole thing reads as propaganda

A TIER: comparison page

better because it kills the last objection directly. the buyer is choosing between you and a named alternative, and the page makes the choice for them. wins on conquest traffic specifically. must admit where the competitor beats you or nobody believes the rest

S TIER: advertorial

reads like a magazine article, converts like a sales page. and the reason it sits alone at the top is the mechanism section

here's the principle underneath the whole ranking

cold traffic doesn't distrust your product. it distrusts YOU

the advertorial solves this by never asking for trust in the brand. it builds a logical framework the reader believes in first, explains why everything they've tried failed at a structural level, and only THEN introduces the product as the obvious implementation of logic they already accepted

trust transfers from the reader, to the logic, to the product. the brand is the last thing that matters

everything below S tier is asking the buyer to trust a stranger. S tier asks them to trust an idea

host it off your main domain or the disguise breaks instantly
