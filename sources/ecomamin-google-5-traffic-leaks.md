your competitors are STEALING your traffic as you read this tweet

placing google ads on:

- [your brand name]
- [your product / category]
- [your brand name] alternatives

every meta ad you run is funding their customer acquisition

i audited an account last month where this happened. let me tell you more about it:

the brand was spending $40k/month on meta. great creative. good ROAS. everything looked healthy inside the meta dashboard

their impression share on their own brand name on google? 43%

their top competitor was picking up the other 57%. paying $3k/month on google search ads targeting this brand's name

this brand had ideal buyers see their meta ads. the buyers closed facebook but kept the brand in name as they went through their day. the buyers got curious after an hour or so, opened google, and typed in your brand name to check it again

and the first result is a competitor running a "[brand name] alternative" ad

$3k to intercept $40k worth of demand. that's the arbitrage your competitors are running against you while you're A/B testing thumbnails

and branded search hijacking is only one of the five leaks

the second one is shopping impression share. your meta ad gets someone interested in the category. they google the product type. your shopping listing isn't showing because your titles lead with your brand name instead of the search keyword

google indexes the first three words the hardest. mobile users see four words max before the title gets cut off. if your title reads "[Brand] Premium Collagen 500mg" instead of "Collagen Supplement for Skin + Joint [Brand]," your competitor's product shows up and yours doesn't

we split tested this on one account. keyword-first titles vs brand-first titles. conversion rate doubled. ROAS nearly doubled. from 1 variable

the third leak is comparison page conquesting. your competitors are building dedicated landing pages targeting "[your brand] vs [their brand]" and "[your brand] reviews." they intercept your most informed buyers at the moment of highest intent

fourth is remarketing overlap. the buyer visits your site from meta, doesn't buy, and then your competitor's google display and youtube remarketing hits them across the entire google ecosystem while you're running one meta retargeting touchpoint

fifth is the bing gap. your competitors mirror their google structure on bing where CPCs are 30-50% lower and the competition is nearly zero. the bing audience is older, wealthier, higher household income. and most brands have never logged into the platform

we launched a fresh bing account for a client. zero history. 4 weeks later: $20k in revenue from $2.8k in spend. 7x ROAS

from a channel most brands don't know exists

your meta ads aren't underperforming because of creative fatigue. they're underperforming because you're creating demand on meta and letting your competitors capture it on google

every dollar of meta spend that generates awareness for a buyer who later searches on google is a dollar that funds whoever owns the google real estate for that query

check your auction insights. look at your impression share on your own brand name. see who's running shopping ads for your product category

the leak is there

the question is whether you keep funding your competitors or start capturing your own demand
