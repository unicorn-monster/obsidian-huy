title: How to reply to one-star reviews
---

A one-star review feels personal, so the first reply most owners write is a defense. Three paragraphs on why the customer is wrong.

The person who left it is already gone. The people reading your reply are deciding whether to buy from you next week, and they're reading it to find out what kind of company they'd be dealing with. A long argument tells them more about you than the review did.

So I'd fix the thing first, if it's real, then write two lines:

You were right. It's been fixed.

If it isn't real, the reply is still two lines: stay polite and say what you'd do for them next time.

A quick check: read the last three replies you wrote and ask whether you'd buy from the place that wrote them.
