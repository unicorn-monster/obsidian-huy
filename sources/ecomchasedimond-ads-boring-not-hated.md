title: People hate boring ads not ads themselves
---

Everyone says people hate ads.

So I tried a weird experiment:

A website where the entire point is to look at them.

I had no idea if anyone would care.

So far, 1,778 people have visited.

They've clicked the ads 1,756 times.

Thirty-five companies are now competing on a live leaderboard. Pay $1 more than the leader and your ad becomes the biggest on the page.

I thought a few companies might bid.

I didn't expect the click count to nearly match the visitor count.

Maybe people don't hate ads.

Maybe they hate boring ones.
