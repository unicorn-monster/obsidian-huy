Average 19 year old dropshipper

- tested 100+ products, knows what works and what doesn't

- has built 50+ stores and can create stuff in a day that agencies would charge $50k+ for

- knows breakthrough advertising by heart and can convince your mum to buy anything

- thousands of hours of graphic design and video editing

- handled thousands of customer service tickets personally and knows how to manage expectations and keep refunds low

- Can scale an ad account better than most people who get paid to do it

- Learned how to stay lean, manage cashflow and scale a business without funding

Average 30 year old 'brand founder'

- lent some money

- paid people to do everything

There is a large skill gap in Ecom and I honestly feel if some of these dropshipping kids had access to the same capital / credit the guys in the startup world have they would absolutely wipe the floor with them

Unfortunately most dropshippers never make the leap into really trying to reinvest a lot of their profits into building something for the long term. The ones I know who have are running rings around your favourite Twitter gurus
