A 10/10 marketing email...

If someone joined your list 30 days ago and still hasn't bought, you can hit them with an email with a stupid strong offer.

At that point they've been through your entire welcome flow and a full month of campaigns. None of it worked, and the odds of them converting on their own only drop from here.

So get aggressive instead...

Trigger it off subscribed at least 30 days ago, placed zero orders over all time.

Then give them credit instead of another generic discount.

Can frame it as:

"We deposited $15 into your account"
"You have $17.63 in unused store credit"
"Your account credit expires tonight"
"A $20 digital gift card has been activated"

Nothing you've sent has worked on them... so might as well take a big swing.
