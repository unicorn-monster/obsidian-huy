title: 15% meta impressions leak to Google search

15% of your meta ad impressions turn into google searches for your brand

they don't buy on the ad. they look you up later

and if you're not running google ads right now, sit with the financial version of what happens next

someone sees your product on instagram at 7am, keeps scrolling, doesn't click

three days later the product comes back to them. they open google and type your brand name, or your product category, or the problem you solve

the search happens whether you're there or not

own the result and you close a sale you already paid meta to create, at a fraction of the CPA you paid for the original impression

lose the result and one of three things happens: a competitor bidding on your brand name takes the click, amazon or a marketplace listing intercepts the category search, or the buyer gives up and the impression returns nothing

run the numbers against your own spend

$1,000/day on meta with 15% leaking to google is $150/day walking out the door, which comes to $4,500 a month and $54,000 a year

none of the loss comes from weak creative or weak targeting. you generated the demand and handed the demand to whoever showed up first

and the buyer doesn't care whose ad planted the idea. they buy from whoever appears when they're ready

the fix is a branded search campaign. one afternoon of work. exact and phrase match on your brand name and variations, $20-30/day, CPCs low because almost nobody else bids on your name

then a non-branded campaign on your product category, the second search they run

go pull your auction insights report and look at impression share on your own brand name. see who's showing up next to you

DM me "LEAK" if you want us to plug the hole
