competitors steal 18-42% of your branded clicks when you're not running branded ads

when your ad IS present? they capture 1-5%

this has been measured properly. here's the data on what you're DIRECTLY handing your competitors

a large-scale ads experiment across 1,000s of brands measured exactly what happens on a branded SERP with and without the brand defending it

no brand ad present: competitors intercepted up to 42% of the clicks on YOUR name

brand ad present: interception collapsed to low single digits

a second study went further. keep your ad running but slip to the 2nd paid slot and theft climbs back to 6-15%. 1 position down is all it takes

think about what a branded search actually is

someone typed YOUR brand name into google. your meta ads, your UGC creators, your packaging, your word of mouth... something you paid for or built made this person seek you out specifically

this is the most expensive click in your entire ecosystem to CREATE and the cheapest one to CAPTURE

and up to 2 in 5 of them end up somewhere else, because a competitor showed up on your name and you didn't

"but we rank #1 organically"

the study accounted for this. ads sit above organic. a competitor running "[your brand] alternative" with a discount in the headline intercepts the buyer before your organic listing enters their field of vision.

the click happens above the fold

luckily, the fix for most of this takes roughly 1 hour:

exact match on your brand name. phrase match on brand + modifier. every misspelling you can think of. manual CPC, aggressive enough to hold the top spot, and 95%+ impression share as the line you never drop under

CPCs on your own name run stupidly cheap because your quality score on your own brand is unbeatable

pennies to guarantee the sale you already paid dollars to create
