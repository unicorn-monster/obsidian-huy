This is a subscription upsell EVERY brand should be running.

Frame it as a "$X Refund" which moves them into your subscription offer in one click on the post-purchase page.

Especially important since only 5-10% of one-time buyers ever come back and subscribe on their own... so if you don't catch them here, you mostly never will.

Saves you from chasing them for weeks in your post-purchase flows…

Just a no brainer deal in front of them and you get them while they're still hot.
