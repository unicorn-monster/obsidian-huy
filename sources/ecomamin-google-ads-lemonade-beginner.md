running google ads before you understand how it actually works is a DEATH WISH

ESPECIALLY if all you've ever run is tiktok or meta

so here's the whole thing explained like you're 12. no jargon

say you sell lemonade

tiktok and meta is walking into the park and shouting "LEMONADE" at kids playing football. a few are thirsty. most aren't. you pay for every kid who hears you either way

google is a stand on the busiest street in town, where people walk up and ask "where can i get lemonade?"

you don't have to convince anyone they're thirsty. they already are. you just have to be the stand they see when they ask

meta makes people want it. google catches the ones who already do

google can show your stand in 4 ways:

1. search
someone types "lemonade near me" and sees a few lines of text with your name on it. you pick the words you want to show up for

2. shopping
a picture of your actual cup, the price and your store name, right at the top of google. for an online store this is the big one, because people see the product and the price before they even click

3. pmax
you hand google your lemonade and say "put my signs wherever you think buyers are". search, shopping, youtube, gmail, maps. google decides everything. great once google knows who buys from you. on a brand new stand it's guessing, with your money

4. youtube
the ad before the video. that's interrupting people again, same game as tiktok. useful later, not where you start

so what are you actually paying for?

every time someone asks for lemonade, every stand selling it raises its hand at the same time. that's the auction and it happens in a split second

google doesn't just pick whoever pays the most. it also looks at which stand best matches what the person asked for

and you only pay when someone walks over to your stand (a click). people who see your ad and keep walking cost you nothing

now the part everyone skips: the feed

for shopping you don't write ads. your product list IS the ad. it's basically a spreadsheet of your products (title, price, picture) that google reads from your store through google merchant center (free to set up)

google matches what people type to your product titles

if your sign says "drink #4", the person asking for "fresh pink lemonade, no sugar" never finds you. if it says "fresh pink lemonade, no sugar, large cup", they do

most beginners spend weeks on the ads and five minutes on the titles. should be the other way round

why does it feel slow?

you're a new stand on a busy street. google doesn't know you yet, so the first weeks are google learning which questions bring buyers to you. no viral day. no sales screenshot at 3am on day two

but a tiktok video can blow up on monday and be dead by friday. on google the same people ask for lemonade every single day. once your stand is in the right spot it keeps working

slower to start. way steadier once it's going

what i'd start with if i were you:

shopping first. one campaign, your best few products, titles fixed before you spend a dollar

then leave it alone for a couple of weeks while google learns. no pressing buttons every morning

add search for the exact things people type when they're ready to buy
pmax and youtube once you have sales data, not before

that's google ads

it's not easy. but it's way less complicated than it looks from the outside
