I see a lot of people struggling with understanding angles and avatars for their ads.

Here’s an example that makes it clearer:

Gruns sells greens gummies that help with protein absorption, gut health, weight loss and more.

Let’s take the protein angle.

It offers the same solution: better protein utilization 

But can be targeted to different avatars:

- Gym bros eating high protein to build muscle

- Women focused on glute development

- Boomers wanting to maintain muscle mass and stay active

Each avatar has the same core problem (protein absorption) but different desired outcomes:

- Gym bro: wants to get jacked

- Woman: wants to grow her booty

- Boomer: wants to stay strong and active

Now you can create different ads for each avatar using the same product and same overarching angle.

The messaging changes based on their specific goals, but the solution stays identical.

Gym bro ad: “Stop wasting your protein powder”

Woman ad: “Finally grow the 🍑 you always wanted”

Boomer ad: “Stay strong and independent longer”

Same gummies. 

Same protein benefit.

Three completely different value propositions.

Once you understand this, you’ll never run out of new ads to test.
