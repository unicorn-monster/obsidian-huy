title: Every marketing claim needs a receipt to earn belief
---

Every marketing claim creates a question in the reader's head.

Fast: How fast?
Trusted: By whom?
Easy: What do I have to do?
Better: Compared with what?

Good copy answers the question before the reader has to ask it.

Take a claim I could make:

"We know ecommerce email."

That tells you what I believe without giving you a reason to believe it.

Now add the receipt:

"Our work spans 150+ brands and $200M+ in email-attributable revenue."

The second version gives you something concrete to evaluate.

Try this on your next draft:

1. Highlight every claim.
2. Write the question each claim creates.
3. Add the smallest piece of proof that answers it.

The proof could be a number, customer quote, side-by-side comparison, screenshot, example, or demonstration.

If the proof is missing, revise the claim until you can support it.

A claim can earn attention.

A receipt earns belief.
