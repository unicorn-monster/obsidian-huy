title: Popup optimization 4 quick wins

While we're talking about popups, here are 4 other quick things I'd also do:

1. Turn the popup off for anyone who bought in the last 30 days. A welcome discount for a recent buyer just trains them to wait for a code.

2. Use different popups by page. Homepage for email signup. Product page for restock or size alerts. Cart for free shipping over a minimum. One offer sitewide is usually the wrong offer for most of those pages.

3. If someone lands from an email, don't ask for their email again. Either skip the popup or show something useful like early access or a new drop.

4. Cap how often the same person sees it across days, not just in one visit. Closing it once shouldn't mean they see it again every time they come back that week.
