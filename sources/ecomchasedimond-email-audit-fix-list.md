title: Email audit fix list dies in Google Doc
---

Every email audit I do ends the same way.

A fix list with 10+ items and big plans to make improvements.

I check back in 90 days, and most of the list is still sitting there. Happens at $800K brands and $8M brands alike.

Every one of those fixes is a dozen clicks deep in your ESP. Building segments, cloning flows, making templates, pulling reports... It's a lot of work.

That's how good email plans die in a Google Doc.

Dig out the fix list from your last audit. Pick the item that's been on it longest and build it over the next few days.
