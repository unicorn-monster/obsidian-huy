The amount of people in disbelief about this is crazy, thinking I'm targeting 3rd world countries etc lol I guess you've never ran an ad people actually like to consume and engage with

A really easy way to do this is put deliberate mistakes or controversies in your ads. Like stating a fact that is objectively false or do something wrong, for example if you're making a cup of coffee put the water in first then the coffee. People will love to jump into your comments to correct you / argue with each other or just have a laugh and joke with each other

If your actually thoughtful about your ads it's amazing how much engagement you can drive and thus get ridiculously low CPM's

Here's an ad that just started spending 2 days ago. This is just one placement in one campaign. Across all placements It's racked up around 8,000 likes and around a thousand comments, In two days! Even look at the amount of likes on the top comments

People are watching this video and jumping into the comments to engage, correct each other, argue, make jokes etc

Remember that Meta is a retention and engagement platform. If your ads align with this, you will be rewarded
