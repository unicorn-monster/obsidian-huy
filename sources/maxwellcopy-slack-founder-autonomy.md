Disagree on this one…

In theory yes, that would be great.

But I'd suggest you SHOULDN'T respond fast in Slack for 3 reasons:

1) If notifications are always on and you're replying instantly, when are you doing focused unbothered work? You're always getting interrupted by random shit, and the important but not urgent stuff never gets done. Ex. cracking a new demand channel.

2) Having the founder respond fast trains your team to be reliant on you. I used to respond instantly to everything and my team got into the habit of just messaging me whenever they were unsure instead of working it out themselves. If you don't reply right away, they learn to figure shit out on their own and you end up with a far more autonomous company.

3) Most of what feels urgent just isn't lol. Is the world going to fall apart if you reply in three hours instead of sixty seconds? Almost never, especially once your team can solve things without looping you in. Your focus as the founder is far more important.

I used to make it a priority responding to every slack within 10 minutes.

But I found myself incredibly unproductive, and big needle moving projects that moved the business forward just got stalled.

Now I do not have Slack notifications on.

Where I do agree with this take though is through text…

If someone I respect business-wise texts me, I'm replying instantly when I see the message.
