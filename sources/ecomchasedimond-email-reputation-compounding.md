title: Email reputation compounds over years not campaigns
---

I've been doing email marketing for ~10 years now. IMO, a lot of what looks like an advantage today is momentum from just staying in the game long enough.

For me, the clearest example is becoming known as "the email guy." A lot of business comes to me now because people already know what I do and trust me to do it well.

That reputation took years and years of doing the work, sharing what I learned, and giving it enough time to compound.
