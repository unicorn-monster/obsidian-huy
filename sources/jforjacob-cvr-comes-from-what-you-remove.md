Get DM's all the time about increasing CVR, 90% of the time the person is struggling and says they've tried a million things and added this, that and the other

First, CVR is not what you should be optimising for. Most people could double their CVR just by halving their prices, doesn't mean you'll be more profitable

But one thing I have learned as I've got more experience is having a high CVR usually comes down to the things you don't do, rather than the things you do. Keep things super simple, remove unnecessary steps, offers, text, elements etc. It just adds confusion and unnecessary friction. I see too many sites that are overwhelming, too much going on and it feels like a chore to consume

Give your phone to your Mum or Grandmother, ask them to buy something from your website. Don't intervene and watch as they do it. If they struggle or get stuck anywhere you need to simplify it. Afterward, ask them if they know exactly what they are getting, how long they have to return it, if there was a guarantee, how much they saved and when they can expect it to arrive, again if they can't answer those things you need to make these points clearer
