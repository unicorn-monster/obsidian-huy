title: Word of mouth repeatability test — message clarity
---

Word of mouth starts before someone recommends you.

It starts when they can explain what you do.

A strong message keeps its meaning when one person explains it to another.

Here's the Repeatability Test:

1. Write what your company does in one sentence.
2. Replace internal language and acronyms with words your customers already use.
3. Ask five people in your target market to read it once.
4. After they read it, hide the sentence and ask them to explain it back in their own words.
5. Write down what they remembered, changed, or missed.

For example:

"We help brands grow" could mean almost anything.

"We help ecommerce brands turn more first-time buyers into repeat customers" tells people exactly who you help and what changes.

You're testing three things:

Clarity: Could they explain it without your help?

Memory: Which parts came back consistently?

Transfer: Could they pass the same idea to someone else?

They can use different words and still carry the same core idea.

If each person comes back with a different idea, simplify the sentence and run the test again.

If people can repeat it, they can spread it.
