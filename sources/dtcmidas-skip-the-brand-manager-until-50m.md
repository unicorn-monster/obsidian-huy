Brand manager is the most overrated position in a DTC team.

You don’t have an actual brand until you’re at least $50M.

Caring about “brand guidelines” and “brand consistency” will hurt your performance when you’re trying to scale.

Scale first, get customers who love your product, then worry about brand.
