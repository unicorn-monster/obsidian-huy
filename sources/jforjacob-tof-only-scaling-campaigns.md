title: TOF-only scaling campaigns rule dropping CPA
---

Stopped adding statics and BOF ads to my scaling campaigns after a convo with @scalesurfer_

We are now spending more than ever right now and CPA's dropping

My new rule:

Scaling campaigns are exclusively for ads with higher first click ROAS than what's reported by platform or triple attribution and higher than account average NVP

All statics and BOF ads stay in ABO testing campaign and scaled there according to triple attribution as well as needing to meet 20% higher ROAS targets or better

---

The reason is obvious in hindsight

Meta optimises off of last click (even though it will sequence ads pretty well according to funnel stage in a CBO)

But you'll always have a higher % of spend in a CBO going to these BOF ads than desired

And it makes scaling budgets less stable

As the campaign will have optimised a decent amount around these BOF ads when you raise spend the campaign overall can't really handle it, as it taps out its audience at a certain point

But if all ads in that CBO are true TOF with higher first click ROAS and high NVP this doesn't really happen

You can fully control spend to TOF instead of Meta sending spend to ads that aren't that incremental

I always was of the school of thought that it was best to have a mixture of funnel stages in a campaign and that would be best in terms of optimisation

Turns out I was completely wrong
