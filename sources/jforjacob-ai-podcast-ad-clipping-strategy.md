Create a 3-5 min ai podcast ad -> take into capcut -> use AI clipping feature -> specify 15-30 second clips -> run the original video in ad account -> take clips and post them on all short form platforms -> find which ones get most reach -> add them to single ad set in ad account

You've just got dozens of variations of your original video, organic exposure, free creative testing and hopefully a couple of banger ads all from a single video that you usually would have just put in your ad account and that's it

Taking the long form to short form clipping strategy into Ecom with minimal effort
