You don't need full localization from day one.

You can scale to multiple countries without localized ads or funnels first.

Yes, there's eventually a plateau where localization becomes necessary to scale further. But you can hit solid numbers before that.

We're now fully localized in 8 countries because in my opinion that's the only way to truly dominate a market.

But to get there, you can use campaigns targeting multiple countries at once to test where there is actual market pull.

Then localize the winners.
