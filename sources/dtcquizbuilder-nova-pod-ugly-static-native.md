"I'm fucking furious writing this."

Nova Pod runs this native static from a persona page, & it prints

No product in sight, just a curiosity photo

The copy reads like a personal confession

A hospital corridor at 2 in the morning in her dressing gown

Her husband collapsed in the garden

And she keeps thinking about the box of tablets on their bathroom shelf

"The one we trusted for eleven years"

The villain isn't dirty dentures

It's the grocery store tablets everyone assumes are safe

The ad turns everyday cleaning tablets into a medical hazard

The click lands on an advertorial disguised as a dental review site

Blue medical header, breadcrumbs, & a prosthodontist byline {nobody questions the white coat}

Headline: "Dentists Exposed: These 5 Denture Cleaning Methods Are NOT Created Equal"

A red subhead links cleaning methods to stroke risk, right above a photo of dentures

On an unsexy product, fighting the default habit is the whole game

I see founders pitch "our cleaner works better" all day

People stick with what they know

Tablets are cheap & familiar

So Nova Pod doesn't sell the device

They make the cheap alternative look DANGEROUS

By the recommendation, dropping dentures into a tablet soak feels reckless
