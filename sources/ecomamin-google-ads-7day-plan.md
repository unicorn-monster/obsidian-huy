if i took over your google ads tomorrow, here's exactly what i'd do in the first 7 days:

day 1: split branded from non-branded
your brand searches make the account look great. i want to see what google is actually bringing in new

day 2: rebuild your top 20 product titles
from the search terms report. keywords first, brand last

day 3: kill everything spending without converting
search terms, placements, products. if it's had 2x your CPA and nothing, it's gone

day 4: fix your merchant center
returns, shipping, support details. google's new checkout inside search needs them and most stores haven't touched it

day 5: launch youtube
one winning meta ad, one campaign. it did 28% of revenue on our $4.65M account

day 6: set the budget controls google just launched
tell pmax where to spend first instead of letting it dump money on display

day 7: read the data and double down on what moved

most agencies take 30 days to "audit"

we're live in 3
