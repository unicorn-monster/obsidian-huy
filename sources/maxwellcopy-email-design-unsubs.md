title: Immersive email design reduces unsubscribes long term
---

Email design is important.

Many brands rock shitty designs or templated emails that look the same every time. The problem with that is unsubscribes. You will constantly be losing subscribers, including valuable customers. Just because you aren't visually appealing them.

Immersive designs like these that look different every time for the user change that.

Unsubs go down, your list looks forward to your email, and valuable customers stay on and buy from your new drops, restocks, etc.

You may not see it right in the moment what strong design does, but in the long term you do feel it.
