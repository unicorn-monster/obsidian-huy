One misconception about AI UGC is that it takes less time which is actually completely false

With regular UGC the waiting time is longer but the actual time input from you is a lot less

With regular UGC once my brief and script is finished, it's one message to the creator and send a payment once finished and we are done

With AI UGC there are a lot more steps involved. Reference images, prompting, re-prompting voiceovers, trying to get exactly what you want in terms of character movement, camera etc

It's like stir frying vs slow cooking

Yes the stir fry will be finished faster but requires a lot of manual input from you

Whereas with slow cooking you just throw it all in a pot and come back whenever it's done

It lets you go about your day and get other things done

The slow cooker is actually a lot more productive as your actual time input is a lot less, which is why busy parents love them

Never see anyone talk about this
