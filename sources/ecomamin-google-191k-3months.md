i made this brand $191K in 3 months with google ads

here's how

june: $0 in the account. no history. no data. nothing for google to learn from

by september it was doing $95K a month, and ROAS never dropped below 3

not because we cranked the budget
because we kept opening new ways in for new buyers

week 1: brand + shopping in 5 countries at once

brand search first. people typing the name in are the cheapest sale you'll ever get, and they warm up a brand new account with real conversions

but branded stays at 20-25% of the budget MAX. you can't scale on people who were buying anyway

shopping is where the money was. 66% of the revenue over the 3 months came through it

mid july: the feed-only campaign

performance max with no images, no videos, no headlines. JUST the product feed, in 2 more countries

that one campaign did $45k on its own

(brand new store with no sales anywhere? start with standard shopping. this brand already sold on meta, so it could skip a step)

august: one product page wasn't enough

everything ran to one page, and that page was trying to talk to every buyer at once

so we built a page for each of them:
- gift buyers
- expecting parents
- the "will it actually work?" crowd
- value shoppers
- a comparison page vs the brand shoppers compare them to

each one with its own ads and its own feed titles

then the part almost nobody does: we tested those pages THROUGH shopping

duplicate the merchant center feed for one angle. new title, new description, new image, pointing at the new page

one advertorial made $7.2k that way. from shopping alone

september: the same loop on repeat
- find an angle buyers already care about
- build a page for it
- write 3-5 feed titles
- test it, keep the winners
- promote the winner into its own feed-only campaign

one rule: duplicate asset groups, not campaigns. a new campaign starts learning from zero

the month by month:

july: $4.5k spent, $19.6k back, 4.35 ROAS
august: $23.6k spent, $75.9k back, 3.22 ROAS
september: $29k spent, $95.4k back, 3.28 ROAS

if you're doing well on meta and you're not on google yet, this is the order to emulate or improve on

most brands raise the budget, watch ROAS fall off a cliff and decide google "doesn't scale" for them

it scales. you just have to keep giving it new buyers to find
