title: "Once you cross $500K/month on Meta with a working product and funnel, start testing new ad channels"
---

Once you have a product and funnel working on Meta, you should start adding new ad channels to scale it even further.

You already have winning creative and proven messaging from Meta.

So adapting these for other channels requires less marginal resources compared to starting from scratch.

You also diversify channel risk.

If one channel underperforms, you have others picking up the slack.

These other channels can be more efficient than Meta, though often less scalable. 

This creates an interesting dynamic.

You can acquire customers cheaper on smaller channels which allows you to spend more at a higher CAC on Meta to scale harder.

The more efficient smaller channels subsidize the less efficient Meta spend that’s needed to scale.

Once you’re cross $500K/month on Meta, start testing new channels.
