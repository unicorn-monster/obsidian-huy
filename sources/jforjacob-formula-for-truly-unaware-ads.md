Cracking a truly unaware ad is very difficult but the formula itself is fairly simple

- Hook people in with an interesting story that's completely unrelated to your product
- tie that story to the problem you are solving
- then either go for call to action and direct to education landing page or:
- continue video bringing people further down the awareness stages

In practice this could look something like:

"In 1946 there was a really interesting study that was carried out on German soldiers after WW2. They looked at 2 groups of people. 1 group went back to regular, happy life seemingly unaffected by the trauma of the war the other group never recovered and spiralled into all sorts of coping mechanisms like drug addiction, alcoholism etc. The thing they discovered that differentiated these groups will amaze you..."

Then you could talk about how one group did a certain thing (supplemented with magnesium or something similar). Then go on to educate on how and why that thing works etc. After that you can talk about how other things don't really work or solve the problem. Then introduce your product

You can then go to a landing page with more education and testimonials etc. Or continue the video and do the same thing

This is very simplified and you would go into more depth in the initial story opening but you get the idea
