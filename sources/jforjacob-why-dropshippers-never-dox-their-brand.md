title: "Dropshippers hide their brand because they have no moat, not because they’re lying"
---

Dropshippers don't dox their brand because they have no moat, not that they are lying or making shit up

The product, angle and creative they are running is everything

If people get a whiff of it you'll have 50 clones the next day and there's nothing you can really do to stop it

They don't have a unique product, they don't have a returning customer base, they don't have distribution, they don't have a brand

They rely on being first order profitable and when they get other people finding out the product and copying it 1 on 1 margin will instantly shrink

I don't think people that have never been in the dropshipping arena in the last few years realise how ruthlessly competitive and difficult it is to be profitable

So when you have something working you need to protect it as much as you can

It's not like back in 2018 when you had $5 CPM's and any old shitty creative would work

But this is why the dropshippers that really do crack it and succeed end up being some of the best marketers and operators in the space

They aren't not doxing their brand because it doesn't exist or whatever reason people think, it's for protection (which anyone with a brain cell can understand)

It's like asking any affiliate who is currently making money with an offer what they are running right now. No chance in hell they are gonna publicly dox, it's just suicide
