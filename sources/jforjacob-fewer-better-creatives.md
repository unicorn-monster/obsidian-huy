It's funny, I've always said we are a low volume of creative brand

Yet we fall into the top 1% of advertisers in terms of active ads, with having more than 1,000 active

Goes to show, if you take your time and make good ads, majority of them will stay running for a long time and compound

Where as the majority of brands chasing volume will publish hundreds of ads at a time and have to kill 99% of them

So ironically, the best way to get volume in your account may be to actually reduce output, make sure everything you test is meaningful and likely they will be able to stay active

For reference, we made 50 creatives last month internally. Again I think the average brand would do a lot more than that yet we end up in the top 1% in terms of active volume

Something to think about
