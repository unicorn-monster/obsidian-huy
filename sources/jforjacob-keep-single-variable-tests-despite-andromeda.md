You should not stop testing or reducing volume of single variable change tests with your ads

Creative diversity comes from new concepts not stopping small variations of those concepts

Hook tests, headline tests etc will always be high leverage and take almost no time to do

If you're making a new concept you may as well make some variations as it's low effort and high leverage, nothing should change for anyone

Stopping small variations will reduce ad volume, throttle learnings and ultimately spend
