One of the biggest problems I've had with AI image generation for ads is spelling mistakes / gibberish on the product label / packaging

I've spent hours trying to find a way to reliably fix this:

- Telling the AI exactly what the text is and how it's spelled
- giving it a separate image of just the label along with the product
- Creating a JSON file for the product stating the text, describing its styling, colour and how it's positioned etc

I've finally found the solution. It works 95% of the time

"make sure there are no spelling mistakes on the label"

I don't know why this works so well over other things I've tried. But it does and I'm not complaining
