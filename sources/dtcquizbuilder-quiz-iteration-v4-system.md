We just beat another 'top performing PDP'

It was on the 4th iteration

So far our winning system is this:

Quiz 1 Launch
Find major weak point

If obvious weakness:
Implement necessary fix (big swing - ie: positioning)
Once major improvement is seen, rebuild quiz accordingly

No obvious weakness? Something is missing in terms of messaging
Revisit winning ads
Analyse answers from customers
Launch v2 split test

It's simple - not easy

If you're spending more than 10k/day and feeling stuck, the most expensive thing you can do is to not test your funnel.
