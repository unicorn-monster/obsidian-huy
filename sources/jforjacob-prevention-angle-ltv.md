title: Prevention angle drives higher LTV than cure
---

Prevention angles tend to have a lot higher LTV than symptom relief or cure angles

If someone is taking your product to help prevent something, the effectiveness of your product is based on them not developing that thing

An example would be preventing early signs of aging

If your product is based around preventing wrinkles forming as easily in the future, and skin becoming dull etc they are not expecting anything they currently have to reverse. They also understand this is a long term play. They are a lot more likely to make this kind of product a part of their daily routine and kind of set it and forget it

Now imagine you've angled your product around removing wrinkles

They'll maybe try it for a couple weeks and if they don't see immediate and undeniable results they are likely to churn. No matter how good your retention strategy is
