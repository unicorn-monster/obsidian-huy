title: Cancel flow survey recovers 15% of churning subscribers

Many people overcomplicate their cancel flows.

Just have a simple survey with a list of reasons why they may be leaving.

Two reasons it works:

1) You can show the right save offer based on what they picked. It's like a doctor asking your symptoms before writing a prescription.

2) The data tells you exactly why people leave, which feeds everything upstream in your funnel. Saves you so much time optimizing things that were never the problem.

A good cancel flow will recover 15% plus of subscribers who are trying to cancel.

Many people try to obsess over keeping people subscribed when they enter this flow...

But if anybody reaches at this point, they're pretty much already lost...

Use this info from your surveys to influence how you use your messaging on the front end.
