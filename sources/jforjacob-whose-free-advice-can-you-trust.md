title: "Most free content is made to sell, not to help"
---

The most annoying thing about all the 'free' content out there is it's always just part of a sales process or to indirectly promote some affiliated tool, course or agency

I've nothing against people making money or promoting the above things but the problem comes when the viewers / followers aren't actually being given good advice, they're just being given the advice that will make the creator the most money or the partner that's paying them the highest commission

Most content is being made primarily with the intention to sell instead of the intention to help

People end up with bloated SAAS bills, overly expensive partners or aren't made aware there are better or cheaper alternatives for things (even if the creator they listened to knows that)

There's some genuinely golden content out there but very hard to sort that or know the difference if you don't have experience

I would say good rule of thumb is:

Never listen to anything promoted on a podcast

Rarely trust any referrals you get from agencies (they're all in bed with each other and SAAS partners)

And be very sceptical of anything you see being promoted by large accounts (especially if co-ordinated with other accounts)

And I get it, being given money to promote something is probably hard to turn down for a lot of people but it just really pains me to see when they take massive advantage of the authority they have, often at the expense of those that need the most help
