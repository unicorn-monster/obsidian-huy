Avatar = WHO you’re selling to

Angle = HOW you sell to them

Example for a collagen powder:

Avatar 1: Women in their 30s concerned about early aging signs

Avatar 2: Women 45+ dealing with visible skin aging

Those are different people at different life stages with different priorities.

Now for Avatar 1, an angle could be:

“Prevent fine lines before they start”

For Avatar 2, a similar angle:

“Reduce fine lines and look like you’re 30 again”

Similar outcome, but framed differently for each avatar’s specific situation
