Tier list for squeezing the most profit out of BFCM:

S TIER
- Cycling through 3-5 different offers instead of running one for three weeks
- Unsuppressing everyone in your list who's ever opened, browsed or bought and sending wide
- Updating your pop-up to feature the actual BFCM offer (different one for early access, BF, Cyber Monday and gifting)
- Using @AliaPopups so you can swap pop-ups per offer period easily (and they will help manage this for you)
- Resending the same emails with a new subject line instead of building new ones

A TIER
- Going heavy on plain text founder emails
- 2+ sends a day during peak, 1 SMS a day on top
- Updating your 5 core flows to carry the BFCM offer with tighter delays
- Making the offer the hero, one big CTA above the fold

B TIER
- Post-purchase push to a second order before gifting season
- Subscription brands giving active subs their own add-on offer so they don't cancel and re-subscribe
- Shipping cutoff flashes in December

C TIER
- Obsessing over design on individual sends
- Protecting or worrying about your open rate in November
