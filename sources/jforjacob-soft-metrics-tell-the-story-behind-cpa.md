Imagine a football team saying they didn't look at the in-game stats

Do you think they just look at the score? Or are they looking at pass completion rate, distance covered, tackles made, shots on target etc?

Do you think any professional team is not looking at these numbers? In any sport?

Yes, the score is what ultimately matters but to think you can't analyse, adapt and improve by looking other data points is ridiculous

"Ok, we lost 2-0 today, what went wrong? how do we improve?" "Uuuh, just score more goals"

As if. They analyse the other metrics, make adjustments, train those specific things and try again next match

It's the same with advertising. Yes, you absolutely should be looking at your hook rate, hold rate, CTR, CPM etc

Yes, CPA / ROAS is what you're optimising for but the other metrics tell the story

Just like a football team can't expect to win any matches with 10% pass completion rate, you can't expect to have a winning ad with a 10% hook rate and 5% hold rate for example

Don't listen to people who say they only look at CPA and nothing else as they are either lying or are unbelievably ignorant
