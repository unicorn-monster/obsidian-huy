Here are the ad iterations I make to milk everything out of a winner ad:

For video ads:

- New hooks - can be as small as changing the text overlay, or as big as recording a completely new spoken and visual hook

- Test new leads

- Reuse the exact same script with different creators 

- Change b-roll (especially when it’s a AI VO + b-roll ad)

- Speed up or slow down the video

- Make shorter or longer versions (cut it down to the core message or extend with more proof or better explanation of mechanism)

- Test different testimonials

For static ads:

I mostly swap headlines and visuals.
Winning visual + new headlines, or winning headline + new visuals.

When it's a "native" looking static with no headline, I experiment with changing the person or main object using Gemini or Higgsfield.

For example: if the winning ad has a young black woman, I'll make variations with an old black woman, young asian woman, white woman, etc…

The key is isolating each variable while keeping everything else constant.

That's how you find out what's actually driving performance.
