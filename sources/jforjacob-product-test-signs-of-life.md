No set answer for this. I'll usually start with at least 10 creatives, budget at around 4x target CPA per day and let it run at least 3 days (ideally over a weekend)

If I'm break-even or profitable, will keep as running and optimise based on the metrics I'm seeing

If I'm unprofitable I'll look for any signs of life in the funnel. High add to carts, high CTR's, High CVR etc and try to make adjustments in other parts of the funnel based on that info. After I make any changes I'll let it run for another day or two to see if there was any effect. If not I'll most likely move on
