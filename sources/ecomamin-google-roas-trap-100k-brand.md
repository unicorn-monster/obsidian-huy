just got off a call with a store doing $100k+ a month

their ads looked like they were crushing it

almost none of it was new customers. google was charging them for people already typing their brand name

here's what we found in 20 minutes:

- branded and non-branded mixed in one campaign
- their pmax was "scaling" on people who already knew them
- 200+ products in one asset group, every margin lumped together
- 31 disapproved products nobody noticed
- shopping titles = just the product name

meta is 90% of their revenue. google isn't growing anything

that's the trap at $30k+. the dashboard looks good and nothing new is coming in

if your store is doing $30k+/mo and google is under 20% of revenue, this is probably you
