Big fan of copy maxxing your hero sections.

Yes, just putting a button in the hero section will get you clicks... but a lot of those people are clicking to click. They haven't been given a reason yet to CLICK TO BUY.

Instead, load the hero with copy that does the actual selling. It might look like a lot at first, but it's worth it. Give the customer everything they need before they hit the CTA.

Long hero variations beat headline plus one line almost every time for us, especially on educational and health products.

Selling them before the CTA >>> selling them after
