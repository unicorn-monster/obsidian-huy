the most friction-heavy moment in your entire funnel is the price reveal

nobody enjoys paying. people enjoy what comes AFTER paying. and the brands printing hardest right now found a way around that exact moment

they turned it into a game. let me explain:

think about every checkout you've abandoned. the product was fine. the moment of pulling out the card is what killed the sale

now look at what the smartest ecom brands do instead

spend $60, unlock free shipping. spend $90, a free gift drops in. spend $120, 15% comes off everything. progress bars filling up in the cart. "you're $14 away from your next reward"

the buyer stops experiencing a payment and starts experiencing a game they're WINNING

same psychology as a good b2b sales call. say "it's 7.5k euro" flat and they start shouting objections before you've finished the sentence. frame it playfully, "it won't be the end of the world, it's 5k," and they're already asking "when do we start?" the price never changed. the FRAME did

and there's a brutal economic reason this matters more than ever:

if you're running paid traffic in any competitive niche, you have to operate as if a first purchase is the ONLY purchase. the retention bros will fight me in the comments, and sure, LTV exists. but 9 out of 10 customers never come back, and pretending otherwise is how brands bleed out slowly

so the first transaction has to carry the weight. AOV is the lever here

and gamified spending thresholds raise AOV without discount-brand energy. a buyer adding a second unit to "unlock" free shipping feels like they beat the system.

bonus effect: the more someone spends with you, the more emotionally attached they become. a $120 first order builds 10x the connection of a $25 one. bigger commitment, bigger trust, bigger chance they come back anyway

audit your cart for a reward ladder today. if there isn't one, you're leaving AOV on the table on EVERY single order
