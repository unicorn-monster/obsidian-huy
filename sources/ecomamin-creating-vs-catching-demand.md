you paid to make them want it. then they googled it and bought from someone ELSE

it's one of the most expensive leaks in ecom, and most founders never see it

here's how it goes:

someone sees your ad on instagram or tiktok
they like it. they don't buy

a few days later they open google
they type what you sell. or your brand name. or "[your product] reviews"

whoever shows up first gets the sale

sometimes that's you

a lot of the time it's a competitor, a reseller, or a big marketplace selling the same thing cheaper

you paid for the whole job of making them want it
someone else got paid for the last step

every ecom business has 2 jobs:

1. CREATING demand
getting people who've never heard of you to want the thing

2. CATCHING demand
being right there when they're ready to pay

most brands doing $30k+ a month put almost everything into job 1
meta, tiktok, influencers, email

and almost nothing into job 2

then the sales don't match the ads, and they decide meta stopped working

it didn't. they just weren't there to collect

the founders who keep growing past this point don't always have the best ads
they cover BOTH jobs

this is where google and youtube come in

google search and shopping are pure job 2
people type exactly what they want, and your product, price and reviews show up right there

youtube does job 1 better than a scroll ever will
a real video gets watched by people who've never heard of you

and a lot of them come back later by searching your name on google

so do this today:

google your product and your brand name like a customer would
look at who shows up above you

that's who's getting paid for your ads
