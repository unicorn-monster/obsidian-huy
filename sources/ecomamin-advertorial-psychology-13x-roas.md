the psychology behind 13x ROAS on google ads funnels

one buyer, one ad, two landing pages. page A produces 1.5x ROAS. page B produces 13x. the gap has nothing to do with traffic quality

the only difference lives in the psychology of what happens after the click

the 1.5x page is a product page with price, features, and a buy now button

the 13x page is an advertorial. editorial content educating the buyer before the product enters the frame. the reason the gap runs this wide comes down to 4 psychological steps 9 out of 10 brands skip entirely

80% of non-branded google traffic is problem-aware or solution-aware. these buyers know the problem, they know solutions exist, they do not know YOU

sending them to a PDP assumes a relationship absent from the equation. you are asking a stranger to trust your brand, evaluate your product, and enter their card number in one page visit from a company they found 3 seconds ago

an advertorial builds the relationship in the right order

first, validation. describe their struggle so precisely their brain registers rapport. "you fall asleep fine, the 3am wake-up is destroying you." the reader thinks "this person GETS me"

second, education. reveal the hidden cause they did not know about. new information creates cognitive investment. leaving now means leaving with an incomplete understanding of their own problem. they stay

third, the mechanism. introduce how the problem gets fixed at a structural level, not the product, the category of solution. "90% of adults are deficient in this mineral and the mineral controls the entire sleep cycle." trust transfers from the author to the logic

fourth, the product. only now does the product appear, not as a brand asking for money, as the best expression of a mechanism the reader already believes in. the product inherits the trust the mechanism built

the reader never trusted the brand. they trusted the logic. the brand became the vehicle

this is the exact sequence behind the $57k spend to $1.21M revenue account at 21x ROAS. same keywords any agency would target

google advertorial funnels print for months without creative refresh. the content is evergreen because intent-based traffic rotates naturally. someone searching "best collagen for joints" for the first time today has never seen your advertorial

build the funnel once. traffic stays fresh. psychology compounds where creative fatigues
