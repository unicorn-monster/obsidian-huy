title: Meta algo sequences full funnel — cover every stage

Yesterday the meta algo reminded me again how strong it actually is.

And also how good your ad funnel needs to be to win.

I clicked on one ad for a hair growth serum.

Now meta floods my feed with the whole category.

First more ads from the same brand, further down the funnel (the algo is really good at sequencing)

Then derma rollers and shampoos.

Then telehealth brands selling finasteride.

Then local hair transplant clinics.

That first click told meta I have intent in that category, so it started serving me every solution in it.

It's very important to understand that you're not only competing with your direct competitors, but with your whole category.

And because it's so good at sequencing, the brand that wins is the one that has an ad live for every part of the funnel.

If you only run top of funnel ads other will scoop up the awareness you paid to create.

Luckily it works the other way around too.

So make sure your full funnel is live in your account at all times.

Middle of funnel:

- statics that remove objections
- ugc creators - showing that other people use it and get results
- comparisons to the other solutions in your category + show why you're better
- social proof and reviews

Bottom of funnel:

Give them a reason to buy NOW, create urgency and fomo, if they get that feeling from another brand because you don't have it in your account, you lose the conversion even though you created the initial intent.
