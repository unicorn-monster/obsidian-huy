if i had to start PRINTING with google from zero, these are the only 9 moves i'd make

people are typing what they want to buy into google right now. most stores just aren't there when they do

after generating $50M+ for ecom brands with google ads, this is the order i'd do it in:

1. sell something people already SEARCH for
check google trends and keyword planner before you spend a cent. if nobody's searching for it, google can't sell it

2. fix merchant center before you spend $1
the price, shipping and returns on your site have to match your feed. one disapproval can stop your bestseller from showing at all

3. track the SALE, not the click
send google every purchase with its real value (google's shopify app or the google tag). if google can't see the money, it can't find you more of it

4. start with shopping, and give your bestsellers their OWN campaign
shopping puts your photo and price right on the search page. bestsellers alone means the budget doesn't get spread across products that never sell

5. read your search terms every week
see what people actually typed. block the junk. put more behind the words that sell

6. don't touch it for 2 weeks
google needs data to learn. changing things every day keeps resetting it

7. scale SLOWLY
raise the budget about 20% at a time on what's profitable. doubling overnight usually breaks it

8. once shopping makes money, add youtube
show your best video ad to people who already visited your store or searched for what you sell

9. send cold youtube viewers to a page that explains the problem first
not straight to a product page with a price on screen 1

the order matters more than any single trick
