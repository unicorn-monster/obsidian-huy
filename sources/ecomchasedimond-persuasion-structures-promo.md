The Best Persuasion Structures for Promo Emails:

(Save this for Black Friday)

- Stack them across one sale: early access to open, proof stack mid-sale, last chance recap to close.

- Tell people why the sale ends. A real reason makes the deadline believable.

- Put the offer in the first line of the email. People skim, so make the deal impossible to miss.

- Resend your best promo to non-openers with a new subject line.
