title: 9-figure brands cost 10x more to serve as agency

I noticed the exact opposite with many nine figure ecom brands…

They have bigger teams
Decisions are much slower
Revisions are much more scrutinizing
Time and effort is 5-10x higher

(Not their fault by the way… as you get bigger there are so many more brand nuances and natural complexity)

For the past couple of years we've only worked with multi 7 and 8 figure brands, and our service was built around that to really crush for them.

But over the past 6-12 months we've gotten a ton of attention from 9 figure brands and we have worked with many of them.

From the agency side, it's not 2x more work to work with them… it's literally 10x more work. And way more complex.

It's a COMPLETELY different service from serving 7 figure and 8 figure brands…

Our systems broke multiple times from it, and I went through a lot of stress figuring it out.

Funny enough, now we're MORE PICKY about working with 9 figure brands now than we are with 7 figure brands.

I've made the mistake multiple times of biting off more than we can chew to work with a big brand.

We have made huge strides and continue to get better…

But the service really does change and you need a completely different team, systems, and scope.
