How to build an ecom business that actually pays you along the way:

1. Get really good at direct response

Understand your customer and learn to make ads, offers and landing pages that sell. If you suck at acquiring customers, you're ngmi

2. Have good margins and know your numbers

Preferably 75%+ after COGS and 60%+ after fulfillment.

Track your unit economics daily.

Know what you can afford to pay for a customer and what's left after all costs.

3. Stay lean

Learn how to implement and use AI across your whole business (creative, ops, supply chain, customer service, email, etc) before throwing more people and expensive agencies at problems.

You can get a shitload done with a small team.

Keep overhead low, both people and software.

4. Have a plan for getting people to buy again

Could be subscriptions, promos, limited editions or new product drops.

Give customers a reason to come back.

And ofc, a product worth buying again and a good experience. No retention flow can replace those.

5. Negotiate every major line item

COGS, shipping, fulfillment, software.

Both prices AND payment terms.

As volume grows, stop accepting the deal you got when you were tiny.

Lower costs and better payment terms free up more cash to take out or reinvest into growth.
