A$3,970 in spend produced A$51,984 in conversion value

three months later, A$30,725 in spend produced A$168,717 in conversion value

10x the spend, 3.2x the revenue, ROAS held above 5x the entire time. here's what we built for this men's fashion brand

the brand was running on meta with decent numbers and zero google presence

we implemented our bread and butter full account build:

BOF layer: branded search protecting every meta-generated query, plus branded shopping capturing buyers who saw the meta ad and went straight to google to check out the product

MOF layer: non-branded search targeting category terms like "men's accessories" and "premium men's fashion," with segmented shopping campaigns running keyword-first titles and margin-based bidding

TOF layer: demand gen with youtube shorts and image ads. adapted the meta UGC to youtube's format and filled the remarketing pool with qualified viewers from a second discovery source

the GMC feed was a massive lever

we restructured every title. keyword-first instead of brand-first, added benefit angles matched to the search terms converting, and set up a supplemental feed with margin data so high-profit SKUs got aggressive bids

same title restructure doubling conversion rate on another account. the feed is the foundation of shopping performance, and 9 out of 10 brands set the feed once and never touch the feed again

the scaling came from the ecosystem, not the budget

in april, A$3,970 produced A$52k at 5.94x ROAS

we didn't throw more money at the same campaigns. we built more campaigns, more layers, more touchpoints

by june, every layer was running: PMAX, search, shopping, demand gen, and youtube

A$30,725 produced A$168,717 at 5.12x ROAS

the ROAS held because each new layer was capturing a different buyer at a different temperature. the campaigns weren't competing with each other, they were feeding each other

youtube shorts viewers entered the remarketing pool, remarketing converted them through display and shopping, branded search protected the demand, and MOF search captured category interest

every layer made every other layer more efficient

the brand went from zero google presence to A$169k a month in 90 days while maintaining 5x+ ROAS at 10x the spend
