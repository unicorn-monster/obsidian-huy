New views and analytics rolled out by @hiroanalytics are sick.

Love this repeat purchase rate by product...

Basically it shows you which products are most likely to bring someone back for a second order.

Which is huge because once you know that, you can push those products more in your welcome flow, your abandonment flows, and everywhere that matters most.

You're acquiring people on the product that's most likely to turn them into a repeat customer instead of just the one with the best margin...

It also shows you the median days between first and second order, so you're not guessing at time delays in post-purchase flows and can set them around when people actually buy again.
