title: Discount change shifts CAC test both directions

The problem is you can't change one number and assume everything else stays the same.

Smaller discount > higher price > higher cac, which can eat into the extra margin.

Of course it doesn't always happen, but you can't assume you'll decrease your discount and keep acquiring customers at the same cost.

It works the other way around too.

A bigger discount/lower price can lower your margin per order, but also lower your cac and expand the pool of people you can sell to. That can let you scale further and make more total profit, even with less margin per order.

That's why you need to test these changes and make sure you're tracking your numbers accurately day by day.
