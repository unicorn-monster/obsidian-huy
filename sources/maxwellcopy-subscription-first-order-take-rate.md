title: Subscription first-order take rate is the biggest lever

The biggest lever you can pull on a subscription brand...

Your first order take rate... which is the percentage of new customers who start on subscription instead of one-time.

Just move that a few points and everything downstream changes with it...

Your LTV goes up, which means you can afford to pay more to acquire a customer, which means you can outspend everyone else in your category.

Problem is most brands either don't track it at all or only see one blended number across the whole store.

Which is why we built it into WellTV...

Now you can see:

- Take rate on first orders specifically
- Filtered by product, so you know which ones actually convert to subscription
- Which means you know exactly where to push harder and which PDPs need work

Usually one or two products carry the whole program and nobody realizes it.
