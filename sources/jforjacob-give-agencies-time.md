This really depends on the agency tbh

Yes there are a lot of agencies out there that will happily take your money and churn you a couple months later

But agencies (good ones) have been key to our growth

I also feel like a lot of people don't give agencies enough time

For example: First month we used @aaronmtrx for creatives, results were decent not amazing

6 months later they have almost all the top spenders in our account

It takes time for agencies to figure out your business, same way it does when you start a new brand or launch a new product
