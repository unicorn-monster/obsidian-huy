I test every ad for 7 days.

Each ad you put out needs to be made with intent. Not just randomly spamming creatives. If you do the latter - yes you’ll waste a lot of money on testing.

If you put in a lot of thought behind every ad you launch, you can even be profitable in your testing phase.
