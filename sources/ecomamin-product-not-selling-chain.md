no one checks the MOST obvious reason their product isn't selling:

and it's almost NEVER the product

it's who saw it, what they were doing when they saw it, and where you sent them after

product, offer, traffic source, intent, page, channel. that's the whole ecosystem. get one of those wrong and the product takes the blame for all of them

take a dog ramp

on tiktok it shows up between a dance video and a cooking clip. nobody was looking for it. you've got 2 seconds to make a stranger care, which is why you end up on a new creative every week

on google someone types "senior dog struggling with stairs"

they already have the problem. they're problem aware, but they don't know a ramp is the answer yet

send that person to a product page with a buy button and a discount and they're gone. they weren't shopping, they were trying to figure out what's wrong with their dog

send them to a page that explains why it happens and what actually helps, and the ramp shows up as the answer instead of another thing to buy

it's the same ramp at the same price. the only thing that changed is where their head was at and the page that met them there

and it keeps going down the chain

someone typing "best dog ramp for couch" is comparing, so they get a comparison page

someone typing your brand name is ready, so they get the product page

and a discount never fixes someone who doesn't understand the problem yet. an explanation does

so before you kill the next product, walk the chain backwards

who saw it, what were they searching for, and did the page match where their head was at?

most of the time the product was fine. it was just in front of the wrong people, at the wrong moment, on the wrong page.
