things nobody tells you when you start ecom:

- your first product probably won't be the one
- most of the work is boring and nobody posts about it
- you can't out-ad a product nobody wants
- the money's in the margin, not the revenue
- improving on a winner beats being original
- you'll learn more from $300 of ad spend than 30 youtube videos
- consistency beats talent every single time
