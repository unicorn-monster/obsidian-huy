Easiest way to lift your opt-in rate before BFCM...

Swap your static form for a gamified one.

Formats like scratch card, mystery gift, and spin to win all beat a plain email field.

It's the same discount you were giving away anyway, but because they played for it, it feels like they won it rather than you handing it out.

Then add a second step to grab the phone number while they're already engaged, since that's where your urgency sends are going during BF week.
