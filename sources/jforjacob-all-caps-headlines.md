FYI capitalising every letter in headlines actually makes them harder to read

People recognise words not just by their letters but also by their shape

When you capitalise a word you remove its shape and it takes longer to process the word

Can't remember where I saw this but I remember people were asked to read words just by their shape (the letters in each word was basically blocked out) and people were able to get the words correct surprisingly when it was lower case, but it was almost impossible if it was upper case

May not seem like a big deal but people mostly skim your page and ads so it does actually matter
