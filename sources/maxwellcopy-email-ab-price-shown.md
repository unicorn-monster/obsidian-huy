title: A/B test showing prices in email lifts sales
---

Here's a random A/B test from the team...

- Top variation: standard campaign with the price sitting under each product
- Bottom variation: exact same email with no prices being shown

50-100% more sales were brought in on the version WITH prices.

Some people just want to know the price before they click... and if they can't see it, they scroll past instead of tapping through to find out.

Can do this for your flows, campaigns, BFCM sends, abandonment, post-purchase emails, etc

Super effective and takes less than 5 minutes to set up.
