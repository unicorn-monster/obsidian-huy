Seeing a lot of people celebrating the news of the potential of section 321 being cut, which could have a huge impact on the likes of Temu and Shein

I think the fact that people are celebrating this is actually more worrying for American brands than it is a relief

These Chinese companies were able to take huge market share despite the huge effort that goes into branding from the Americans. I often hear of people not running sales or discounting heavily to 'protect the brand'

It appears consumers don't give a shit. They just want stuff and they want it cheap. We're living in a disposable and discount economy.

While there may be some disruption in the flow of goods from China to the US that will bring relief to many brands, I'd still be worried.

A lot of these American brands are still overly bloated, paying too much for everything and leaving massive gaps underneath themselves for others to move into.

There are huge strides being made in operating / margin innovation that a lot of these brands aren't aware of and aren't prepared for. Yeah Temu may have to pay some VAT on imports but they're also not paying $85k for a content shoot

Take a moment to get some air sure, but the waves are gonna keep on coming.
