This switching listicle is built around why your old routine failed

I've seen this page structure ripping across DTC accounts

The headline: "5 reasons women are ditching their morning pills"

Cold traffic doesn't click wanting a new habit

They click annoyed at what sits on their counter

So the copy never pitches features first

Each reason on the screen knocks out an alternative that failed:

1. The first reason attacks the old hassle
It names the annoyance {swallowing dry pills that upset your stomach}
Quitting becomes the buyer's own idea

2. Reason two kills the silent objection
"I tried vitamins before and felt nothing"
The copy blames the pill format, not the buyer
It wasn't your fault, the old stuff couldn't absorb

3. Reason three installs the mechanism
A visual showing liquid absorption vs hard tablets
Standard pills aren't even an option anymore

4. Reason four removes the friction
"I don't have time for another routine"
The screen shows one dropper that takes three seconds
Laziness isn't an excuse to bounce

5. Reason five kills risk at the offer
A comparison table showing one bottle replaces five
A refund guarantee right under the button

By the offer, switching feels safer than sticking with what failed
