Get your spend as high as possible as quick as possible. Sacrifice efficiency if you have to

If you've started something new and are getting traction this is so important. Understand you will likely get a bunch of copy cats ripping your stuff. Best way to deal with this in my experience is just bully them out of auctions

Have had countless people rip my new brand 1 on 1 since we launched. Old me would have sent out a bunch of DMCA's and prayed they didn't retaliate

New me realises that much easier to just outbid them. If you've already got the momentum, and get budgets high enough. It's going to be very tough for anyone copying you to compete

Every copy cat I see, I keep an eye on their ad library. None have them have lasted more than a week. They're in the same markets as me, using my creatives, with the same offer and website. The only difference is my budgets are likely 1000X+ of anyone trying to come in and test this product

If I've raised spend enough to the point where I'm deliberately sacrificing margin and efficiency I know I'm pushing at the edge of what's actually possible to achieve with the current creatives and offer. Literally leaving no room for anyone to come in and get any kind of efficient spend next to me

Plus, I can negotiate much better cogs and shipping just through the sheer volume of orders I'm doing. Anyone new will likely be paying 4-5x what I am.

There is literally no way anyone has a chance of coming in and taking market share unless they are willing to run at a loss at high spend for a decent period of time.

Fuck your efficiency in the beginning. Get as much volume and spend as you can so you can start swinging your dick around getting the best deals, the best supplier terms and favour in the auctions. Once you've got higher gross margins that anybody else, a large social following and the copy cats have given up you can start to relax and dial things in

Few understand this
