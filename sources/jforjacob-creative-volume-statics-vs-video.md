Depends on what stage the brand is at. Generally the more the better

Right now for me, it's about 15 new concepts a week on one brand, each with 3-5 variations. This will also vary massively depending on the time of year

I think for anyone starting out or at lower scale 5 concepts a week should do well as long as their is actual intention behind them

Preference for statics or videos completely depends on the product / brand. A jewellery brand for example can do very well and scale with statics. The product doesn't need to be explained or demonstrated, it just needs to look aesthetic. Image in the quickest way to do this

A supplement brand with some strong claims for example needs a completely different approach. Will benefit more from showcasing testimonials, explanations etc with video

I'd say for the majority of people though video will be much more scalable. Even think about how you yourself consume content. Do you find you watch more reels on IG or look at photos? We've all got caught in the doom scroll, simply because short form video is so much more engaging than images

Still good to have a bit of a mix in your account though. Even if video works much better for your brand having a few BOF statics will likely improve overall account efficiency
