PDPs as the one-size-fits-all landing page are lowkey dead

lets say 2 people search "nike air force 1s." one is ready to checkout in 30 seconds. the other is still debating if $120 is worth it for shoes that crease

1 query, 2 completely different buyers, and a single page that can only ever serve one of them...

so you split your traffic by who's arriving, not by what they typed:

branded traffic gets the PDP... with light sales copy woven in

even people typing your exact brand name carry different intents. the skeptic needs a nudge of persuasion on the page. keep it light though. bury the buy button under 2,000 words and the ready buyer bounces to a competitor out of pure annoyance

non-branded search gets an advertorial or listicle

and the counterintuitive rule here: be honest about your weaknesses. say who your product is NOT for. alienate some people ON PURPOSE

a page claiming to help everyone resonates with no one. a page saying "skip this if you're X" earns trust with everyone who isn't

also, ground them in the FULL problem stack before the price appears. someone aware they sleep badly isn't aware bad sleep drives cortisol, low testosterone, low libido. overlook this part and the price reveal gets met with "eh, i'll keep the insomnia"

product-aware non-branded gets a quiz funnel

it beats the advertorial here because it personalises instead of explains. rules: real branching so answers change the result, advanced questions without drop-off length, dopamine on every click, human language, and make it FUN like those facebook IQ quizzes

and collect email + SMS before showing the result. they proved intent by finishing. that list prints later

shopping traffic gets the PDP with a sales angle. VSL embedded or quiz variant if the product needs explanation

demand gen gets the full advertorial. coldest traffic on google. the ad sells the click, the page sells the product. never skip straight to a pitch

high-sophistication solution-aware ("X vs Y" searchers) gets the comparison page. unbiased framing, criteria you chose, honest losses included

match the page to the temperature and the same ad spend multiplies
