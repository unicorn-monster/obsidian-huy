Email/SMS revenue up 59% → store revenue up 27% since we started working with this brand.

We did a cool abandonment flow trick you should try too.

Their abandonment flows were under built for a higher ticket product. Just a couple emails and it showed the same copy for a repeat customer as for someone who'd never bought before, and the same copy whether their cart was $40 or $140…

So we split the flow three ways.

Returning customers got their own version first. These people already trust the brand so they don't need convincing… just the reminder with a simple nudge and nice copy.

Then we split the first time buyers by the free shipping threshold…

If they're already above it, we remind them that they have free shipping. That's sitting at 8.2% placed order rate.

And if they're below free shipping, we tell them how close they are and exactly what gets them there. That one's at 11.1% placed order rate…

This is the kind of thing you only catch and implement when you've built this flow a few hundred times.
