title: Adaptive email frequency floor ceiling per subscriber

The question I get more than any other on strategy calls is: how often should we be emailing?

Every answer I've ever given has been a compromise.

Three a week is too much for some people on your list and nowhere near enough for the ones who'd happily hear from you twice that often.

So you pick a number in the middle and accept that it's wrong for most of them.

We call that a sending strategy.

The fix: set a floor and a ceiling. Minimum one a week, maximum four, whatever you're comfortable with.

Then decide where each subscriber sits inside that range, and what time of day to reach them.

Someone opening everything and browsing twice a week gets four. Someone drifting off gets one.

Same list, same flow, the frequency just stops being one number you applied to everybody.
