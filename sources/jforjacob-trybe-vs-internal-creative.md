Trybe is cool platform, I like it, I use it, we make money with it

But it's a very small percent of our overall spend

We have to run all our Trybe ads in a separate campaign to our regular stuff as the Trybe stuff just does not get spend competing with our internal team

And yes, we train them very well, give higher than normal commissions and have quite a lot of volume coming through Trybe

And this is the thing, if you're using Trybe and it's been what your relying on to scale your brand, your internal creative team is shit

I'm sorry but it's true

I would be eternally embarrassed if random creators were able to come in and outspend our teams ads considering our team does this all day every day and knows everything about our products and audience

It's like being a so called expert at MMA and getting knocked out by a random dude on the street

So yeah, if Tybe has been what's scaled you, you need to take a long hard look at your own creative team and be ashamed
