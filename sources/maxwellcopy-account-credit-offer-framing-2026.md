We changed a brand's sale offer from "$75 OFF" to "$75 Account Credit"…

Literally 2.5x'd the revenue performance on their sale.

Changed NOTHING else besides dollars off vs calling it account credit (same code on the backend lol)

Human psychology is so funny…

Slightly different framing scratches a completely different itch in the brain.
