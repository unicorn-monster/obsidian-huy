The emails I save usually have one thing I want to try.

Sometimes it's the headline. Sometimes it's how they explain the offer or answer a question before I even think to ask it.

Here are 5 of my favorites, with an idea to borrow from each:

1. Surreal makes the referral reward clear for both people.

2. Graza shows how real customers use the product.

3. Who Gives A Crap gives new subscribers a reason to care.

4. Vacation shows buyers how to use the product.

5. Bite makes returning to checkout easy.

Next time you save an email, write down what caught your attention. You'll have something specific to work with when it's time to build your own.
