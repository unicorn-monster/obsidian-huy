title: Real brand vs DR brands — what brand actually means

See too many people justifying their lack of success by saying "I have a real brand though"

First off, you don't. None of us do

Secondly, how do you define a real brand?

Is it through repeat purchase rate? If so, the DR demon supp brands have you beat

Is it LTV? Again, DR demons have you beat

Revenue? DR demons again

Is it pretty images on a website? Everyone can have that with a few clicks

In my mind having a real brand is simply that you are associated with something and that association sits deep in the collective conscious of the consumer market

Again, none of us on here have this. I don't care how cool your brand guide is

Mcdonalds has this, Coca Cola has this, Rolex has this

The people I see coping about 'brand' are almost always just bad at ads and funnels and they need some way to make themselves feel better instead of just facing reality

Skill issue
