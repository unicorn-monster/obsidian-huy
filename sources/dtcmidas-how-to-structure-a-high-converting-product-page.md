Here's how to structure a high converting product page:

1. Announcement Bar: "Back to Work Sale: Buy 1, Get 1 FREE + Free Shipping"

2. Product Media Gallery (Horizontal Funnel):

- HQ Product Image

- Product In Use + Benefits Overlay

- Before & After Transformation

- What's in the Package / Ingredients

- How It Works (3-4 steps)

- Us vs. Them Comparison Chart

- Review Screenshot / Quote

- Lifestyle shots (if suitable)

3. Product Title, Reviews, and Quick Benefits:

- Clear, keyword-rich product title

- 5-star reviews + count (★★★★★ 4.8/5 based on 123 reviews)

- Short description (1-2 lines)

- 3-6 USP bullet points

4. Offer & Purchase Section:

- (Bundle) pricing + strike-through higher price (~30% markup)

- Bold Add to Cart button

- Guarantee + shipping estimation

- Micro-trust signals ("Ships in 24 hours," secure payment icons)

- Optional: Free gift unlock ("Free X when you buy 2+")

5. UGC-Style Product & Testimonial Videos

6. FAQ Section Handle objections: ingredients, usage, sizing, shipping, "will this work for me?"

7. Press Logos / "As Seen In"

8. Desired Outcome Section
- Large lifestyle image
- Outcome-driven headline
- Short benefit text (2-3 lines)

9. Testimonial Section

10. How It Works / Timeline: 3-4 visual steps or realistic results timeline (week 1, week 4, week 8)

11. Us vs. Them

12. Risk Reversal: "X-Day Risk-Free Guarantee" callout

13. Review Section
