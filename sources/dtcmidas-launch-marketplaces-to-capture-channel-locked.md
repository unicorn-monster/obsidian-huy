Some people are very channel dependent eg. only buy from Amazon, or Walmart.

You won’t be able to convert them through your Shopify store. Therefore launching marketplaces is very valuable. 

In the UK we see a very clear correlation between our adspend and our Amazon revenue.

There’s a high chance we wouldn’t be able to make these sales if we hadn’t had Amazon setup.

So you’re missing out if you don’t have other sales channels than Shopify setup.
