most ecom stores running google ads are COOKED

and it's not their ads

it's the settings. here are the 9 i fix in almost every account i open:

- location on "presence or interest". you're paying for people who only searched ABOUT your country

- tax and shipping counted in conversion value. your ROAS is lying to you

- enhanced conversions switched off. google is bidding half blind

- add to cart or page views set as primary conversions. the algo starts optimising for window shoppers

- judging yesterday's ROAS. most ecom accounts have a 2-7 day conversion lag, read 14-30 days

- budgets jumped 50%+ overnight. go 15-20% every few days so bidding doesn't relearn

- one target ROAS for every product. a 70% margin product and a 25% one don't need the same number

- no new customer goal. you're paying full price to "acquire" people who already bought

- reading in-platform ROAS instead of blended MER. every channel takes credit for sales it didn't start

the first 4 take 20 minutes and cost $0
