title: Use customer review words as subject lines
---

Your best subject lines are sitting in your reviews right now.

Customers already tell you why they love your product, in their own words. Those words beat anything a copywriter can make up.

How to do it:

1. Pull your 4- and 5-star reviews
2. Find the line that makes you feel something
3. Use their exact words as the subject line
