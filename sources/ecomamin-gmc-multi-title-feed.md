$38.5k to $70k in revenue in four weeks

health niche. and the single biggest lever wasn't a new campaign type or a bid strategy change. the GMC feed did the heavy lifting.

the brand had one hero product driving the majority of revenue. the previous setup ran the product with a generic title and a single shopping campaign, no segmentation, no angle testing, no feed strategy whatsoever

we ran the hero product through multiple keyword-first titles across supplemental feeds and product variants, each mapped to a different search intent

same product family, different titles, different descriptions, with each feed entry targeting a different buyer psychology (structured through supplemental feeds and item_group_id so everything stays clean with GMC policy)

the buyer searching "natural supplement for joint pain" sees a title leading with "Joint Pain Relief Supplement Natural"

the buyer searching "best anti-inflammatory supplement" sees a title leading with "Anti-Inflammatory Supplement Clinically Tested"

the buyer searching "supplement for knee pain after running" sees a title leading with "Knee Support Supplement for Athletes"

one product, six feed entries, six different buyer psychologies captured through six different keyword-first titles

this is the multi-title feed strategy nobody else runs. and the results speak for themselves

on top of the feed work, we implemented the full account build:

BOF: branded search at 95%+ impression share plus branded shopping

MOF: non-branded search targeting pain-point clusters and category terms. PMAX with ICP-segmented asset groups, each group carrying tailored copy, creatives, and landing pages matched to a specific buyer angle

TOF: demand gen with youtube shorts and image ads. MOF/TOF search campaigns routed to advertorials for problem-aware queries

the combination of the duplicated feed strategy and the full ecosystem build took revenue from $38.5k to $70k in four weeks

the buyer never knows they're looking at the same product. they see a listing matching their exact search intent, the title speaks their language, and the description addresses their specific pain point. click-through rate spikes because the listing feels custom-built for them

99% of brands run one title per product. the brands printing run 3-6 titles per hero product, each targeting a different search intent
