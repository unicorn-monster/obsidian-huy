Bad Order Confirmation vs Good Order Confirmation

Bad:
- Default Shopify receipt with zero branding
- Leads with what they were charged
- Nothing about how to use the product or what happens next

Good:
- Strong branding that makes them feel part of something
- Reaffirms the subscription perks and what they saved
- Educates on how to use it and sets the results timeline
- Founder note and a clear place to get support
