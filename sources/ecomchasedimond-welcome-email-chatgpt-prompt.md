Copy this ChatGPT prompt to fix your welcome emails:

"You just joined my email list for [brand name], a store that sells [what you sell] to [who your customer is]. You haven't bought anything yet.

Read my welcome emails one by one. After each email, tell me:

1. What you think
2. Where you lost interest
3. What would make you buy

Then rewrite each email to fix those problems, starting with the weakest one. Keep my brand voice: [describe it in 3 words, like 'playful, warm, direct'].

Email 1 (sent right after signup):
[paste subject line + body]

Email 2 (sent [X] days later):
[paste subject line + body]

Email 3 (sent [X] days later):
[paste subject line + body]"

How to use it:
1. Fill in the brackets
2. Copy each welcome email from your email platform, subject line included
3. Paste them in order (add more slots if your flow has more emails) and run it

If you just ask AI for feedback, it's way too nice. Make it act like a real shopper and it'll tell you where people lose interest.

Save this for your next flow audit.
