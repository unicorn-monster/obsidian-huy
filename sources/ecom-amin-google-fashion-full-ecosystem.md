"Google ads doesn't work for fashion. It's a demand capture platform. It ONLY scales BOF traffic"

This is the biggest lie in ecom RIGHT NOW and I proved it COMPLETELY WRONG with a men's fashion account.

When it came to us it was running branded search plus a basic shopping campaign. Both converted fine, because the buyer already knew the brand or was browsing product grids with intent. So they tried to scale by pushing more budget, ROAS tanked, and they walked away thinking Google can't generate demand, ONLY capture it.

Their setup was the problem, not the platform.

So we built the top of funnel it NEVER had.

YouTube shorts ads showing the products in real-world context: someone getting ready for a night out, sliding the wallet into the jacket pocket, adjusting the bracelet. No voiceover, brand name ONLY in the final 2 seconds, native to the shorts feed. Demand gen campaigns like that don't convert directly, and they aren't supposed to.

Their job is to fill the remarketing pool that search and shopping close from later.

(These shorts went on to do 2.26 million impressions in 60 days.)

A chunk of those viewers went and searched the brand or the category on Google weeks later, and got captured by the same branded search and shopping campaigns that used to be the whole account.

This is the CRUCIAL part people miss — the ECOSYSTEM is what scales, not any SINGLE campaign.

YouTube builds the awareness, demand gen carries the visual identity, non-branded search picks up the category-aware buyers, shopping picks up the product-aware ones, and branded search closes everyone the rest of it warmed up.

Build it that way and the account scales past $100k a month across fashion, accessories, and apparel. Run Google as branded search plus shopping and you stay stuck at the ceiling this brand was at before we rebuilt it.

Google generates demand if you build the ecosystem for it.

The capture-only setup is why people think it doesn't.
