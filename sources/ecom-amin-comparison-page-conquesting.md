title: Comparison page conquesting competitor search traffic

comparison pages convert competitor search traffic at 2-4x higher rates than generic shopping

and the strategy behind them is the same one napoleon used to win battles he was outnumbered in

someone googles "[competitor brand] reviews" or "[brand A] vs [brand B]"

this buyer has already decided to buy something in your category and they're doing final validation. the intent sits at absolute peak

whoever controls the comparison narrative wins the sale

napoleon's principle: if a battle is coming regardless, choose the ground. control the terrain, control the outcome

a comparison page is choosing the terrain of the buyer's final decision

the anchoring effect does the heavy lifting

when two products sit side by side, the buyer evaluates them relative to each other, and the comparison page sets the anchors

if your advantage is "natural ingredients," the comparison makes natural vs synthetic the primary evaluation frame. the buyer evaluates both products through your lens before forming their own opinion

the editorial disguise makes the page convert

the page reads like a third-party review. 4-5 criteria where you win, 1-2 where the competitor wins

the last part matters most. including criteria where the competitor wins gives the page a fair feel. the moment the buyer senses commercial intent, their ad filter fires and they bounce

the conquesting play makes competitors fund your growth

you build comparison pages targeting your competitors' brand names on google. when someone googles "[competitor brand]," your comparison page appears in the results

the competitor's meta spend created the demand, their brand recognition drove the search, and your comparison page captured the sale

and the founder objection: "won't this send traffic to my competitor?"

the buyer was already going to compare. if you don't provide the comparison, they'll find a third-party review site where you have zero control over positioning

by building the page yourself, you control the criteria, the descriptions, and the framing. your competitor gets a mention and you get the sale

with opus 5 producing comparison page copy in 20 minutes from competitor review data, the production economics work at any scale. one page per major competitor, targeting their brand keywords
