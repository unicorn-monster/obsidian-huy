title: Eternal ad formats vs trend formats longevity
---

If you are in any way involved in the creative process in ecom you need to be doom scroll maxxing

If you aren't and you simply rely on copying competitor ads or solely relying on direct response principles you will simply not be able to compete

You need to know what organic formats are trending

Topic trends across Tiktok and IG

what the latest buzzwords being used are

Trending audios

And in general just have a feel for the culture

The ads that perform the best usually blend seamlessly into the organic feed

And the problem is trends move quick

So that format that was scaling last month is not guaranteed to work as well this month

Being on the edge of this stuff is where the alpha is

By the time people have swiped our new ads and copied them, we've already scaled them and moved on

There of course are formats that last for a long time.

Ad formats that tend to last the longest with paid media are the ones that remain eternal on the organic feed (yappers for example)

The reason that format has always and likely will always work well with paid media is that it has always and will always be a top organic format

So when people come across a paid yapper they don't automatically assume its an ad because such a high percentage of their normal feed looks the same. So there is no yapper = ad association

Other formats like song ads, I'm not so sure will last so long

You don't see this format on the organic feed, so soon people will just associate this format with ads. Which means soon enough as soon as they see it, they will just scroll past

And this is why it's key to be early with these formats, because if they aren't an eternal format like a yapper the window of opportunity will be very small to scale them before they just get associated as ads in the users mind
