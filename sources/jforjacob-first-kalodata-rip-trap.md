Biggest mistake I see from new comers to ecom:

They rip a product from Kalodata, scale it up, make good money, get a false sense of confidence and then go spend it on cars, fancy apartment etc

I have nothing against a kalo rip to get your first bag but please understand you actually know nothing about ecom. That income could be stripped away in an instant and there would be very little you could do about it

The majority are one defenceless DMCA away from going broke as they don't own any of their own assets. All they've really learned to do is copy and paste. Don't know how to make ads, don't know how to build and manage a team, don't know anything about retention etc They don't even understand why the thing they're selling works, just that it was doing good numbers on TT

I'd say most of the young dudes I see flexing on here haven't built a single winning ad from scratch

If this is you, please realise that money you've made should be used to buy your time and freedom for the next year or so. Stay in your parents house or some cheap apartment, work out how you're going to build something legit that isn't relying on another brand paying affiliates for your success
