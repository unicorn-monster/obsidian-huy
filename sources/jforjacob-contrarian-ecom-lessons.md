Things i've learned that go against popular opinion:

- You don't need a high AOV to scale. Often times a lower AOV can open you up to much larger audience and more markets. High margins & low AOV can work extremely well

- ASC has been better than CBO for a long time

- % of new visitors is not lower with ASC. % of new visitors is ultimately decided by the spend ratio's in your ad account

- You should not put most of your focus on US market if you fulfil from China. The main benefit of Chinese fulfilment is seamless worldwide shipping & logistics. If done right, US should make up less than 50% of your revenue

- Your ad account structure does matter, a lot

- You should have a testing campaign

- you should not use flexible ads for launching or testing

- You should not run world-wide campaigns to scale. The breakeven point will be wildly different between countries meaning you'll always be leaving spend on the table in some countries and running margins tighter than desired in others. It's lazy and inefficient. And I don't care how well it's working for some people, they could still be doing better

- You should have all the markets you ship to in your testing campaign. Efficiency is not that important here. What's more important is seeing what works and where. You'll often find creatives that don't work in US work in Europe or vice versa

- Your packaging is not that important

- Brand matters less than price

- Email pop-ups are not high leverage
