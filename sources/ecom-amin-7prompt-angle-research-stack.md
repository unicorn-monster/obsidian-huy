fable 5.1's research capability roughly DOUBLED and the place it hits hardest isn't writing

it's finding KILLER angles before you write anything

it now crawls 20+ sources in a single pass, holds all of it in context at once, and reverse engineers a competitor's entire funnel from their ads down to their offer

so here's the exact 7-prompt research stack we run before touching a single campaign:

1. load the brain before you ask it anything

the research is only as sharp as the context. upload product pages, every review you can export, refund reasons, support tickets, sales call notes, your winning and losing meta creatives, 90 days of search terms, and your top 5 competitor URLs

then:

"act as the angle research lead for this brand. read everything uploaded and build a working profile: ICP segments ranked by revenue, the pain points in the customer's own words, the objections ordered by how often they appear, the proof we already own, and the beliefs a buyer must hold before purchasing. do not propose angles yet. summarise what you found, then tell me what context is MISSING"

that last instruction is the whole game. it stops the model inventing a buyer and tells you what to go collect

2. mine the databases keyword tools never touch

"search reddit threads, quora answers, amazon reviews and Q&A sections, youtube comments on competitor and category videos, tiktok comments, trustpilot, and niche forums for people discussing [PROBLEM CATEGORY]. extract VERBATIM language, never paraphrase. for each: the quote, the pain underneath it, what they already tried, why it failed them, the outcome they want, and the emotional state behind it"

paraphrasing is how angles end up sounding like marketing. the whole value is in their exact words

amazon Q&A and 3-star reviews carry more signal than anything else on that list. 5-star reviews tell you what they liked. 3-star reviews tell you what nearly stopped them buying

3. diagnose market sophistication BEFORE choosing an angle

"assess the market sophistication level for [CATEGORY] using schwartz's 5 stages. analyse competitor ads and landing pages as evidence. stage 1 means nobody has made the claim yet. stage 2 means claims are escalating. stage 3 means mechanism differentiation has started. stage 4 means mechanisms are competing. stage 5 means the market is exhausted and identification wins. tell me which stage we're in, the evidence, and what type of angle still works at this stage"

if your category is at stage 4, a bigger promise does nothing. every competitor already made one. the only lever left is a sharper mechanism

if it's at stage 5, mechanism is exhausted too, and the angle has to shift to identity and experience

running a stage 2 angle in a stage 4 market is why founders think their creative is fine and their traffic is bad

4. reverse engineer every competitor funnel end to end

"research [COMPETITOR 1, 2, 3]. use google ads transparency center, meta ad library, their landing pages, checkout flow and email capture. for each: every live ad and where it routes, the landing page TYPE, the hero promise, the mechanism they claim, the proof they lead with, the objections handled and in what order, the offer and guarantee, and what they are NOT saying"

then:

"for each competitor page, tell me which awareness level it was built for. then identify which of their traffic is landing on the wrong page type"

that second prompt is the one that finds openings. a competitor sending problem-aware search traffic to a product page is leaving that entire pool uncontested

5. score every angle against awareness and funnel stage

"from the research, generate 15 angles. for each: the awareness level it serves, the funnel stage it belongs in, the sophistication stage it's valid at, whether it's validated by competitors or white space, the proof required to run it, and the funnel type it should be tested through. rank by expected impact and flag any we lack proof to support"

an angle without proof is a claim. rank those last

6. map the keywords to the angles, not the products

"build the keyword universe for [BRAND] using the customer language and competitor research. split into branded, competitor, problem-aware symptom queries, solution-aware category queries, comparison, use-case, and occasion. tag each with awareness level, the angle it pairs with, the funnel type it routes to, and the campaign that should own it"

pairing the keyword to the angle in the same table is what stops the account becoming a keyword dump with generic copy on top

7. turn the research into the campaign plan

"turn everything above into a 30-day google ads launch plan for this specific brand, its AOV, margin structure and market sophistication stage. for each campaign: the angle it carries, the keyword cluster, the landing page type, the creative direction, and the signal that decides whether it scales, holds, or gets cut. separate must-launch from phase 2 tests"

then:

"tell me which 3 angles to test first and why, given our sophistication stage and the proof we currently own"

the whole point:

your competitors are guessing at angles and testing them with budget

all before you spend a dollar finding that out the hard way
