CPMs are usually lower on third party pages.

But it's not because they're non-brand pages.

It's because the ads look more organic, which drives higher engagement, which lowers CPMs.

You can achieve the same lower CPMs on a brand page if your ad is engaging enough.

The variable that matters is engagement, not whether it's a brand page or third party page.

Third party pages just make it easier to create that organic feel.
