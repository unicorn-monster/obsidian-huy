The click looks identical from google's side

same keyword search, same ad copy, same person hitting the link

one landing page produces 1.5x ROAS. the other produces 13x

the only difference is the psychology of what happens after the click. let me walk you through it

80% of non-branded google traffic is problem-aware or solution-aware. they know something is wrong, they don't know you

sending this traffic to a PDP is walking up to someone at a party and immediately asking them to sign a contract. no rapport, no trust, no context, three-second bounce

an advertorial fixes the problem with four psychological steps:

validation: describe their struggle so precisely their brain registers "this person gets me." "you fall asleep fine. the 3am wake-up is destroying you"

education: reveal the hidden cause they didn't know about. the reader becomes cognitively invested, and leaving means leaving with an incomplete understanding of their own problem

mechanism: introduce how the problem gets fixed at a structural level. "90% of adults are deficient in a mineral controlling the entire sleep cycle." the trust transfers from the author to the logic

product last: the product appears as the best implementation of a mechanism the reader already believes in, and the product inherits the trust the mechanism built

the reader never trusted the brand. they trusted the logic. the brand became the vehicle

same exact sequence sitting behind $57k spend to $1.21M revenue at 21x ROAS on keywords any agency would target

and here's what makes the approach sustainable versus the meta creative treadmill:

meta creative dies every 14-21 days, audiences saturate, and constant refresh is required

google advertorial funnels print for months. the content stays evergreen because intent-based traffic rotates naturally. someone searching "best collagen for joints" for the first time today has never seen your advertorial

build one funnel, traffic stays fresh, and the psychology compounds instead of fatiguing

the brands running PDPs for all traffic keep asking why google "doesn't work." the brands running funnels keep asking why they didn't start sooner
