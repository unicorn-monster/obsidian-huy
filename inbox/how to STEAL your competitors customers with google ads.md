## the four inputs

**input 1: product and offer**

the specific product. the mechanism behind it. the USP. the price point. the margin per unit. the average order value. whether the product is consumable or one-time.

this determines which funnel type converts best. a £30 consumable supplement with a 70% margin needs a different approach than a £300 high-ticket gadget with a 40% margin.

**input 2: AOV and margin tier**

this determines the campaign prioritisation and bidding strategy. high-margin products get aggressive TOF campaigns. low-margin products need conservative, high-intent campaigns where every click has to count.

**input 3: niche and competitive landscape**

the AI researches your niche using manus and fable 5 together. it pulls competitor ad data, identifies who's running google shopping in your category, what their product titles look like, what their landing pages do, and where the gaps are.

**input 4: current channel mix**

are you 100% meta? first priority is branded search protection. already on google but plateaued? priority is funnels and youtube demand gen. never run bing? mirror your google structure for instant incremental revenue.

## the AI stack behind it

**manus handles the research layer.** competitive landscape analysis, keyword opportunity identification, ICP mapping from review sites and forums, and market positioning assessment.

**fable 5 (claude) handles the strategic architecture layer.** it takes the research from manus plus the four inputs and produces the full battle plan.

the prompt architecture for fable 5 is structured in five blocks:

**block 1: context injection** - product details, margin structure, competitive landscape from manus, current channel mix

**block 2: funnel type recommendation** - advertorials for products under £100, quiz funnels for products above £100, product-matcher quiz for 3+ SKUs

**block 3: ICP cluster prioritisation** - identify 3-5 highest-opportunity segments ranked by search volume, competition level, buyer intent, and alignment with product USP

**block 4: campaign architecture** - under £5k/month: branded search + non-branded shopping + 1 search campaign. £5-15k/month: add PMAX, non-branded search by ICP angle, DSA. £15k+/month: add youtube shorts demand gen, bing mirror, remarketing

**block 5: timeline and sequencing** - 90-day implementation timeline showing which campaigns launch in which order, when to transition from manual to smart bidding, and the KPI benchmarks to hit before scaling each layer
